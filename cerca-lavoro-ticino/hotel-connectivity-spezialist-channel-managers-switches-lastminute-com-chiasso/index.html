  • Engage directly and technically with channel managers, switches and aggregators (e.g.

  • Read and interpret partner API documentation and integration specifications to assess fit against our needs.
  • Identify technical gaps and limitations in current connections that affect content quality, availability, competitiveness, conversion or operational efficiency.
  • Diagnose recurring connectivity issues and pinpoint root cause on the partner or protocol side.
  • Translate technical findings into simple, credible business cases to justify upgrading, replacing or onboarding channel managers and switches, quantifying expected impact coverage, competitiveness, error reduction, operational savings — with data and analytics colleagues.
  • Support commercial and partnership conversations that require technical depth.
  • Partner with Product and Engineering during discovery and integration work, coordinating between external partners and internal teams across Europe and Asia.
