Technical Drawer & Tooling Warehouse Specialist — MKS PAMP SA, Castel San Pietro (TI).

MISSION The Technical Drawing & Tooling Warehouse Specialist is responsible for managing technical drawings, developing and maintaining operational procedures, and overseeing the tooling warehouse.

As part of a Center of Excellence, this role supports not only the Castel San Pietro site but also teams in the US and India, requiring strong technical competence, cross-regional coordination skills, and the ability to standardize practices across multiple locations.

Responsabilità principali

  • MAIN ACTIVITIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Technical Drawing Management Manage technical drawings in line with engineering and customer standards.
  • Ensure drawing revisions are controlled, approved, and released through the correct change management process.
  • Support drawing requests and interpretation queries from local, US, and India teams.
  • Ensure compliance with applicable regional/customer drawing standards and specifications.
  • Procedure Development Develop, document, and maintain standard operating procedures (SOPs) and work instructions.
  • Standardize procedures across sites to ensure consistency for the Center of Excellences global scope.
  • Review and update procedures periodically to reflect process improvements, audit findings, or regulatory changes.
  • Train and support local and international teams on new or revised procedures.
  • Tooling Warehouse Management Manage day-to-day operations of the tooling warehouse, including inventory control, storage, and maintenance scheduling.
  • Track tooling usage, availability, and lifecycle across all supported regions.

Requisiti principali

  • SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE Technical Diploma in Mechanical Engineering. 8-10 years of experience in a manufacturing or industrial environment.
  • Proven ability with drawing revision and change management processes.
  • Experience in managing work instructions, or other controlled technical documentation.
  • Familiarity with ERP.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams and different cultures.

Azienda e contesto

  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to maintain accurate documentation and inventory records.
  • Structured and process-oriented mindset, with a strong focus on standardization, continuous improvement, and operational efficiency.
  • Fluency in English, both written and spoken, to support regular collaboration with international teams.
  • Swiss or EU citizen, or holder of a valid Swiss work permit.
