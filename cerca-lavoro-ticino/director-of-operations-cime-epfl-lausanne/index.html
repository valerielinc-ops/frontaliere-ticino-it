Mission

EPFL is seeking a dynamic and experienced scientist or engineer to head its Central Electron Microscopy Facility (CIME).

The head of CIME will lead a multidisciplinary platform providing state-of-the-art electron microscopy services, training, and collaborative research support to the EPFL community and external partners.

  • Mission
  • EPFL is seeking a dynamic and experienced scientist or engineer to head its Central Electron Microscopy Facility (CIME).
  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Lead and manage the CIME team (scientific, technical, and administrative staff).

Responsabilità principali

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Lead and manage the CIME team (scientific, technical, and administrative staff).
  • Lead maintenance, upgrade, and optimal operation of the electron microscopy infrastructure (SEM, TEM, FIB, cryo-EM, etc.).
  • Develop and implement the strategic vision of the platform in coordination with the AVP-Platforms and user community.
  • Ensure efficient user support, training, and education at all levels (students, researchers, industry users).
  • Foster collaborations across EPFL schools (STI, SB, SV, INC, ENAC) and with external partners (academia and industry).
  • Manage the operational budget, resource allocation, and long-term investment planning. Profile
  • PhD or equivalent experience in materials science, physics, chemistry, or related fields.
  • Proven expertise in advanced electron microscopy techniques (TEM, SEM, FIB, tomography, cryo-EM, or in-situ EM)
  • Active engagement in the electron microscopy community, including regular participation in conferences, workshops, user meetings, and professional societies, with the ability to build and maintain scientific and technical collaborations that support the facility's mission.

Dettagli ulteriori

  • Manage the operational budget, resource allocation, and long-term investment planning.
