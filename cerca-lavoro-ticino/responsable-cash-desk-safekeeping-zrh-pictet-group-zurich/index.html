Responsabilità principali

  • Your responsibilities include the following:
  • Lead cash operations (withdrawals, deposits and foreign exchange) for the Zurich and Basel branches, in line with internal controls and with an excellent level of service
  • Supervise the central cash fund, monitor currency stock levels and ensure the smooth operation and replenishment of ATMs
  • Organise and support the team (planning, coaching, performance follow-up, recruitment where needed)
  • Oversee the handling of physical securities, precious metals, coins and cheques, as well as the coordination of cash and valuables transport
  • Ensure proper management of safe deposit boxes for clients and employees (processes and controls)
  • Maintain and update procedures, supporting effective internal controls

Requisiti principali

  • Completed commercial apprenticeship, ideally complemented by further education in banking/finance or equivalent
  • Significant experience (minimum 5–8 years) in a banking environment, including exposure to cash/treasury operations, central funds and physical securities handling
  • Proven experience in managing an operational team
  • Very good understanding of operational risks and regulations related to cash activities
  • Comfortable working with demanding high-end clientele and senior internal stakeholders
  • Strong leadership, ability to federate teams, take decisions and manage priorities
