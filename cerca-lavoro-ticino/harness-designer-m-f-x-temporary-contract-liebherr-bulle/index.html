Your Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the design and optimization of wiring harness assemblies for combustion engines, while taking into account technical, scheduling, and financial aspects (design-to-cost based on customer requirements)
  • You prepare technical presentations and define the wiring harness topology and technology approach together with the E/E design team
  • You create and manage 2D drawings.

Having profound knowledge with Zuken E3 is a must.

PTC CREO Cabling is an added value

  • You create bill of materials for the wiring harness assembly
  • You design and define the cabling components (electrical cables, connectors, fastening material) in accordance with standards, regulations, and functional requirements.
  • You support the PLM system (BOM creation, lifecycle promotion, release process)
  • You create and maintain technical documentation and component specifications
  • You plan, execute and evaluate tests as part of the approval process for prototypes or series
  • You cooperate with the corresponding departments within the project environment, as well as with component suppliers to clarify technical properties of components and support manufacturing related issues Your Profile

  • You have several years of experience in working in wiring harness design
  • You have advanced knowledge of MS Office, Zuken E3, PTC Creo Cabling and PLM Albatros
  • You are known for your autonomy and commitment, as well as your strong organizational skills.

Flexible and resilient, you enjoy working in a multicultural environment.
