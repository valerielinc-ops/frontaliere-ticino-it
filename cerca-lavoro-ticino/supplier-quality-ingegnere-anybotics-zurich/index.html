ANYbotics is a fast-growing tech company dedicated to shaping the future of mobile robotics across multiple industries.

Join our highly talented and motivated team of more than 200 people and work on cutting-edge robot technology. The Opportunity

As a Supplier Quality Engineer, you will own supplier quality for a portfolio of critical mechanical, electrical and mechatronic suppliers, from supplier qualification through production and continuous improvement.

  • ANYbotics is a fast-growing tech company dedicated to shaping the future of mobile robotics across multiple industries.
  • Join our highly talented and motivated team of more than 200 people and work on cutting-edge robot technology. The Opportunity
  • Review and negotiate QAA terms with suppliers, covering responsibilities, control points, traceability, non-conformities and other quality requirements.
  • Coordinate internal Quality, Legal and Purchasing approvals for QAAs and monitor ongoing supplier compliance.
  • 5 years experience in supplier quality management with complex mechatronic product
  • Hands-on experience conducting supplier audits and driving corrective actions through to verified closure.

Responsabilità principali

  • Review and negotiate QAA terms with suppliers, covering responsibilities, control points, traceability, non-conformities and other quality requirements.
  • Coordinate internal Quality, Legal and Purchasing approvals for QAAs and monitor ongoing supplier compliance.
  • Define and maintain the supplier NCR process, from detection and reporting through escalation, follow-up and closure.
  • Establish effective NCR templates and tracking tools, and communicate non-conformities clearly with suppliers.
  • Analyse NCR trends and recurring issues to identify systemic quality problems and drive corrective actions.
  • Initiate supplier development, improvement plans or requalification where supplier performance falls below expectations.

Requisiti principali

  • 5 years experience in supplier quality management with complex mechatronic product
  • Hands-on experience conducting supplier audits and driving corrective actions through to verified closure.
  • Strong understanding of root-cause analysis, 8D and structured problem solving.
  • Experience defining and negotiating supplier quality requirements, ideally including Quality Assurance Agreements.
  • Ability to work effectively across Procurement, Engineering, Manufacturing and Quality, while challenging suppliers when quality or delivery requirements are not met.
  • Willingness to travel to suppliers and spend time on-site when required. Bonus Points
  • Experience with ISO 9001 and/or ISO 80079-34, particularly in an industrial or manufacturing environment.

Azienda e contesto
