Processo di candidatura

  • As an Associate Specialist Functional Microbiome at Nestlé Research, your mission is to advance research on microbial metabolites and translate functional microbiome science into meaningful business applications.
  • You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with scientists, technicians, research assistants, innovation partners and external experts across microbiome, metabolomics, immunology and related fields.
  • This is your opportunity to turn ideas into sustainable and innovative solutions for people, pets, and the planet.
  • How you’ll make an impact as an Associate Specialist Functional Microbiome
  • Developing research that deepens understanding of microbial metabolites and their role in microbiome-host interactions.
  • Translating scientific insights into nutrition, health and petcare applications with business relevance.
  • Strengthening scientific approaches that move microbiome research from correlations toward causal understanding.
  • Building cross-functional collaboration with internal research teams, technical experts and innovation partners.
