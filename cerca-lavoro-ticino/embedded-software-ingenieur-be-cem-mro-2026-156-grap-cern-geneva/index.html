Panoramica

Help shape the future of the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) machine protection systems.

We are looking for a motivated Engineer to contribute to the renovation of the LHC Collimator Control System, a critical component ensuring the safe and reliable operation of the world's most powerful particle accelerator.

Working in a highly collaborative environment you will help to increase the reliability, usability, and long-term maintainability of systems at the heart of LHC operations.

Processo di candidatura

  • Develop and maintain control applications for the LHC Collimator system using the FESA framework, ensuring integration within CERN's accelerator control environment.
  • Coordinate and support control system commissioning, performing functional testing, validation, and verification of interlocks and fail-safes prior to operational deployment.
  • Integrate control systems with the alarm server and develop high-level tools to enhance usability and real-time monitoring.
  • Contribute to the design and validation of system-level tests, including functional, integration, and regression testing.
  • Support expert and operational interfaces, providing diagnostic capabilities and improving intervention workflows.
  • Skilled in system integration, commissioning, and validation of control applications for hardware-software systems.
  • Strong ability to troubleshoot, analyse, and document complex technical issues in operational environments.
  • Proactive, organised, and collaborative, with the ability to work effectively in multidisciplinary engineering teams and communicate technical progress clearly.
