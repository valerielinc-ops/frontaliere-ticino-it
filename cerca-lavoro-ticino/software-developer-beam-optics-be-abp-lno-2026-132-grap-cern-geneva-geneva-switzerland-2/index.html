Panoramica

Join the Linear and Non-Linear Optics (LNO) Section within CERN's Accelerator and Beam Physics (ABP) Group and help shape the future of the High-Luminosity Large Hadron Collider (HL-LHC).

As part of the Optics Measurement and Correction (OMC) team, you will develop advanced software and analysis tools that enable precise beam-optics measurements and corrections, as well as improve commissioning procedures.

Your work will play a key role in ensuring the safe, reliable, and high-performance operation of the HL-LHC, contributing directly to the successful delivery of its novel physics programme. of CERN

Responsabilità principali

  • Your responsibilities
  • Maintain and extend the core OMC framework and toolset used during LHC commissioning and operation.
  • Explore software solutions to improve the efficiency and capability of optics measurement and corrections in the LHC.
  • Update accelerator model creation and correction algorithms to utilise new simulation tools.
  • Explore the viability of automation for key measurement and analysis workflows, implementing solutions where possible.
  • Develop new GUIs and analysis tools to be used in the CERN Control Centre during optics commissioning.
  • Participate in, and contribute to the analysis of, optics measurements in the CERN accelerators.
