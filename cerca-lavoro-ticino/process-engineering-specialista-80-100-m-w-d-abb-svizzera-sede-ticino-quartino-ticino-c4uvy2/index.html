Responsabilità principali

  • Your role and responsibilities
  • This Process Engineering position plays a key role in driving the successful deployment and adoption of Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) across the Business Line.
  • Acting as the link between Manufacturing Units, Process Engineering teams, local project managers, and the Process Competence Center, this position ensures that manufacturing requirements are effectively translated into standardized and scalable MES solutions.
  • The role combines process engineering expertise, project coordination, and continuous improvement leadership to support manufacturing transformation initiatives.
  • The successful candidate will contribute to process harmonization across sites, promote best practices, and ensure alignment with global manufacturing standards while supporting project execution from analysis and design through implementation, validation, and stabilization phases.
  • By fostering collaboration among stakeholders and enabling data-driven process optimization, this role is instrumental in enhancing operational excellence, standardization, and the overall success of MES deployment programs. The work model for the role is: onsite You will be mainly accountable for:
  • Support local project managers during all implementation phases and monitor project progress and escalation points related to process engineering activities.
  • Coordinate activities across stakeholders to ensure the timely delivery of project milestones and deliverables, while preparing and presenting status updates, reports, and deployment documentation.
  • Work closely with the Global Manufacturing Manager and Process Competence Center to ensure consistency across deployments.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Operations, Quality, Engineering, IT, and Supply Chain, and at the same time, share lessons learned and contribute to the continuous improvement of the global MES roadmap.
