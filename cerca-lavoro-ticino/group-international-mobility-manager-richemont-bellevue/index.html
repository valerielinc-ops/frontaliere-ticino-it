Processo di candidatura

  • Reporting to the Head of Global Mobility, you will support the translation of strategic framework designs into seamless, day-to-day processes, reinforcing governance and consistency, and expertly managing international assignments with a strong focus on policy application and compliance.
  • HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?
  • In this role, you will position International Mobility as a strong operational and business-enabling capability for HR, combining delivery excellence, stakeholder partnership, mobility governance, and continuous improvement.
  • In Particular, You will: Own the end-to-end delivery of international mobility assignments (e.g., long-term assignments, short-term assignments), ensuring accurate, consistent, and timely execution.

  • Interpret Group International Mobility policies and guidelines, ensuring consistent and fair application across the organization.
