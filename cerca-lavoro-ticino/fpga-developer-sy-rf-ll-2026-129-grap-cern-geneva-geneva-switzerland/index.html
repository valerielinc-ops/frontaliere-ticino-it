Panoramica

The SY-BI group in CERN's Accelerator Systems Department develops and operates the instruments used to observe particle beams and measure their key properties.

These systems are essential to the reliable operation, optimisation, and future development of CERN's accelerator complex.

You will contribute to the development of next-generation beam-position monitoring systems for CERN's injector complex, including the pre-accelerators and Antimatter Decelerator.

  • The SY-BI group in CERN's Accelerator Systems Department develops and operates the instruments used to observe particle beams and measure their key properties.
  • These systems are essential to the reliable operation, optimisation, and future development of CERN's accelerator complex.
  • Your responsibilities:
  • Develop, integrate and test FPGA gateware and embedded SoC firmware for a SoC device.

  • Demonstrated experience with FPGA and SoC.

Responsabilità principali

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Develop, integrate and test FPGA gateware and embedded SoC firmware for a SoC device.
  • Implement data acquisition and data processing logic and real-time digital signal-processing algorithms.
  • Validate system performance in laboratory and accelerator environments.
  • Perform data analysis in Python.
  • Assist measurement campaigns in the accelerator complex.
  • Collaborate with experienced engineers, physicists, and technicians from different teams across CERN.
