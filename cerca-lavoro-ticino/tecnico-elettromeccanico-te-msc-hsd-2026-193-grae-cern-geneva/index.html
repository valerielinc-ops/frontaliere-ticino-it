Panoramica

Do you enjoy building, assembling and bringing machines to life?

Join CERN and contribute to the development of advanced electro-mechanical equipment used in a unique high-technology environment.

Working alongside experienced engineers and technicians, you will take part in the design, assembly, wiring, testing and commissioning of specialised machines, gaining hands-on experience across electrical, mechanical and control systems.

Responsabilità principali

  • Your responsibilities
  • Learn how to inspect and test advanced materials, carrying out quality checks and measurements to ensure they meet specifications.
  • Support laboratory testing activities and help evaluate how materials behave under different mechanical conditions.
  • Gain hands-on experience in creating electrical diagrams, wiring equipment, programming PLCs, and commissioning electro-mechanical machines.
  • Participate in the assembly, testing and operation of specialised machinery used in a cutting-edge engineering environment.
  • Work with experienced engineers and technicians to integrate new materials into innovative cables and coils for future technologies.
