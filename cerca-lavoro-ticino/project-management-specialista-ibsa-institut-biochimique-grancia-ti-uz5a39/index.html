Mission

The Project Management Specialist is responsible for planning, coordinating, monitoring, and supporting the execution of strategic and operational projects within a pharmaceutical manufacturing environment.

The role requires the ability to manage complex cross-functional initiatives, ensuring alignment across multiple business functions while delivering projects in accordance with agreed objectives, timelines, budget, quality standards, and regulatory requirements.

Responsabilità principali

  • Support project governance activities, including the preparation of Steering Committee meetings, Project Reviews, and management reporting.
  • Foster effective coordination and collaboration across key business functions.
