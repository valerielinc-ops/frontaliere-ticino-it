Introduction

You will join a multidisciplinary team developing the next generation of high-performance superconducting radiofrequency (SRF) cavities.

In this role, after dedicated training in the team, you will develop and optimise advanced surface treatments essential to their performance, including chemical polishing, electrochemical polishing and electroplating.
