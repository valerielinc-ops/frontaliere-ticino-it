Responsabilità principali

  • Main responsibilities

  • Hedge accounting: define relationships, designation, valuation and effectiveness testing; align with Finance; support swaps/XCCY; testing and reconciliation.
  • Collateral management: repo/reverse, lending/borrowing, collateral swaps, derivatives eligibility, allocation, haircuts margin workflows

  • External integrations: BNY Mellon, Euroclear (triparty), Acadia (margin), MarkitWire (OTC); data/messaging, identifiers, lifecycle, reconciliation, exceptions, end-to-end testing.
