The Position Job Purpose This role acts as a "T-shaped" Value Orchestrator. The primary mission is to break down traditional silos between procurement and finance by autonomously leading procurement strategies and end-to-end (E2E) processes. Crucially, this role ensures that the "savings" and "cash flow optimizations" generated from procurement activities are directly reflected in the affiliate's financial planning and budgeting (P&L), thereby maximizing enterprise business value. Key Responsibilities 1. Procurement Strategy & Vendor Management

  • Develop and implement procurement strategies and savings plans to support business objectives and financial targets.
  • Execute comprehensive Vendor Management, which includes coordinating supplier selection, onboarding, regular performance evaluations, and maintaining strong, strategic vendor relationships.
  • Lead supplier negotiations to secure favorable commercial terms.
  • Optimize payment terms to significantly reduce deposit/advance invoices and improve company cash flows. 2. Budgeting & Financial Planning
  • Integrate procurement savings and cost optimization plans directly into the affiliate's planning events, including Business Plan (BP), T0, T3, Baseline, as well as Period cost, and M&D budgets.
  • Ensure procurement savings initiatives are tightly aligned with and realized in the company’s financial targets and P&L. 3. E2E Process, Governance & SOPs
  • Create, maintain, and regularly update Procurement Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and formalized purchasing policies to ensure operational efficiency and compliance across all departments.
  • Establish and maintain governance frameworks for the full procurement lifecycle and internal controls.
  • Continuously evaluate and enhance procurement processes and controls to ensure scalability and effectiveness. 4. Collaboration & Network Orchestration
  • Act as the bridge between Finance and Procurement, and collaborate closely with the Supply Chain team to align procurement processes with operational needs.
  • Work with the Roche network (other affiliates and global) to find better solutions and share best practices. 5. Compliance & Risk Management
  • Perform risk management on supply contracts, proactively identifying and resolving supply or quality issues.
  • Conduct all business activities in full compliance with Roche Secure, Roche Behavior in Business, Roche Competition Law Interactive Dialogues, and external regulations. Key Requirements
  • Education: Bachelor’s Degree or above, preferably in Business Administration, Finance, or related.
  • Experience: 5+ years of experience in procurement, supply chain management, or financial planning in Multinational Companies (MNCs).
  • Professional Skills: Strong negotiation, analytical, and financial acumen (understanding of budgeting and P&L). Expertise in vendor management, procurement governance, SOP creation, and E2E processes. Familiarity with financial systems (e.g., SAP) is required.
  • Attributes & Languages: Proven ability to solve problems, influence cross-functional teams, and build stakeholder relationships. Fluent in both oral and written English and Mandarin.
