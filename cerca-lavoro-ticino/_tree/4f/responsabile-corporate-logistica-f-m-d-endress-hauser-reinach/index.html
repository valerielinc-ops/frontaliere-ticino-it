Descrizione completa

At Endress+Hauser, progress happens by working together. As the global leader in measurement instrumentation, our over 18.000 employees shape the future in the field of process automation. Whether developing and realizing new technology as a team, collaborating to build instrumentation, or strengthening vital relationships with countless global industries, we work to create trusted relationships that help everyone thrive. As a family-owned company, we look beyond short-term moves and work to create a vision that is forward-thinking for our people, our clients, and the world at large.

We are looking for a Head of Corporate Logistics (f/m/d), to join our Group Headquarters in Reinach/BL. In this global leadership role, you will define, own and implement Endress+Hauser’s worldwide logistics strategy. You will be responsible for developing a high-performing, resilient and future-ready global logistics network that supports our customer promise and long-term growth.

What is the role about?

As Head of Corporate Logistics (f/m/d), you will provide strategic and functional leadership for logistics across the Endress+Hauser Group. Working closely with our Sales Centers, Production Centers, Regional Logistics Operations Centers and our Global Logistics Operations Center in Ireland, you will bring the global logistics organization together around a common strategy, operating model and performance ambition

You will establish global logistics governance, standards and performance expectations and ensure that strategic priorities are translated into clear regional and local roadmaps. A key part of your responsibility will be to actively steer implementation, remove barriers and ensure that global logistics initiatives deliver measurable and sustainable results.

Your responsibility will cover the continued development of our global logistics network as well as transportation, warehousing, distribution, logistics processes and strategic partnerships with logistics service providers.

Through standardization, digitalization, automation and data-driven performance management, you will help create a globally connected logistics organization that combines customer focus, operational excellence, cost competitiveness and resilience.

As a member of the Corporate Supply Chain Leadership Team, you will represent Corporate Logistics in senior management discussions and contribute the logistics perspective to relevant Group-wide strategic decisions.

Which tasks will you perform?

You will

  • Define, own and continuously develop our global logistics strategy in line with corporate objectives, growth ambitions and customer requirements
  • Translate the global logistics strategy into clear implementation roadmaps, priorities, responsibilities and measurable outcomes
  • Provide functional leadership to the global logistics organization and create alignment across regions, Sales Centers, Production Centers and Logistics Operations Centers
  • Establish and maintain global logistics governance with clear policies, standards, processes, decision rights and performance management principles
  • Shape and lead the development of our global logistics network, including regional hub strategies and transportation concepts.
  • Drive logistics transformation through standardization, digitalization, automation and process harmonization, including TMP and other strategic programs
  • Partner with regional and local supply chain leaders to translate global strategy into actionable roadmaps while balancing global standards with local business requirements
  • Drive logistics cost transparency and sustainable cost optimization while protecting customer service, delivery performance and operational resilience
  • Strengthen supply chain resilience through risk management, business continuity planning and strategies addressing geopolitical challenges
  • Lead strategic partnerships with global logistics service providers, freight forwarders, carriers and distribution partners
  • Build alignment across Operations, Procurement, Sales, Finance, IT, Production Centers and Sales Centers to turn strategic priorities into effective execution

What do we expect from you?​

You

  • Bring extensive senior leadership experience in global logistics within a complex and internationally operating matrix organization
  • Have a strong track record of developing and successfully implementing global logistics strategies, governance models and transformation programs
  • Bring sound experience in enterprise logistics network design and know how to balance customer service, cost and , resilience and working capital requirements
  • Have experience with digital transformation, process standardization, automation and large-scale TMP or comparable enterprise transformation programs
  • Combine strategic thinking with a strong implementation focus and the ability to move confidently between corporate and operational perspectives
  • Use data, KPIs and benchmarks effectively to manage performance, identify opportunities and support fact-based decisions
  • Communicate confidently with senior stakeholders and translate complex logistics topics into clear strategic priorities and actionable decisions
  • Lead through collaboration and build trust across cultures, functions and organizational levels and enable teams to succeed together
  • Bring a relevant university degree or equivalent qualification in supply chain management, logistics, business administration, engineering or a related field
  • Communicate fluently in English - also on C-Level. Any other language is an advantage.
  • Are willing to travel internationally as required by this global role (30%)

What can you expect from us?

  • Join an international, family-owned company where long-term thinking, collaboration and respectful relationships shape the way we work
  • Take on a highly visible global leadership role within Corporate Supply Chain with the opportunity to shape the future of logistics across our international organization
  • Work on strategic topics that directly influence customer satisfaction, operational performance, resilience and sustainable business growth
  • Collaborate with colleagues and senior stakeholders across regions, Production Centers, Sales Centers and corporate functions
  • Shape major transformation initiatives and contribute to the continued development of our global supply chain organization
  • Work from Reinach, Switzerland, in an international environment that combines global responsibility with a strong culture of collaboration, trust and continuous improvement
