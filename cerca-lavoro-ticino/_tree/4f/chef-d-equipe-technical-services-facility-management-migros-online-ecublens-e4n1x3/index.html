Team Lead Technical Services & Facility Management — Migros Online

Questa posizione non è più attiva. Di seguito trovi i dettagli originali e altre posizioni aperte.

Descrizione completa

As Team Lead Technical Services, you're responsible for ensuring the reliable operations of our technical systems and infrastructure in Ecublens. Together with the team, you ensure high system availability, optimise processes, and continuously develop our technical services and facility management. In this role you actively contribute to making our technical operations more efficient and reliable.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Online, Migros Online SA - Ecublens, Chemin du Dévent 11, 1024 Ecublens

Key information

For this job you need to speak either fluent German or English (ideally both) as well as French. We welcome all applications, but can only consider applications submitted online. Applications submitted by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned. An application letter or video is required for this position.

Mansioni

  • You ensure the reliable operations and functionality of our technical systems through maintenance, inspections, repairs and preventive maintenance.
  • You maintain close collaboration with MLR (Migros Logistics Romandie) and internal specialist departments.
  • You lead, develop and support the Technical Services team in Ecublens and ensure efficient workforce and task planning.
  • You plan and coordinate maintenance and repair work and, when required, manage external service providers as well as the procurement of materials and spare parts.
  • You are responsible for the technical infrastructure and facility management, ensuring the safe and smooth operation of the building and its facilities.
  • You ensure compliance with our safety, quality and environmental standards and, together with your team, maintain professional and reliable technical operations.

Requisiti

  • At least 5 years of professional experience in a similar role: within technical maintenance and operations
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): in a technical field, ideally with a permit OBIT 15 (NIV 15)
  • English (Very good knowledge) French (Fluent) German (Very good knowledge): This role requires a level of fluency in French and English and/or German.
  • You have completed technical vocational training and ideally hold an additional qualification in maintenance, building services engineering or facility management.
  • You have several years of professional experience in technical maintenance and operations and have a solid understanding of technical systems, building services and infrastructure.
  • You have leadership experience and lead your team in a clear, respectful and hands-on manner. You actively support the development of your team members and are willing to get involved operationally when needed.
  • You work independently, in a structured and solution-oriented manner, and keep a clear overview even when dealing with technical failures or unexpected situations.
  • You are confident in planning and coordinating technical work and collaborate effectively with internal stakeholders and external service providers.
  • You communicate very well in French and in German and/or English and are willing to provide on-call support and work nights or Sundays in emergency situations.

Cosa offriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18-week maternity leave paid at 100% and 4-week paternity leave paid at 100%
  • #oneteam: You will be part of a motivated, hands-on team that supports one another, embraces diversity, and is focussed on achieving our common goals.
  • Informal culture: We stand for respectful cooperation on the same level
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: We value direct communication and involve our employees in the decision-making process
  • Sports and fitness offers: We value your health and offer CHF 250.- per year when taking out a sports subscription
  • Cumulus points: Benefit from additional Cumulus points

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we require your CV, certificates, and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a cover letter are not necessary. We are committed to equal opportunities for all applicants. We follow a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, please mention it in your application documents.
  • Personal video or telephone call: In the event of a large number of applicants, we may conduct a personal telephone or video call. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates better within a short space of time, with a view to inviting suitable people to the next round. Reply provided: within one week
  • Technical interview: In a technical interview, specific knowledge relevant to the performance of the job is examined by the line manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, by the team. Whenever possible, time is then allocated for the candidate to get to know the workplace and team better by means of open discussion. Reply provided: within three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether or not someone fits into the team is an important factor for both the employer and the candidate when deciding whether to make an appointment. We therefore provide an opportunity for everyone to get to know each other in an informal atmosphere.
  • Offer & issuance of the contract (digital): Once our oral job offer has been issued, the key contractual points are checked once again. Once an agreement has been reached, the issuance of the contract is commissioned and sent by e-mail for digital signature. Reply provided: within one week
