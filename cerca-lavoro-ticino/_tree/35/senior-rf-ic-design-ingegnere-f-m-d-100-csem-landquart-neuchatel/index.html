Descrizione completa

Facing the challenges of our time Help us grow and be more impactful! The “Integrated and Wireless Systems” Business Unit , based in Neuchâtel, Switzerland is looking for a Senior RF IC Design Engineer.

Your mission

Bridge research and industry working alongside experienced engineers, contributing to circuit design, simulation, implementation, and silicon validation while developing technical expertise. Participate in the design of ultra-low-power wireless transceivers for narrowband communications such as Bluetooth, UWB solutions for ranging and localization, and mm-wave radar systems for sensing applications.

Your responsibilities

  • Specify, architect, design, integrate, and verify low-power RF and analog ICs for both research and industrial projects, from block level to top-level integration.
  • Translate system requirements into circuit specifications and architectures, and assess trade-offs in power, noise, linearity, dynamic range, area, robustness, testability, and manufacturability.
  • Guide RF layout, parasitic extraction, and electromagnetic simulation; plan and execute silicon characterization, measurement automation, debugging, and correlation with simulations.
  • Plan and document design activities according to applicable quality practices, including design reviews, configuration control, verification evidence, and reusable technical documentation.
  • Provide technical leadership, conduct design reviews, share know-how, and mentor less experienced IC designers.
  • On request, lead intermediate-size R&D projects involving industrial partners or academic institutes, coordinating technical scope, resources, deliverables, quality, budget, risks, and deadlines.
  • Support acquisition and the setup of new industrial projects by defining technical concepts, writing technical proposal chapters, structuring project tasks, and providing effort estimates.
  • Collaborate with customers, universities, research institutes, suppliers, and multidisciplinary CSEM teams; represent CSEM at technical conferences and customer visits and promote its technologies and know-how.
  • Contribute to future R&D orientations, the group strategy, technical roadmap, and investment plan; disseminate results through publications, invention disclosures, and patent submissions.

Your profile PhD or MSc in Electrical Engineering, Microelectronics, or a closely related field. As a guideline, at least 7 years of relevant experience after a PhD or 10 years after an MSc in industrial or research analog/RF IC design. Know-how

  • Proven track record of successful analog/RF silicon implementations and their laboratory validation.
  • Expertise in low-power and low-noise analog/RF integrated circuit design in CMOS technologies from 55 nm down to 22 nm
  • FinFET experience is an advantage.
  • Excellent understanding of low-power RF transceiver architectures, including sub-GHz, 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz narrowband radios, UWB, and mm-wave systems.
  • Expertise in several RF and mixed-signal functions, such as LNAs, mixers, PAs, VCOs, PLLs, dividers, filters, or analog baseband circuits.
  • Strong experience with the complete ASIC flow: architecture definition, specification, circuit design, top-level integration, verification, layout, tape-out, silicon characterization, and documentation best practices.
  • Proficiency with Cadence IC design tools and analyses covering RF noise, linearity, stability, PVT corners, Monte Carlo, parasitic extraction, and electromagnetic simulation.
  • Hands-on experience with RF laboratory equipment, measurement automation, data analysis, and debugging of complex ICs.
  • Fluent written and spoken English
  • French or German is an advantage.

Interpersonal skills

  • Strong analytical judgment and a pragmatic, structured approach to complex technical problems.
  • Technical curiosity, autonomy, ownership, attention to quality, and the ability to prioritize and manage technical risks.
  • Ability to communicate design decisions and trade-offs clearly with customers, partners, universities, suppliers, and colleagues.
  • Collaborative behavior in multidisciplinary and multicultural teams, with willingness to share knowledge and mentor others.
  • Leadership capability appropriate to technical work packages and intermediate-size R&D projects.
