Descrizione completa

For our Pharma Manufacturing in Visp, we are looking for a CAPEX Project Planner

Key Responsibilities

  • Update Schedule Level 4 based on Progress status
  • Support resource planning and resource-loaded schedules where required.
  • Monitor project progress against approved baselines and identify schedule variances and deviations.
  • Maintain accurate records of scope changes, trends, delays, and variances affecting project timelines.
  • Prepare and distribute regular schedule progress reports, dashboards, trend analyses, and schedule performance metrics.
  • Collaborate closely with Technical Project Team Members,Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Commissioning teams to enable weekly progress tracking

What you need to bring

  • Bachelor's Degree or equivalent qualification (preferredDegree in Engineering, Science, Construction Management, Project Management)
  • 5+ years of project planning and scheduling experience supporting large capital projects within the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, chemical, engineering, or related industries.
  • Demonstrated experience planning projects valued at CHF 20 million or greater, from design through commissioning and start-up.
  • Experience working within an EPCM environment.
  • Experience developing and managing integrated project schedules in complex, multidisciplinary project environments.
  • Experience within Pharma
  • Advanced proficiency in Primavera P6
  • Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Project.
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel skills and proficiency in PowerPoint and Word.
  • Experience with progress measurement methodologies.
  • Understanding of project controls principles, including planning, forecasting, reporting, and change management
  • English fluent, German is a plus
