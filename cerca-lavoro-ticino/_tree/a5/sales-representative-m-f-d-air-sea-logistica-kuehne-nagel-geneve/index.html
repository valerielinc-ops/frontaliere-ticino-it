Descrizione completa

It's more than a job

As a Sales Representative Air & Sea Logistics, you will drive commercial growth for Kuehne+Nagel in Geneva by winning new business, building long-term customer relationships, and delivering exceptional logistics solutions. If you are passionate about sales, enjoy opening new doors, and thrive in a target-driven environment, this is your opportunity to make a direct impact and be rewarded through an attractive bonus scheme.

How you create impact

  • Identify, develop, and win new Air & Sea Logistics business opportunities.
  • Build and grow your own customer portfolio through a proactive hunting approach.
  • Generate leads through cold calling, networking, referrals, and market research.
  • Conduct customer meetings and on-site visits to develop strong business relationships.
  • Collaborate with Product and Trade Managers to create tailored logistics solutions.
  • Manage and maintain a healthy sales pipeline.
  • Achieve and exceed targets for new business, volume growth, and profitability.

What we would like you to bring

  • Freight forwarding apprenticeship, logistics qualification, or comparable experience.
  • Proven experience in international freight forwarding, within Air & Sea Logistics.
  • Successful track record in field sales, business development, or new customer acquisition.
  • Strong commercial mindset with an entrepreneurial and results-driven approach.
  • Excellent communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills.
  • Experience working with CRM systems.
  • Fluent in French and English; German is a plus.

What's in it for you

  • Competitive salary package with an attractive performance-based bonus scheme and 13th-month salary.
  • Company car and flexible working arrangement.
  • Modern office facilities with fitness, gaming, and kitchen areas.
  • Continuous learning and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts with a wide range of partner brands.
  • Employee referral bonus program.
  • Team events and an inclusive, collaborative working environment.

Who we are

Logistics shapes everyday life - from the goods we consume to the healthcare we rely on. At Kuehne+Nagel, your work goes beyond logistics; it enables both ordinary and special moments in the lives of people around the world.

As a global leader with a strong heritage and a vision to move the world forward, we offer a safe, stable environment where your career can make a real difference. Whether we help deliver life-saving medicines, develop sustainable transportation solutions or support our local communities, your career will contribute to more than you can imagine.
