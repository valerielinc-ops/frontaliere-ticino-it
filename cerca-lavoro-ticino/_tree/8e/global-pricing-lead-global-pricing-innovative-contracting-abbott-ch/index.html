Abbott | Established Pharmaceuticals Division

At Abbott , we believe people can live fuller lives through better health. Every day, our medicines and technologies help millions of people around the world manage health conditions, overcome barriers to care, and achieve better outcomes. Within our Branded Generics business, we provide trusted, high-quality medicines to more than 18 million people daily across Latin America, Asia-Pacific, India, and other emerging markets. Our portfolio spans areas including Women's Health, Gastroenterology, Cardiometabolic Health, CNS & Pain, and Respiratory Care , helping address some of healthcare's most pressing needs.

Abbott Established Pharmaceutical Division (EPD) is looking for a

Global Pricing Lead, Global Pricing & Innovative Contracting

for its global Pharma Division Headquarter based in Allschwil-Basel.

Position Summary The Global Pricing Lead, Global Pricing & Innovative Contracting is a strategic leadership role responsible for driving pricing excellence, revenue management, innovative contracting, and value optimization across Abbott's Established Pharmaceuticals Division (EPD). The role will lead the development and execution of innovative contracting strategies across both in-market brands and new launches and, thus, expand Abbott EPD’s portfolio of competitive and financially viable commercial offerings to our customers across the globe. The role will support the Global Head of Pricing & Innovative Contracting to transition the pricing function in Abbott EPD from a transactional and operational function to become a strategic, forward-looking and business-integrated function. The role serves as a key strategic partner to senior leaders across Commercial, Market Access, Finance, Supply Chain, Regulatory Affairs, Government Affairs, and General Management to support sustainable growth, increase patient access, and maximize portfolio value globally. Beyond traditional pricing responsibilities, the Global Pricing Lead is expected to act as a trusted business advisor and enterprise problem solver, helping Abbott EPD identify, prioritize, and address complex business challenges and growth opportunities. The individual will combine strategic thinking, analytical rigor, commercial acumen, financial expertise, and deep pharmaceutical industry knowledge to provide proactive, efficient and actionable insights and recommendations to complex problems that influence critical business decisions. The successful candidate will possess an exceptional ability to identify opportunities, formulate hypotheses, assess alternatives, and develop practical solutions using a broad strategic and analytical toolkit. They will effectively communicate recommendations through compelling executive presentations, data-driven storytelling, dashboards, and senior leadership engagement, enabling informed decision-making across the organization. This role plays a critical part in helping Abbott EPD achieve its mission of expanding global patient access, accelerating business growth, optimizing portfolio performance, and increasing the reach of high-quality medicines worldwide.

Key Responsibilities

Innovative Contracting & Market Access Support

  • Lead the development and implementation of innovative contracting strategies that support access, affordability, and sustainable growth.
  • Design and evaluate value-based agreements, risk-sharing models, volume-based contracts and alternative commercial arrangements in collaboration with other functions.
  • Partner with Market Access, Commercial, Finance, Legal, and regional stakeholders to assess and execute contracting opportunities.
  • Drive best-practice sharing and capability development related to contracting excellence throughout the organization.

Global Pricing Strategy & Portfolio Value Optimization

  • Develop and execute global pricing strategies that maximize product value, patient access, revenue, and profitability across EPD's portfolio.
  • Provide strategic leadership for pricing throughout the product lifecycle, including launch, growth, maturity, and loss-of-exclusivity phases.
  • Lead pricing strategy development for new product launches, including branded pharmaceuticals, generics, and biosimilars.
  • Optimize pricing and value realization for established and in-market products across diverse healthcare systems and market environments.
  • Monitor and assess the impact of external reference pricing, competitive dynamics, reimbursement trends, healthcare policy changes, inflationary pressures, and tender environments.
  • Establish and continuously improve pricing frameworks, governance processes, and decision-making methodologies.
  • Drive pricing capability building across the value chain.

Revenue Management & Pricing Performance

  • Lead global revenue management initiatives to improve pricing effectiveness, price architecture principles and overall financial performance.
  • Identify, quantify, prioritize, and track pricing and revenue optimization opportunities across markets and portfolios.
  • Conduct financial modeling, business case development, scenario analyses, and risk assessments to support strategic decisions.
  • Monitor price-volume-mix dynamics, discounting practices, net pricing performance, and revenue leakage risks.
  • Partner with Finance and Commercial teams to ensure effective execution and measurement of pricing initiatives.

Strategic Business Leadership & Enterprise Problem Solving

  • Act as a trusted advisor to senior leadership on complex strategic, commercial, operational, and financial business challenges.
  • Proactively identify opportunities to accelerate growth, increase competitiveness, improve profitability, expand access, and enhance patient reach.

  • Utilize a comprehensive toolbox of strategic and analytical approaches including:
  • Market analysis

  • Develop and test hypotheses using quantitative and qualitative data to guide decision-making and resource allocation.
  • Lead strategic assessments supporting portfolio prioritization, launch planning, investment decisions, market expansion opportunities, and organizational transformation initiatives.
  • Translate complex analyses into actionable business recommendations that deliver measurable impact.
  • Support enterprise growth objectives through initiatives that extend beyond pricing-related challenges.

Pricing Analytics, Insights & Executive Reporting

  • Establish and continuously enhance world-class pricing analytics and business intelligence capabilities.
  • Develop actionable insights that support strategic and operational decision-making.
  • Lead the development of executive dashboards, KPI tracking systems, scorecards, and performance reporting tools.
  • Deliver regular strategic business reviews and pricing performance updates to executive leadership teams.
