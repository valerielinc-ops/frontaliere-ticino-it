Descrizione completa

We are a small, interdisciplinary and international team based at the University Children's Hospital Basel (UKBB) and the University of Basel. Our research focuses on understanding the underlying mechanisms of paediatric diseases and developing new therapeutic approaches, with a particular emphasis on paediatric tumours, including neuroblastoma, and intestinal diseases included research projects funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF). The Laboratory of Pediatric Surgery (Prof. S. Holland-Cunz / Prof. S. Gros) is embedded within the research infrastructure of the UKBB, the Department of Clinical Research and the Department of Biomedicine of the University of Basel, providing a collaborative and international research environment. We are seeking a Technical Research Assistant (80-100%) to join our team. The position combines experimental laboratory work with the coordination of research projects and clinical studies. We are looking for a motivated, reliable and well-organised individual to support our research activities and contribute to clinically relevant research. This is a one-year fixed-term position with the possibility of extension.

Eintrittsdatum: per 1. März 2027

Ihre Aufgaben

  • Organisational and administrative support of ongoing SNSF-funded research project
  • Coordination and organisation of research activities in the fields of paediatric oncology and intestinal diseases
  • Organisation and coordination of clinical research studies, including communication with clinical partners and study teams
  • Support with study documentation, data organisation and maintenance of study-related records
  • Coordination of patient sample collection, processing and documentation in close collaboration with clinical staff
  • Performing hands-on laboratory experiments, including advanced and 3D cell culture techniques
  • Performing flow cytometry (FACS) and related cellular analyses
  • Preparation of patient and other biological samples for downstream molecular analyses
  • Performing molecular and cell biological techniques, including qPCR and protein analyses
  • Organisation of laboratory workflows, materials, equipment and day-to-day laboratory activities
  • Providing technical and organisational support to students (M.D./Ph.D.) and other team members

Ihr Profil

  • Education or professional experience in life sciences, biomedical sciences, laboratory technology or a related field (e.g. Biology Laboratory Technician, BSc/MSc in Biology or equivalent)
  • Practical experience in cell culture, ideally including advanced or 3D cell culture systems
  • Experience with flow cytometry (FACS), RNA preparation and basic molecular biology techniques is expected
  • Experience in laboratory work involving biological or patient-derived samples is an advantage
  • Strong organisational and coordination skills
  • Experience in clinical research or clinical study coordination is highly desirable
  • Experience in managing research projects, study documentation and administrative workflows
  • An interest in paediatric research, particularly in paediatric oncology and intestinal diseases
  • A structured, reliable and solution-oriented approach to work, with the ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks simultaneously
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with very good written and spoken English
  • German is an advantage
  • Training or experience in biosafety and laboratory safety procedures is a plus

Unser Angebot

  • A varied and meaningful role with a skilled, motivated and dedicated team
  • Flexible working arrangements and opportunities to develop new skills in an active research environment
  • Comprehensive, structured and supportive onboarding
