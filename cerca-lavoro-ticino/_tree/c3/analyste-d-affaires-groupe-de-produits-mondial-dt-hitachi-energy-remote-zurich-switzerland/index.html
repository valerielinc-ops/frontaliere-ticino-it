You will contribute cross-functionally by collaborating with IT function and business domains, as well as being responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining the GPG DT Power BI reports to monitor KPIs and performance metrics How you'll make an impact Ensure adherence to data governance standards and maintain comprehensive documentation for all reports and processes within GPG DT Operations Digital area. Collect and design business requirements into required documentation format and templates. Identify opportunities to enhance reporting processes, tools, and methodologies for greater efficiency and scalability. Implement best practices and leverage emerging technologies to improve data analytics capabilities. Provide training and guidance to business teams on the analytics reports, core digital tools, TrafoSignature tool. Collaborate with global teams, HUBs, and factories to gather requirements and deliver tailored reporting solutions. Act as a key liaison between technical teams and business stakeholders to ensure alignment and transparency. Lead functional and business analysis for core projects: Digital TrafoSignature, ECS, IBA, Matterport from business perspective. Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work with global technical teams and stakeholders. Strong understanding of functional and business analysis. Strong problem-solving abilities, attention to detail, and ability to work independently in a global team environment.
