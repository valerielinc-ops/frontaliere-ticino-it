Descrizione completa

Location : Zurich or Geneva Work Pattern: 80-100% About the Role Join our Finance Transformation Consulting team and help clients transform their finance functions with AI. Develop our AI offering within Finance Strategy and Performance: its value proposition and services, create reusable delivery methods, lead go-to-market activities and build team capabilities. Work closely with other teams, including AI & Data. Finding the talent that makes Deloitte unique Our Deloitte Finance Transformation consultants help clients translate finance needs into integrated solutions, from strategy and technology selection to implementation. We are seeking a Senior Manager / Director who combines practical experience defining and implementing AI use cases for finance functions with consulting leadership. You will lead client engagements, grow client relationships and revenue, and help shape our AI offering within our Finance Strategy and Performance team. How you can make an impact As a Senior Manager / Director in Finance Transformation with AI, you will:

  • Define, prioritise and implement AI use cases for finance functions. Guide clients from identifying business needs and developing business cases through deployment, adoption and tracking benefits, including in enterprise resource planning (ERP) and enterprise performance management (EPM) transformation programmes.
  • Lead Finance Transformation consulting engagements from start to finish, with accountability for delivery quality, scope, budgets, commercial performance and teams.
  • Work with cross-functional teams and technology partners to integrate AI-enabled finance solutions that align with clients’ strategic objectives and governance requirements, including data quality, internal controls and human oversight of AI outputs.
  • Advise clients on finance strategy, target operating model design, process optimisation and AI adoption to improve decision-making, performance management and value creation.
  • Drive sales and revenue growth with the team by identifying opportunities, developing proposals, leading pitches and winning AI-enabled Finance Transformation engagements.
  • Manage client relationships throughout each engagement, acting as a trusted adviser to finance leaders, aligning stakeholder expectations and building long-term partnerships.
  • Lead, coach and develop diverse teams, support recruitment and performance development, and build practical AI skills across our Finance Transformation practice
  • Prepare thought leadership publications on AI in Finance Transformation and present insights and practical use cases at industry conferences and client events. What you bring to succeed
  • You hold a university degree in Finance, Business, IT, or a related field.
  • You have at least 10 years of experience in finance strategy, transformation or consulting, including consulting experience at a Big Four firm or an equivalent consultancy.
  • You have a track record of leading end-to-end client engagements involving AI in Finance. Experience in ERP or EPM transformation programmes is an advantage.
  • You have a strong understanding of core finance processes, including controlling, financial close, consolidation, planning and reporting.
  • You have personally defined and implemented AI use cases for a finance function, from identifying the business need and designing the use case through implementation and adoption, with demonstrable business outcomes, for example in forecasting, management reporting or financial close. What sets you apart
  • You understand the capabilities and limitations of predictive, generative and agentic AI and can assess their suitability for Finance use cases with technical specialist.
  • You have a proven consulting track record in winning engagements, generating revenue, managing client relationships, and developing and managing a Finance Transformation with AI offering.
  • You possess excellent communication, stakeholder management, and analytical skills.
  • Established client relationships and a strong professional network in the Swiss market are highly desirable.
  • You are fluent in English and either German or French, and willing to travel within Switzerland and internationally. Your team Our Finance Strategy and Performance team at Deloitte helps clients address complex finance challenges and improve performance through innovative operating models and technology-enabled solutions. We combine deep industry knowledge with advanced technologies, including AI, to transform finance functions globally.
