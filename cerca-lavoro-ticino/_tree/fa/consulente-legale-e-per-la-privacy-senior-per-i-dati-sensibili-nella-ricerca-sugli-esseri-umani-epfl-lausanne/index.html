Descrizione completa

Mission

Join EPFL at a pivotal moment in the evolution of its human research ecosystem.

As Senior Legal & Privacy Counsel for Sensitive Data in Human Research, you will work across the Data Protection Office and Legal Affairs and play a key role in shaping and maintaining the legal, contractual and governance framework of the EPFL's Trusted Research Environment (TRE) and institutional biobank. Together, these strategic initiatives aim to provide researchers with secure, compliant and scalable infrastructure for the use of sensitive and health-related data, in line with Swiss and international regulatory requirements.

Collaborating with researchers, technical experts and institutional partners across EPFL, you will provide legal, privacy and regulatory expertise across all stages of the TRE and biobank lifecycle. Ultimately, you will help create the conditions for innovative research to thrive while ensuring the highest standards of compliance, data protection and ethical responsibility.

Main duties and responsibilities

  • Establish and maintain the legal, privacy and governance framework supporting the secure and compliant operation of the TRE and institutional biobank.
  • Draft, negotiate and manage contractual documents and agreements for the TRE and biobank, including collaboration, data and material transfer, partnership and confidentiality agreements.
  • Contribute to the data protection and privacy compliance, ensuring alignment with the Swiss FADP, GDPR, and standards such as ISO 27001, SATRE and Swiss Biobanking Platform (SBP) requirements.
  • Provide legal counsel on human research and biobanking, covering regulatory compliance, ethics, consent frameworks, and data and sample access models.
  • Ensure privacy governance and risk management processes, including data protection impact assessments, record of processing activities, privacy-by-design measures, data subject rights and data breach response procedures.
  • Advise on intellectual property and governance matters relating to biological samples, research data, software, research results and licensing.
  • Support the development of the TRE and biobank, including SBP accreditation, procurement, policy and governance.
  • Promote compliance and responsible research practices through training, awareness activities, reporting and participation in expert working groups.

Profile

  • Master’s degree in law (Swiss degree or recognized equivalent); a background in biology, biotechnology, genetics, and human biology related subject is an asset.
  • Extensive experience in legal and privacy advisory roles, ideally within research, healthcare, life sciences or innovation environments.
  • Strong knowledge of data protection law (FADP/LPD, GDPR) and experience advising on complex contractual matters; certification in data protection (CIPP/E, CIPM or equivalent) is an asset.
  • Expertise in human research legislation (HRA), health-related data, biobanks, clinical research or similar environment; applied knowledge of SATRE compliance and SBP label is an asset.
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to collaborate with multidisciplinary stakeholders and translate legal and regulatory requirements into practical solutions.
  • Demonstrated expertise in drafting, reviewing and negotiating contracts and agreements in both French and English.
