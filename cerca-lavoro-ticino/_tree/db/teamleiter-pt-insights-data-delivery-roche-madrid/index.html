The Position The Team Lead PT Insights & Data Delivery (Product Manager) is accountable for the entire product lifecycle, from initial ideation and strategic planning through to launch, scaling, and eventual retirement. This role is pivotal in transforming scientific and business needs into tangible, high-value technology solutions. You will be responsible for defining the product vision, strategy, and roadmap, ensuring it aligns with the overarching goals of our organization and ultimately accelerates the delivery of life-changing diagnostics and medicines to patients. This role demands a distinctive combination of scientific inquisitiveness, technical proficiency, and business insight to successfully navigate the landscape. Your primary objective is to lead engineering and service teams building desirable, feasible, viable, and sustainable products that meet the evolving needs of our internal users while maintaining strict compliance with relevant industry standards.

Description of the area PT Insights and Data Delivery focuses on the reliable delivery of high quality data combined with the use of data in analytics solutions for both existing and anticipatory business needs with a cross-functional scope and mandate. The role will lead a team of over 20 people to bring the following key benefits to PT

  • Reusable and scalable systems that are efficiently operated
  • Faster and more robust solutions to meet business challenges
  • Reliable delivery of key metrics and performance indicators
  • Identifying areas for cost savings and where to invest
  • Data/evidence driven decisions
  • Process enabling efficient maintenance of reliable enterprise data
