The Position What we do: The People Support Solutions (PSS) organization brings its purpose to life by providing our colleagues worldwide with integrated HR solutions tailored to their needs. We are a team that works very closely together to ensure good employee relations, anticipate our customers' future needs, and proactively develop solutions. Join us now and be part of our unique transformational journey: we are building a global People & Culture (P&C/HR) Center of Excellence by bringing in talented HR professionals in order to cover complex and challenging HR functions and areas. The Opportunity: As a German speaking People & Culture (P&C) Business Partner (Advisory) you partner flexibly and mostly virtually with leaders across the globe, offering on-demand, ad-hoc support on Business Partnering topics that need more immediate or short-term guidance and value creation. With a good understanding of the business needs and the business context and an excellent ability to apply key P&C concepts, policies and processes, you provide coaching, guidance and support to leaders to help them diagnose and translate their business needs into immediate P&C approaches and ensure effective implementation of solutions in partnership with other chapters. You are a trusted member of the Business Partner Chapter and closely integrated into the PSS organization. Your day to day responsibilities are:

  • Strategic Guidance: Provide on-demand coaching and advisory to global leaders on critical P&C topics, including talent management, succession planning, performance management, and organizational design.
  • Transformation Support: Lead and advise on complex organizational changes, transformations, and employee representation topics, ensuring risk-based decision-making and compliance with established policies.
  • Global Rollouts: Deliver first-class support and coaching during the implementation of new global processes and projects, such as global grading systems or new system launches.
  • Data-Driven Insights: Analyze P&C metrics and business data to recommend innovative solutions and provide insights that support our culture and strategic direction.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with global/regional squads and PSS chapters to ensure the seamless execution of P&C initiatives and consistent policy application. ​ Who You Are:
  • Experienced HR Professional: You bring 3–7 years of experience in similar roles such as HR Business Partnering, People Relations, or Labor Law.
  • Bilingual Expert: You possess an excellent written and spoken command of both English and German, enabling you to support the DACH region and global stakeholders effectively.
  • Skilled Coach & Communicator: You have a proven ability to provide difficult feedback, resolve conflicts, and influence leaders at all levels of management.
  • Strategic Thinker: You can thoughtfully diagnose business needs, weigh the risks and benefits of various options, and propose fit-for-purpose HR approaches.
  • You demonstrate strong knowledge in interpreting employment laws and policies, ensuring all solutions are legally sound and culturally sensitive.
  • Agile Mindset: You thrive in ambiguous, fast-paced environments, demonstrating high customer centricity, end-to-end accountability, and a passion for continuous learning.
  • Relationship Builder: You are a natural networker who excels at navigating complex, matrixed reporting relationships across multiple cultures. In Exchange we provide you with:
