Process Safety Chemist (m/w/d) – Your next role?

As an Process Safety Chemistbased in Vernier, reporting directly to the Head of Ingredients Production, you will drive process safety initiatives and participate in/lead process safety risk assessments. You will work closely with production units, EHS and engineering/maintenance to deliver safe and innovative manufacturing solutions.

Sounds interesting? It could well be your perfect opportunity if, like us, you always aim to exceed customer needs, provide value and are experienced at promoting relationships.

In this exciting role you will

Safety, Quality, and Compliance : You will Support QEHS risk assessments and participate in/lead process safety risk analyses for current and new processes, and ensure all activities are conducted in accordance with Givaudan’s EHS policies, GMP requirements and applicable regulations.

Production Support :You will evaluate product and process related safety issues and conduct equipment troubleshooting to reduce process safety risk exposure across our highest-risk assets. You will also support production transfers within and across sites and ensure proper knowledge management through the development of SOPs and technical documentation.

Continuous Improvement : You will contribute to development initiatives such as those required to improve process understanding, process control and process capability. You will propose necessary improvements and investments to comply with Givaudan and regulatory requirements.

Your success profile includes

  • Master's degree or PhD in Chemical Engineering or Organic Chemistry ;
  • 4 to 7 years of experience in chemical manufacturing, specialty chemicals, ingredients, flavors, fragrances, or related industries ;
  • Experience in process safety risk analysis is mandatory (Chemical Hazard Analysis, Process Risk Analysis, HAZOP/LOPA …) ;
  • Experience in chemical processes, scale-ups, transfers and industrial implementations ;
  • Experience in continuous improvement (CI) methodologies
  • Understanding of industrial batch processes
  • Leadership, able to manage multiple activities in parallel and to work in a cross-functional environment with a proactive and solution-oriented approach
  • Fluent in English and French, Spanish and German is a plus
  • For safety and hygiene reasons, please note that wearing a beard is not allowed in Laboratory / Production roles
