Descrizione completa

At Board, we power financial and operational planning solutions for the world’s best brands. Thousands of enterprises use our technology to optimize resources, drive growth, and ensure profitability. With advanced analytics and forecasting, plus AI-driven insights, customers transform complex, real-time data into actionable intelligence. What’s been key to our success? Our people—we value everyone’s unique perspective and energy they bring to the organization. We collaborate openly across teams and borders. We embrace a growth mindset to get results. And we celebrate shared success as goals and milestones are achieved. Ready to join a team where innovation meets collaboration? If you're driven by bold ideas and a customer-centric mentality, your next adventure starts here! ## Role Overview We are seeking an ## FP&A Solution AI Developer to help embed AI capabilities into our FP&A solution strategy and assets. In this role, you will act as the primary resource for implementing AI use cases across FP&A solutions, working at the intersection of Board development, financial planning processes, and enterprise AI tools. You will help identify where AI can improve planning, forecasting, reporting, analysis, user guidance, automation, and decision support — then translate those opportunities into practical, scalable solution components. We are looking for someone with an AI-centric mindset: curious, hands-on, and enthusiastic about using AI as a core part of how work gets done. You should be highly skilled in prompting, understand how to apply prompt engineering best practices, and be comfortable working with common enterprise LLM tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, or similar platforms. This role is ideal for someone who can combine technical solution development, FP&A understanding, and AI experimentation to create reusable capabilities that can scale across customers and solution assets. ## Key

  • Responsibilities
  • Act as the primary developer for AI use cases across FP&A solution assets and customer-ready frameworks.
  • Design, test, and implement AI-enabled features for planning, forecasting, reporting, analytics, insights, and user assistance.
  • Translate FP&A business needs into practical AI use cases, prompts, workflows, and solution components.
  • Develop and maintain prompt libraries, AI workflows, prototypes, demos, and reusable AI solution assets.
  • Work with Product Owners, Solution Architects, FP&A experts, and developers to embed AI into existing solution frameworks.
  • Test, validate, and refine AI outputs to ensure accuracy, relevance, usability, consistency, and business value.
  • Document AI use cases, prompting logic, usage guidelines, limitations, and enablement materials for internal and customer-facing teams ## Required Skills
  • Proven experience working with enterprise AI and LLM tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, or similar platforms.
  • Excellent prompt engineering skills, including prompt design, testing, refinement, evaluation, and documentation. Ability to translate FP&A requirements into AI-enabled workflows, use cases, prototypes, and scalable solution components.
  • Strong understanding of AI output validation, including accuracy checks, hallucination risk, context quality, and business relevance.
  • Experience integrating AI into existing business, data, analytics, planning, or application frameworks.
  • Ability to work independently, experiment quickly, and turn AI concepts into practical deliverables. Preferred Skills
  • Basic understanding of FP&A processes such as budgeting, forecasting, financial planning, management reporting, variance analysis, and scenario modelling.
