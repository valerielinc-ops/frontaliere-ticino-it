The Position As a member of the Global Digital Hub Team, you will work to ensure fulfillment of the Global Digital Hub long term outcomes and strategy as well as excellent delivery of priorities related to content. You are responsible for supporting Roche disease area teams in translating their brand strategy into content. In addition, you work with relevant stakeholders to ensure the effective deployment of resources and tools to support and ensure that all content related activities are conducted efficiently, cost-effectively, and in accordance with the business needs. This position has a global scope. Your Opportunity In the position of Digital Content Strategist in the area of Immunology/CVRM you will be focusing on:

  • Aligning content strategy across disease area teams to ensure business objectives flow seamlessly through the full content supply chain
  • Developing customer engagement goals and driving customer-centric content planning that solves identified business problem
  • Building integrated omnichannel plans by collaborating with strategy leads, insights teams, and data analytics experts to ensure content reaches customers effectively
  • Auditing and optimizing content by identifying gaps, repurposing existing assets across new channels and formats, and maximizing content value
  • Fostering cross-functional collaboration across commercial, medical, corporate communications, and global teams to co-create innovative content strategies
  • Measuring content performance through KPIs and A/B testing, while building a repository of best practices tailored to customer segments
  • Driving continuous improvement by establishing governance principles, encouraging experimentation, and recommending enhancements to service and responsiveness for business partners Who you are You are an upbeat, dynamic individual who is highly involved and passionate about planning, creating and executing content strategies.
