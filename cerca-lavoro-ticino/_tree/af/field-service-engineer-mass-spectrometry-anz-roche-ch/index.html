Reporting to the Engineering Manager, Roche Diagnostics Australia is looking for a Field Service Engineer, ideally with specific experience working with Mass Spectrometry. As a Field Service Engineer, you are responsible to perform the field service activities for all our Diagnostics portfolios with a primary focus on Mass Spectrometry, by provision and coordination of all activities relating to instrument installation, instrument preventative maintenance, instrument troubleshooting, software and hardware modifications, relating to both pre, during, and post sales requirements. Service will need to be performed locally, interstate, and including travel overseas to support Roche Diagnostics New Zealand when needed. This service is provided on the basis of hours of employment typically being from 9 am to 5 pm Monday to Friday. In addition, you will be asked to participate in an on-call roster to cover the out of hours’ service requirements from 5 pm to 9 am Monday to Friday and 24 hours Saturday and Sunday. Field Service Engineers are required to actively collaborate with the local account management teams and to participate in local and national team meetings. We are looking for an experienced engineer with a proactive mindset, who enjoys working collaboratively in a close team environment, and is capable of working fully independently. Key responsibilities include:

  • Provide outstanding quality instrument service and support, in a timely manner, to meet the high standards expected by our customers of Roche and the products we supply and support.
  • Responsible for the timely pre-installation inspection and installation of Roche and other analytical systems working with Regional Engineering Manager, Application Specialist, Project Lead, Solutions Manager, and Local Account Management Team to ensure successful installations occur.
  • Ensure all Preventative Maintenance is carried out in a timely manner as per the manufacturer's guidelines.
  • Ensure any ongoing instrument problems or issues are communicated to Regional Engineering Manager, Head of Service & Support, Local Account Manager, Solutions Team and or manufacturer.
  • Accurately capture and report all field activities undertaken, including all labour and travel incurred via CRM system in a timely manner.
  • Compliance with all Roche processes as documented in policies and procedures- complete all mandatory training by the due date.
  • Ensures compliance and promotion of all safety, health, and environmental policies and procedures to ensure the safety, health and wellbeing of all employees and visitors. Who You Are:
  • Tertiary biomedical engineering or equivalent qualification with 3+ years of experience in field services engineering, ideally with specialist Mass Spectrometry knowledge and hands-on experience.
  • Experience with Lab IT and middleware systems.
  • Exceptional communication skills, both written and verbal.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and handle multiple projects.
  • Availability to travel locally, regionally, nationally and internationally as required.
