Descrizione completa

Are you eager to begin your professional career in a multinational environment? Join our 12-month internship program, gain practical purchasing experience and develop your capabilities within an international industrial company. We are looking for motivated candidates available to start their internship in February/March 2027. Your responsibilities

  • You support the preparation and follow-up of Quality Gates (QG), project reviews, quality documentation, and action plans,
  • You contribute to risk assessments, FMEA activities, and validation processes to ensure product quality throughout the development phase,
  • You support development teams in analyzing and resolving quality issues, including the follow-up of non-conformities (NCs), root cause analysis (8D, 5 Why), and implementation of corrective actions,
  • You coordinate control and analysis requests with laboratories (metrology, material analysis, technical cleanliness, etc.) and support prototype build and industrialization activities,
  • You support the transition from development to series production by following up on open quality topics and assisting operational teams during launch phases,
  • You contribute to audits, quality reviews, Lessons Learned activities, and continuous improvement projects in collaboration with Engineering, Production, Purchasing, and Suppliers. Your profile
  • You are currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Mechanical, Industrial, Manufacturing, or Quality Engineering,
  • You possess strong analytical and organizational skills,
  • You are proficient in MS Office Suite, knowledge of Power BI is a plus,
  • You demonstrate an interest in quality tools and methodologies such as FMEA, 8D, and problem-solving techniques,
  • You are a proactive team player with strong communication skills,
  • You are fluent in English. French and/or German is a plus. Our offer You will have the opportunity to immerse yourself in the professional world and gain varied and exciting experience within an international family-owned company. We offer you the chance to take on interesting assignments, assume responsibility, and contribute your own ideas. You will be trained, supported, and guided by a dynamic team. You will benefit from modern working conditions, including flexible working hours, the possibility to work remotely, an employee restaurant, and many other benefits. The position is based in Bulle, in the beautiful Gruyère region. Plan your personal and professional development with us. To ensure a fair recruitment process, we do not accept applications submitted by post or email. Have we awoken your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your online application. If you have any questions, please contact Sabrina Sotheary BEAN. One Passion. Many Opportunities. The company ​Liebherr Machines Bulle SA is the centre of excellence for combustion engines (diesel and gas engines) and hydraulic components (axial piston pumps and motors). The company develops and produces various products, which find their use in building construction and civil engineering, mining, agriculture, decentralised energy, machine and plant engineering, as well as aerospace.
