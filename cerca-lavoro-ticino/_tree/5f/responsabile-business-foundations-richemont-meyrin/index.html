Just as importantly, it will transform how technology and business teams work together—moving us to an agile, product-led model focused on clear business outcomes.” - Kim Hartlev, Chief Information Officer

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

We are looking for a Head of Technology to lead the platforms, data and digital services behind our corporate functions: HR, Finance, Legal, Sustainability, Corporate Affairs, and Real Estate. The position is a member of the Group Technology Executive Committee.

The role at a glance

  • Reports to: Chief Information Officer
  • Key stakeholders and business partners: Group Platforms Product

and Data offices, Group Functions leadership and teams

  • Location: Meyrin, Geneva, Switzerland
  • Contract: Permanent, full-time

Purpose of the role

You will be accountable for the technology strategy, architecture, engineering and delivery capability, platforms and day-to-day technology services supporting our corporate functions. Working in partnership with Product Management and the Functions, you will ensure that roadmaps are translated into scalable, secure, reliable and cost-effective technology solutions while making trusted data and AI available to every function.

You will also lead a change in how we work: from project-based delivery to an agile, product-led model. Long-lived, cross-functional teams will own outcomes for each value stream and work side by side with the product owners and the functions they jointly serve.

You will be the senior technology partner to the leaders of HR, Legal, Sustainability, Corporate Affairs, Real Estate and Finance, and a member of the Technology leadership team. Success means corporate processes that are simpler, more automated and better controlled, at a lower cost to run, delivered by empowered product teams.

Business areas in scope

The role covers six corporate functions from a technology perspective and the shared platforms supporting them. Our core platforms are SAP, Workday, Ariba, IFS and Sustaira.

  • HR – Hire-to-retire: recruiting, core HR, payroll, learning, talent and performance management
  • Legal – Contract lifecycle, obligation management, matter management, legal entity management.
  • Sustainability – ESG data collection, carbon accounting including Scope 3, regulatory reporting (e.g. CSRD), supplier sustainability.
  • Corporate Affairs – Internal and external communications, intranet, media monitoring, stakeholder and public affairs management, corporate website.
  • Finance – Record-to-report, order-to-cash, planning and forecasting, consolidation, treasury, tax.
  • Real Estate – Portfolio and lease management, lease accounting (IFRS 16), space and workplace management, facilities and maintenance, capital projects, building energy data.

Key responsibilities

Strategy and roadmap

  • Define the multi-year technology strategy and evolution roadmap for Business Foundations, in alignment with the enterprise technology strategy and the product roadmaps defined by Product Management in collaboration with the Functions.
  • Provide technology expertise, architecture choices, delivery capacity, cost and risk perspectives to support Product Management in shaping achievable product roadmaps.
  • Manage the budget across the six functions, balancing transformation, compliance and run.
  • Lead build, buy and retire decisions, and consolidate the application estate.

Agile, product-led ways of working

  • Lead the move from project-based, waterfall delivery to a product-led operating model, with long-lived cross-functional teams aligned with Product Management.
  • Work closely with product management and align on common KPIs and funding for products rather than projects.
  • Embed agile practices (Scrum, Kanban and scaled agile) and DevOps for SaaS, including automated testing, continuous integration and a regular release cadence.
  • Ensure product owners and technology teams work as one, so backlogs are fully aligned and outcomes co-owned.
  • Coach managers and teams through the change and track progress with clear outcome-based metrics.
  • Reshape partner and vendor contracts to support agile, outcome-based delivery.

Delivery and service

  • Lead multi-year transformation programmes for the business areas, such as SAP S/4HANA modernisation and the evolution of Workday, SAP Ariba and Sustaira, from business case to realised benefits with clear accountability for technology cost, feasibility, delivery and system performance.
  • Own platform service quality: availability, incident and problem management, release and change, and business continuity for critical processes such as payroll, payments and financial close.
  • Keep platforms close to standard (a clean core for SAP), so the business can more easily adopt vendor innovation such as Workday's twice-yearly releases.

Architecture, data and AI

  • Together with Enterprise Architecture, define the target-state architecture, integration patterns and master data ownership for the corporate platforms.
  • Make corporate data trusted and reusable, so finance, people, supplier and ESG data feed analytics and regulatory reporting from a single source.
  • Help to identify and scale automation and AI where it pays back, for example software delivery, invoice processing, contract review, HR service agents and ESG data collection, within the Group’s responsible-AI guardrails.

Governance, risk and compliance

  • Ensure systems meet control requirements: internal controls, external audit readiness, segregation of duties and data privacy.
  • Partner with Group Security on third-party risk and resilience of business-critical platforms.
  • Provide the systems and data controls behind statutory, lease accounting (IFRS 16), tax and sustainability reporting (e.g. CSRD).

Vendors and budget

  • Own the Business Foundations technology budget, tracking cost and value delivered.
  • Manage strategic vendors (SAP FICO and Ariba, Workday and Sustaira), system integrators and application support partners.

People and leadership

  • Lead, develop and transform the team, and build a succession pipeline for key roles.
  • Shape the operating model and sourcing mix across in-house teams both in Switzerland and Portugal and external partners.
  • Build a culture of ownership, collaboration and continuous improvement.

Stakeholder partnership

  • Act as the senior technology adviser to function leaders and their leadership teams, working alongside Product Management to bring expertise on technology strategy, architecture, platforms, delivery capability, cost, risk and innovation.
  • Explain technology trade-offs in business terms.
  • Lead the technology-side change required to adopt new ways of working, platforms and engineering practices, and support Product Management and the Functions in broader business adoption and change management.

What success looks like

In the first year, success means a roadmap the six function leaders back, visible delivery against it, and a simpler, better-controlled estate.

  • First 90 days: Understand every function’s priorities and product backlog, mapped to the technology estate across SAP, Workday, SAP Ariba, and Sustaira with its costs and risks, assess how teams deliver today, and confirm the key focus areas.
  • By 6 months: a well-defined product operating model, and the first product teams operating accordingly and delivering value.
  • By 12 months: product teams work in harmony with business owners across all value streams, priority programmes are on track, and measurable gains in at least two business areas.

Measures we will track

  • Business satisfaction of function leaders and end users
  • Roadmap delivery on time, on budget and on benefits
  • Delivery flow, such as lead time from idea to production and release frequency
  • Share of change vs run as well as spend funded through products rather than projects
  • Platform availability and incident trends for critical services
  • Cost to run and applications retired
  • Audit findings raised and closed

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

Essential skills and experience

  • 12+ years in technology, including 5+ years leading enterprise applications or corporate-function technology teams in a large, matrixed organisation.
  • A proven track record of transforming ways of working at scale: moving technology and business teams from project-based delivery to agile, product-led models, and making the change stick.
  • Hands-on experience of setting up and running a product operating model: product management interfaces, value-stream teams, outcome-based roadmaps and product funding.
  • Deep practical knowledge of agile frameworks (Scrum, Kanban, SAFe or similar) and DevOps, and of how to apply them to SAP and SaaS platforms.
  • A track record of delivering large transformations on SAP, Workday or SAP Ariba, from business case to realised benefits, experience with S/4 is a plus.
  • Strong understanding of corporate processes: record-to-report, source-to-pay, hire-to-retire, contract lifecycle, ESG reporting and real estate portfolio management.
  • Grounding in integration architecture, master data management and data governance across SAP and Workday.
  • Experience in controlled environments: internal controls, external audit and data privacy.
  • Commercial skill in managing strategic vendors, system integrators and large budgets
  • Executive presence and credibility with the CFO, Chief People Officer, General Counsel and their peers.

Desirable skills and experience

  • Experience of SAP S/4HANA transformation, including clean-core principles.
  • Experience deploying AI and automation in corporate functions, such as the AI capabilities built into SAP and Workday, agents and intelligent document processing.
  • Experience with Sustaira or another ESG data platform, and with reporting standards such as CSRD and ISSB.
  • Luxury retail experience is an advantage.
  • A degree in IT; SAFe, Scrum or product management certifications are a plus.

Leadership qualities

  • Business first: starts from the outcome a function needs, not from the technology.
  • Change leader: brings people through new ways of working by example and coaching, not by mandate.
  • Empowers teams: gives product teams clear outcomes and the authority to deliver them.
  • Simplifier: removes complexity, retires what no longer adds value, and says no when it matters.
  • Builds trust across boundaries: brings six functions with competing priorities to shared decisions.
  • Delivers with rigour: clear plans, honest reporting and early escalation of risk.
  • Develops people: grows leaders, coaches openly and builds inclusive, high-performing teams.
  • Curious: keeps up with new technology, especially AI, and turns it into practical value.
