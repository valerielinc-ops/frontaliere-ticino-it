Descrizione completa

Shape the Future and Create the Value That Matters with AI

The AI & Analytics Solution Owner team is part of the Analytics Center of Excellence at Hilti and plays a key role in unlocking the value of AI, Analytics and Automation for Hilti and our customers. Working closely together with business functions, we help shape the vision and roadmap and solutions across different business areas with strong partnerships and actively develop the solution portfolio at Hilti. As a Solution Owner, you will get the opportunity to work on AI, analytics and automation driven impactful use cases in a large organization that is leading the digital transformation in the construction sector.

This full-time position is available as soon as possible. No travel is required.

What You’ll Bring

  • Master's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Data Science, or Information Systems; a PhD’s degree is a plus.
  • 8-10 years of total professional working experience, with good understanding of business processes and/or digital product design or development.
  • Ability to bridge the gap between business needs and technological solutions; for example, in AI, Analytics or Automation space.
  • Solid understanding of the full lifecycle of data and AI solutions, with practical knowledge of data platforms, automation, AI (incl. Gen AI and Agentic AI) or Machine Learning.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to partner with business stakeholders.
