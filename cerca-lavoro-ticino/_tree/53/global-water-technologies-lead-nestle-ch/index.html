Be a force for good and inspire high-performing Operations teams

As a Global Water Technologies Lead at Global Operations , your mission is to lead technical expertise in water and wastewater treatment and to guide a worldwide network of water experts, so our factories run safe, efficient and sustainable water systems. We are a global community of 150K+ colleagues across the value chain –Manufacturing & Engineering, Supply Chain, Quality, Procurement and Agriculture– driving focus, speed and meaningful impact for consumers. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with factory engineering teams, regional and market technical leaders, sustainability champions, procurement teams, suppliers, universities and research institutes. This is your opportunity to strengthen culture, competitiveness, and future-ready capabilities as #OneOperations

How you’ll make an impact as a Global Water Technologies Lead

  • Leading technical expertise in water and wastewater treatment across Nestlé factories worldwide, setting the standard for safe, efficient and compliant systems.
  • Driving the water use reduction strategy by applying the Reduce, Reuse, Recycle model and introducing new technologies and digital solutions.
  • Building and guiding a global network of water experts who support wells, water treatment and effluent treatment operations and projects.
  • Delivering major investment projects, from new wells to water recycling systems, through standardised and future-ready technologies.
  • Safeguarding food safety, quality and the environment by shaping standards for water as an ingredient, legionella prevention and effluent management.
  • Growing talent and partnerships by developing expert careers, global training programs and collaboration with universities, suppliers and knowledge partners.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing recognized expertise in water treatment and effluent treatment, from system design to daily operations.
  • Leading and inspiring technical teams and expert networks through influence, coaching and collaboration.
  • Using a problem-solving and continuous improvement mindset to manage risk, compliance, cost and performance.
  • Communicating fluently in English.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

What to expect from our hiring process

We are committed to an inclusive and respectful hiring experience and are happy to provide reasonable accommodations if needed. Our workplaces are fully accessible and supported by a strong safety culture, with modern installations and procedures to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

The hiring process typically includes an interview with Talent Acquisition, an interview with the hiring team, and a final discussion and offer. We review applications as they are received and close the role once a strong list of relevant candidates is identified, so we encourage you to apply early. All information is treated confidentially.

Stay vigilant: Nestlé will only contact candidates via official company email addresses and will never ask for payments or sensitive personal information at any stage of the hiring process.
