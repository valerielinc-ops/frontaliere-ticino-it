The Position The Manufacturing Science & Technology (MSAT) team operates in an agile framework to continuously improve the manufacturing of highly integrated sensors and cartridges (S&C). Our work spans from supporting early concept ideas to enabling efficient design transfer into routine manufacturing and ensuring sustainable product care throughout the product lifecycle. Organized into Sub-Networks and Squads supported by Chapters, our agile structure enables rapid adaptation to changing priorities and business needs. We foster collaboration, continuous learning, and mutual mentorship while driving partnerships across and beyond Operations. By combining expertise, data-driven insights, digital solutions, and new technologies, we solve complex challenges and deliver high-quality S&C products that meet customer needs throughout their lifecycle — always guided by our core values: Courage, Transparency, Open-Mindedness, and a Can Do Attitude. The Opportunity:

  • Taking responsibility for regular product care activities within the Squad. Like handling of non-conformity notifications, leading investigations and root cause analysis, coordinating corrective actions in the manufacturing process, implementing changes to the process.
  • Working according to internal processes defined by GxP-regulated environment
  • Monitoring production data and analyzing key production performance indicators
  • Driving Continuous Improvement initiations within the sensor production process
  • Supporting production operators as an SME on the ramp up of newly established production line
  • Plan and execute experiments (DoEs) in laboratory and production environment for investigation and product improvements
  • Coordination and collaboration with internal and external partners Who you are:
  • University Degree in (Electro-, Analytical- or Technical) Chemistry, (Chemical-, Process- or Manufacturing) Engineering or related field
  • First experience in a highly regulated production environment and GxP standards (pharma / medical preferred)
  • Strong team player with self-reflection, solution-oriented mindset, and collaboration skills
  • Strategic thinking with interest in agile framework (Scrum/Kanban) and project management
  • Ability to apply a programming language (e.g., python or R-Studio) or AI Agents in a practical setting
  • Fluent in English, German at least B2 level
  • Nice to have: statistical analysis, database handling (SQL, Snowflake) or experience with software projects Who we are A healthier future drives us to innovate. Together, more than 100’000 employees across the globe are dedicated to advance science, ensuring everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Our efforts result in more than 26 million people treated with our medicines and over 30 billion tests conducted using our Diagnostics products. We empower each other to explore new possibilities, foster creativity, and keep our ambitions high, so we can deliver life-changing healthcare solutions that make a global impact. Let’s build a healthier future, together. Roche is an Equal Opportunity Employer.
