Job Description Summary Location: Basel #LI-Hybrid

This role is based in Basel. Novartis is unable to offer relocation support for this role: please only apply if this location is accessible for you.

Shape the operational strength of a global Legal Brand Protection Support function while leading and developing a high-performing paralegal team. This working manager role combines hands-on operational trademark portfolio management with team leadership, taking responsibility for legal brand protection operational activities, global trademark procedures and IP systems. You will work closely with Legal Brand Protection attorneys, Legal Innovation, Procurement, Finance and Intellectual Property Support colleagues to strengthen collaboration, improve ways of working and maintain effective brand protection operations worldwide.

Job Description

Responsibilities

  • Lead, develop and inspire the Legal Brand Protection paralegal team as a centre of expertise for Legal Brand Protection support matters, including administrative procedures, while fostering a collaborative, solution-oriented and stakeholder-focused culture.
  • Independently manage operational activities across the Legal Brand Protection portfolio, including deadlines, processes, guidelines and resource requirements, to maintain effective portfolio management and business continuity.
  • Oversee the effective use and quality control of the intellectual property management and electronic document management systems, and develop and implement global procedures and guidelines that improve the efficiency and effectiveness of Legal Brand Protection operations.
  • Apply knowledge of worldwide trademark prosecution and maintenance formalities and enforcement procedures, trademark litigation principles in major markets, and relevant law, case law and regulations across jurisdictions to manage Legal Brand Protection operational activities effectively.
  • Manage Legal Brand Protection vendor engagements, budgets and litigation-related costs in close collaboration with Finance and other relevant functions, including vendor onboarding and ongoing relationships, invoice management and budgeting.
  • Maintain continuity of Legal Brand Protection operational procedures and effective deputy coverage, including leading meetings, distributing workloads during unplanned absences and ensuring business continuity in trademark related operational support.
  • Lead the recruitment, onboarding, training, performance appraisal and development of Legal Brand Protection paralegals, while collaborating with Legal Brand Protection leadership, attorneys, Legal Innovation, Procurement, Finance and Intellectual Property Support teams on functional initiatives, meetings and paralegal involvement.

Essential for the role

  • A university degree or relevant legal, commercial or management education and experience.
  • Proven experience as a trademark paralegal, or equivalent experience in the IP or legal field.
  • Fluency in English, with excellent written and verbal communication skills, particularly in an intellectual property context.
  • Demonstrated ability to work independently when preparing reports and supporting documents for subsequent attorney review.
  • Strong ability to communicate with stakeholders across multiple countries, constructively challenge established approaches, plan creatively and adapt to stakeholder needs.
  • Proven ability to lead, energise and motivate a team, together with experience designing, delivering and coordinating training and coaching to build team capability and individual development.

Desirable for the role

  • A trademark paralegal qualification, law or intellectual property-related degree, or relevant professional certification.
  • Proficiency in an additional language alongside English.
