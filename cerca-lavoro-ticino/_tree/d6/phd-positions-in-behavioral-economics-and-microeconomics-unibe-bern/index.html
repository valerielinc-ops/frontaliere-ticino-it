Descrizione completa

UniBE is prestigious. With us, you benefit from the excellent reputation of a long-standing institution all around the world.

Your responsibilities

  • Doctoral research with Jean-Michel Benkert (up to two positions).
  • You will join a group of PhD students, postdocs, and faculty who exchange ideas in reading groups, seminars, and research discussions.
  • We provide close supervision and support careers in academic research and public or private institutions.
  • Successful candidates participate in the group's teaching and research activities and normally attend first-year doctoral courses at the Study Center Gerzensee.

Your profile

  • Applicants must hold, or expect to complete before starting, a master's degree in economics or another relevant discipline, with an excellent academic record.
  • Beyond economics, we welcome backgrounds including mathematics, physics, computer science, psychology, sociology, and business.
  • We value research potential, strong analytical skills, and sufficient quantitative preparation for rigorous doctoral coursework in economics.
  • Proficiency in English is required.
  • We welcome applications from qualified candidates of all backgrounds.

International reputation Application and Contact Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis until the positions are filled. Full application instructions and cover-letter guidance: Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis until the positions are filled. Please send a CV, a cover letter (maximum one page), your master's thesis or another research sample, and academic transcripts directly to Jean-Michel Benkert (jean-michel.benkert@unibe.ch). Please arrange for one academic recommendation letter to be sent to the same address. Questions are welcome at this address. We are interested in your thinking. Please write your cover letter yourself, using a concrete question or experience to introduce your research interests and yourself. AI tools may assist with language correction, but please do not submit an AI-generated letter. With us, you create value. For yourself and for society.

About the positions

One position is funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) through the project "When Do Expectations Bite? Micro-Founding Reference-Dependent Preferences." The project combines theory and experiments to study how expectations shape preferences and economic decisions. The second position is departmentally funded and offers scope for broader research in microeconomics, including behavioral economics, mechanism design, organizational economics, and industrial organization.
