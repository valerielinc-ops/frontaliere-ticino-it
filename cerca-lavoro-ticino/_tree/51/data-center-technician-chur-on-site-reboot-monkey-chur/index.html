Descrizione completa

Reboot Monkey provides on-site technical services inside third-party datacenters, in 190 countries and more than 400 locations. We deliver the work our clients order and engage independent technicians who live near those datacenters to carry out the hands-on jobs. Your contract is with RM Staffing B.V. (Tappersweg 10F, 2031 ET Haarlem, the Netherlands), the Reboot Monkey company that engages independent technicians. You work as a self-employed contractor, not on our payroll. How the work runs

  • We offer jobs in and around Chur as work orders come in. You accept or decline each job; there is no obligation either way.
  • A job takes from 30 minutes to a few days. There are no guaranteed hours and no fixed weekly pattern.
  • Some jobs are urgent or fall at night or at weekends. You choose whether to take them, and urgent or out-of-hours jobs pay a higher rate.
  • You decide how to carry out each job, within the site's safety and access rules and the work order for that task.
  • You are free to work for other companies and clients at the same time.
  • You bring your own laptop and basic hand tools.

Levels in Switzerland

  • Level 1: escort and access, basic smart hands; at least 6 months of hands-on IT hardware work.
  • Level 2: rack and stack, cabling and hardware swaps; at least 1 year of hands-on datacenter or IT hardware work.
  • Level 3: break-fix on servers, storage and network gear; at least 3 years of datacenter or IT infrastructure work.
  • Level 4: lead technician for installs and migrations; at least 5 years of datacenter work.

Your level is agreed after a short technical call and can go up as you complete more complex jobs. How you are paid

  • Per hour, on the hours you log for each job, paid every week.
  • Every job pays at least 30 minutes on business days and at least 1 hour at weekends.
  • A travel allowance of CHF 14 per visit.
  • You handle your own taxes and any registration your country asks of self-employed people.

Typical jobs

  • Rack and stack: installing and moving servers, network gear and other datacenter equipment.
  • Hardware swaps: disks, memory, power supplies, optics and other parts.
  • Structured cabling in copper and fiber: patching, labelling, cable management and connectivity checks.
  • Break-fix: diagnosing and fixing server, storage and network hardware faults (Cisco, Juniper and others).
  • Basic Linux and Windows tasks during installs, updates and troubleshooting.
  • Photos, ticket notes and asset records for every job, and clear updates to our remote team.

What you bring

  • At least 6 months of hands-on work with servers, network gear or other IT hardware, or a completed IT hardware course.
  • You follow a written procedure step by step and report clearly with photos and notes.
  • A business registration in Switzerland (AHV affiliation as self-employed), or you arrange one before your first job.
  • You live within about an hour's drive of Chur.
  • You can legally work as a self-employed contractor in Switzerland. We cannot sponsor visas or work permits.
  • Working English, to talk with our remote team.
  • Some datacenters run their own identity and access checks before your first visit.
  • Certifications such as CompTIA A+, Server+, Linux+, CCNA or JNCIA are a plus.

How to apply

  • Apply here and answer the short questions.
  • A short call with our recruitment team about your experience and your level.
  • You sign the independent contractor agreement with RM Staffing B.V. and start receiving job offers near you.

Requisiti

  • Istruzione

  • Laurea o laurea magistrale in Informatica, Ingegneria dell'Informazione o campo correlato è preferita ma non richiesta.

  • Esperienza

  • 3+ anni di esperienza in operazioni dei data center, con un forte focus su progetti indipendenti o freelance.

  • Abilità tecniche

  • Estesa esperienza pratica con apparecchiature Cisco e Juniper.

  • Profonda competenza nella gestione e risoluzione di problemi con sistemi Windows e Linux.

  • Solida comprensione dei principi di rete (TCP/IP, DNS, VPN, VLAN).
