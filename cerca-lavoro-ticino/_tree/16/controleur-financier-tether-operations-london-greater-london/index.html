About the job: The Financial Controller will oversee financial reporting, audit coordination, and accounting operations across a multi-entity investment and treasury platform. The role requires a technically strong finance professional with deep accounting expertise, strong operational discipline, and the ability to operate in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

  • Prepare accurate and timely financial statements, management accounts, and supporting reports in accordance with IFRS and US GAAP.
  • Manage day-to-day accounting operations across multiple entities and jurisdictions.
  • Oversee month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close processes, including general ledger reconciliations and control reviews.
  • Maintain oversight of accounts payable, accounts receivable, accruals, and balance sheet reconciliations.
  • Lead coordination with external auditors, ensuring timely delivery of audit requests, supporting documentation, and issue resolution.
  • Support preparation of any regulatory, tax, statutory and ad hoc reporting requirements.
  • Strengthen and maintain internal controls, accounting policies, and financial governance frameworks.
  • Drive continuous improvement across accounting processes, reporting workflows, and operational controls.
  • Leverage technology and automation to improve efficiency, scalability, and reporting accuracy.
  • Partner closely with operations to ensure integrity of financial data and reporting.
  • Assist with implementation and optimisation of financial systems and reporting infrastructure.
  • Support ad hoc financial analysis, projects, and strategic initiatives as required. Qualifications & Experience:
  • Bachelor’s degree in accounting, finance, or a related field; ACCA, ACA, CPA, CFA, or equivalent certifications.
  • 10+ years of experience in financial control, audit, or accounting, preferably at a credit hedge fund. Prior experience in digital assets or blockchain ecosystems is a distinct advantage.
  • Proficiency in Xero and Enfusion portfolio management system.
  • Proficiency in Excel + VBA / Python is mandatory.
  • Exceptional analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Strong understanding of regulatory and tax requirements.
  • Excellent attention to detail and communication skills.
