Descrizione completa

Main Responsibilities The Head of Facilities at UBP UK Ltd is responsible for the strategic and operational management of the organisation’s facilities, ensuring that offices are safe, secure, compliant, well maintained and fit for purpose. The role oversees building services, maintenance, health and safety, security, space planning, suppliers, budgets and facilities-related projects, while supporting business continuity and an effective workplace environment. The position also provides regional oversight of the facilities in the Jersey, Guernsey and Gibraltar offices, including the management and coordination of the local Facilities Managers to ensure consistent standards, governance and service delivery across all locations. This is an office-based role.

Key Responsibilities

Building Management and Technical Maintenance

  • Provide strategic leadership and overall accountability for the facilities operations of UBP’s offices in the UK and Gibraltar, ensuring that all offices, including their technical installations are maintained to consistently high standards.
  • Support the facilities management, maintenance and operational strategy for all locations, supporting business continuity, regulatory compliance, operational resilience and employee wellbeing.
  • Maintain oversight of critical building systems and infrastructure, including HVAC, electrical and power distribution, plumbing, lifts, lighting, fire detection systems and the building fabric, ensuring that appropriate maintenance arrangements are in place.
  • Lead the development, procurement and governance of facilities and maintenance contracts, ensuring that scopes of work, service level agreements and key performance indicators are clearly defined and aligned with business requirements.
  • Provide oversight of the performance of the building management company and other facilities service providers, including cleaning, maintenance and security contractors, using regular service reviews and performance reporting to drive continuous improvement.
  • Direct the facilities support function by setting priorities, allocating responsibilities to local Facilities Managers and ensuring that requests, incidents and corrective maintenance actions are appropriately managed through to timely resolution.
  • Maintain appropriate governance and reporting arrangements, ensuring the accuracy and completeness of work orders, inspection records, statutory certificates and other facilities-related documentation.
  • Maintain strategic oversight of the asset register and lifecycle planning, anticipating equipment replacement, refurbishment and capital investment requirements and incorporating these into forward plans and budgets.
  • Provide leadership and direction on building maintenance activities, ensuring that minor works are appropriately managed internally and that specialist contractors are appointed and supervised for more complex requirements.
  • Ensure that building signage and related facilities arrangements comply with applicable legal, regulatory and organisational requirements.
  • Act as the senior point of contact for the landlord, building management representatives and senior management on facilities-related matters, representing UBP’s interests and ensuring that issues are escalated and resolved appropriately.
  • Ensure that appropriate building insurance arrangements are maintained in coordination with the relevant insurance contact in Geneva, and oversee the management and follow-up of claims where required.

Compliance and Safety

  • Manage compliance with applicable UK building regulations, health and safety legislation and organisational standards, including the Building Regulations 2010 and the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974.
  • Coordinate and support regular health and safety audits, inspections and risk assessments, ensuring that identified actions are recorded, monitored and completed within agreed timescales.
  • Manage the delivery of planned preventative maintenance (PPM) activities for building systems, equipment and infrastructure, ensuring that schedules are maintained and any exceptions are appropriately escalated.
  • Oversee the effective operation and maintenance of building security arrangements, including access control systems, ensuring that access permissions and related controls are managed appropriately.
  • Liaise with the Security Manager in Geneva to prepare and provide monthly reporting, and support the implementation, monitoring and adherence of relevant Group security procedures

Projects and Space Management

  • Manage space planning, occupancy data, desk and floor allocation, and internal moves
  • Lead or support small works projects, from scoping and budgeting to delivery and handover.
  • Contribute to the workplace strategy, including hybrid working, wellbeing and employee experience initiatives.

Budget Management & Administration

  • Manage the facilities budget, monitoring expenditure on a monthly basis, analysing variances and providing clear financial reporting to senior management.
  • Review facilities-related cost lines, including contracts, utilities, consumables, service charges, catering and security, to identify and deliver cost efficiencies without compromising service quality, safety or compliance.
  • Lead facilities-related tender processes and contract negotiations in collaboration with the Procurement team, including supplier benchmarking and the assessment of value for money in accordance with internal procurement policies.
  • Ensure that facilities contracts are accurately recorded in the Bank’s systems, with key dates, renewals and expiries appropriately monitored and managed.
  • Manage the integration of contracts and purchase orders through the Coupa accounting system, ensuring that supplier invoices are reviewed, validated and allocated accurately to the appropriate cost centres.
  • Assist with consolidate building consumption data and maintain supporting evidence, including meter readings, utility invoices and other relevant documentation, for submission to the CSR Group team.
  • Review and update relevant facilities procedures as required, ensuring that they remain accurate, effective and aligned with internal requirements

Physical Security

  • Hold responsibility for the maintenance of physical security of the building, its occupants, visitors and assets.
  • Overseeing operations of the external contracted security provider: guarding schedules, post instructions, training, performance and contractual KPIs.
  • Ensure that there are regular audits of building access rights, ensuring joiners, movers and leavers are processed promptly and correctly.
  • Produce monthly security reports (incidents, access audits, alarms, KPIs) with recommendations for improvement.
  • Carry out security risk assessments and liaise with local authorities when required.

Business Continuity and Emergency Management

  • Lead the on-site response to incidents (system failures, floods, power outages, security events) and ensure clear communication to staff and management.
  • Ensure Fire Wardens and First Aider resources are delivered appropriately across all offices and in line with H&S regulation.

Team leadership and Stakeholder Management

  • Lead, develop and motivate the facilities managers at UBP UK.
  • Manage outsourced partners (security, cleaning, catering, technical maintenance) as an integrated service team.
  • Build strong relationships with internal stakeholders
  • Produce a monthly facilities dashboard covering maintenance, compliance, security, budget, hospitality and sustainability.

The job description is not exhaustive and may include other duties as assigned from time-to-time by management.

Qualifications

Qualifications and Professional Knowledge

  • Degree-level qualification in Facilities Management, Business Administration, Engineering, Property Management or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience.
  • Relevant professional qualifications, such as IWFM Level 4 or 5, IOSH Managing Safely, NEBOSH or an equivalent health and safety qualification, would be advantageous.
  • SIA qualification or relevant experience in managing contracted security services would be beneficial.
  • Strong working knowledge of UK building regulations, health and safety legislation and associated compliance requirements, including the Building Regulations 2010, the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, fire safety requirements and accessibility standards.
  • Good understanding of building services and technical infrastructure, including HVAC, electrical systems, power distribution, plumbing, lifts, lighting, fire detection and building management systems.

Experience

  • Significant experience in a senior facilities management role, with responsibility for multiple sites, complex buildings and technical installations.
  • Proven experience of leading facilities operations within a regulated, corporate, financial services or client-focused environment where high standards of service, security and confidentiality are essential.
  • Experience of managing and developing Facilities Managers, local teams and outsourced service providers across multiple locations.
  • Demonstrable experience of managing building management companies and contractors, including the negotiation, implementation and performance management of service contracts, SLAs and KPIs.
  • Experience of managing planned preventative maintenance, statutory compliance, health and safety audits, risk assessments and corrective actions.
  • Experience of overseeing physical security arrangements, access control systems, security contractors, incident reporting and security risk assessments.
  • Experience of managing facilities budgets, procurement processes, supplier negotiations, cost control and financial reporting.
  • Experience of space planning, occupancy management, workplace moves and the delivery of small works or refurbishment projects.
  • Experience of supporting business continuity, emergency response and the management of facilities-related incidents.

Leadership and Management Capability

  • Demonstrable ability to provide strategic direction while maintaining effective oversight of day-to-day facilities operations.
  • Strong people leadership skills, with the ability to develop, motivate and hold Facilities Managers and service providers accountable for performance.
  • Ability to establish clear priorities, delegate effectively and coordinate activity across multiple offices and stakeholder groups.
  • Experience of leading integrated service teams comprising internal employees and outsourced providers.
  • A collaborative and commercially aware approach to supplier, landlord and contractor management.

Technical and Operational Skills

  • Ability to assess building and infrastructure requirements, identify risks and ensure that appropriate maintenance and lifecycle plans are in place.
  • Strong understanding of contract governance, asset management, planned maintenance and facilities-related documentation.
  • Ability to interpret technical information, service reports, compliance records and performance data, and use these to inform decisions.
  • Proficiency in facilities management systems, building management systems, access control platforms, procurement tools and Microsoft Office applications.
  • Ability to produce clear management information and dashboards covering facilities performance, compliance, security, budget, sustainability and service delivery.
  • A practical and solutions-focused approach, with the confidence to provide direction during operational issues while recognising when specialist support or escalation is required.

Personal Attributes

  • Excellent organisational, planning and prioritisation skills, with the ability to manage competing demands across multiple locations.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a structured approach to identifying risks, resolving issues and delivering improvements.
  • Clear, confident and professional communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to engage effectively with senior management, employees, landlords, contractors and Group stakeholders.
  • Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills, with the ability to build effective relationships and represent UBP’s interests appropriately.
  • High level of integrity, discretion and professionalism, particularly when handling sensitive information and matters relating to security, incidents and business continuity.
  • Proactive, accountable and service-oriented, with a commitment to maintaining high standards of safety, compliance, quality and employee experience.
  • Flexible and resilient, with the ability to respond calmly and effectively to emergencies, operational disruption and changing business requirements.
  • Willingness to travel between UBP offices in the UK and Gibraltar, and to work outside normal hours when required to support incidents, projects or business continuity arrangements.
