Descrizione completa

Do you enjoy tackling complex business challenges, connecting the dots across different topics and turning analysis into practical recommendations? Join Swiss Re's Group Underwriting team and contribute to initiatives that shape how underwriting decisions are made across the organisation. About the Role You will work on a broad range of strategic initiatives within Group Underwriting, covering topics such as underwriting costing, performance and governance. The role offers the opportunity to combine technical understanding with strategic thinking, gain exposure to different areas of the (re)insurance business and contribute to senior management decision-making. Key Responsibilities

  • Strategic initiatives: Take ownership of defined workstreams within Group-wide underwriting initiatives, from structuring business problems and conducting analyses to developing recommendations and supporting implementation.
  • Business and technical analysis: Analyse topics across underwriting costing and governance, connecting technical considerations with business implications and identifying opportunities for improvement.
  • Cross-functional collaboration: Work with stakeholders across business units and Group Functions, bringing together different perspectives, challenging assumptions and building alignment on recommendations and next steps.
  • Management decision support: Develop well-founded recommendations and prepare clear, concise presentations and decision materials for senior management.

About the Team You will join the Underwriting Costing & Governance team within Group Underwriting, reporting into the Group Chief Underwriting Officer. Our team combines technical expertise with strategic business perspectives, working on Group-wide underwriting costing methodologies, performance frameworks and governance principles. We work closely with Swiss Re's business units and Group Functions on topics spanning different areas of the underwriting business. You will be part of a collaborative team working on diverse topics, with broad exposure across the organisation.

About You

You are curious, versatile and enjoy getting to grips with unfamiliar topics. You combine strong analytical skills with a good understanding of the (re)insurance business and are equally comfortable exploring technical details and stepping back to see the bigger picture. You think independently, challenge assumptions and connect different perspectives to develop well-reasoned recommendations.

We are looking for candidates who bring

  • 3–5 years of relevant experience in (re)insurance , including underwriting, risk management, business transformation or related areas. Experience in strategy/management consulting or equity/credit research focused on (re)insurance is also valuable.
  • Solid understanding of (re)insurance economics , including the key drivers of underwriting profitability, performance and capital requirements.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills , with the ability to work with quantitative and qualitative information, understand technical concepts and draw meaningful business conclusions.
  • Structured and strategic thinking , with the ability to break down complex problems, connect different topics and develop actionable recommendations.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills , including the ability to explain complex topics clearly and concisely to senior stakeholders.
  • Curiosity, initiative and a collaborative mindset , with the ability to learn quickly, work independently and build effective relationships across teams.
  • A university degree in Business, Economics, Finance, Actuarial Science or a related discipline, and excellent command of English.
