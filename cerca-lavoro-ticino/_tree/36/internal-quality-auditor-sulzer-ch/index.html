About the role The entry-level Internal Quality Auditor is responsible for the execution of day-to-day quality assurance activities. This includes setting operational objectives, ensuring adherence to quality standards, and resolving routine quality issues. Key Responsibilities:

  • Implement and maintain QESH management systems in accordance with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001.
  • Ability and willingness to travel to Service Centers across the US 5-7 times per year.
  • Conduct internal audits, inspections, and risk assessments across production areas.
  • Lead incident investigations, identify root causes, and track corrective actions.
  • Provide training and guidance to employees on QESH policies and safe work practices.
  • Monitor and report on QESH KPIs, including non-conformities, near misses, and environmental metrics.
  • Ensure compliance with OSHA, EPA, and other relevant regulatory requirements.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives and lean manufacturing efforts
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate QESH into daily operations.
  • Maintain documentation and prepare for external audits and customer inspections.
  • Ensure compliance with internal and regulatory quality standards.
  • Investigate and resolve routine quality issues.
  • Support audits and continuous improvement initiatives Requirements:
  • Intermediate knowledge of MS Word MS Excel required
  • Basic knowledge or MS SharePoint and MS PowerPoint preferred
  • 1+ years of experience in Internal Quality Audits preferred but not required
  • Knowledge of quality assurance practices and tools.
  • Strong attention to detail and analytical thinking.
  • Effective communication and documentation abilities.
  • Familiarity with manufacturing environments.
