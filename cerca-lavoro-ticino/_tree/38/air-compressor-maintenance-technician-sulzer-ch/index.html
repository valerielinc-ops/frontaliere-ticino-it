About the role The Air Compressor Maintenance Technician is responsible for the installation, inspection, maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair of air compressors and related systems. This role ensures equipment operates safely, reliably, and efficiently while meeting company, customer, and regulatory standards. Responsibilities

  • Install, maintain, inspect, and repair industrial air compressors and auxiliary equipment
  • Perform preventive and corrective maintenance to minimize downtime
  • Diagnose mechanical, electrical, and pneumatic issues and recommend repairs
  • Conduct routine inspections and performance testing of compressor systems
  • Replace worn or defective parts, including motors, valves, seals, belts, and filters
  • Read and interpret technical manuals, schematics, and work orders
  • Ensure compliance with safety policies, procedures, and industry regulations
  • Document maintenance activities, inspections, and repairs accurately
  • Support equipment upgrades, modifications, and system improvements
  • Coordinate with operations, engineering, and management teams as needed
