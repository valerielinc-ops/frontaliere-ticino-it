The Position Mission As a Global Patient Care Compliance Assurance Lead (East/West), you will play a key role in ensuring the GPC - Global Patient Care Organization has the highest quality standards and remains compliant at all times. You will lead and manage a team of PRI & Case Monitoring Specialists. You will ensure Roche and partners' products and solutions meet global regulations, corporate standards, and expectations by overseeing the monitoring, reviewing, and timely reporting on the quality of interactions. You will help maintain an effective quality management system and governance, globally, and continually improve the governance to meet evolving business and compliance needs. Together with your team, you will implement a common quality framework across the GPC Hubs. You will analyze results regularly, conceive corrective and preventive measures in case of findings, and collaborate in the design of training concepts to overcome identified knowledge gaps. You will ensure the GPC Organization complies with regulatory requirements and corporate standards. You will communicate significant quality, or regulatory issues and risks that may impact operations or regulatory compliance to local, regional or global management. In addition, you will be supporting the preparation, coordination, and conduct of audits, ensuring the GPC Organization operates in an “inspection ready” manner and that audit measures are strictly implemented. Key Accountabilities

  • Effectively lead and develop a team of GPC PRI & Case Monitoring Specialists.
  • Design, implement, and continuously improve the Global Compliance Governance Model and related monitoring standards.
  • Support the end-to-end audit process, including preparation work, managing the audit schedule, and ensuring the timely implementation of audit-related measures.
  • Implement and follow up on measures to continuously maintain or optimize the overall compliance of GPC in the processing and documentation of customer concerns.
  • Act as a contact person and expert with the different interfaces for all quality and compliance topics, proactively and in an advisory manner.
  • Main responsible for auditing and analyzing the compliance levels of the cases handled by GPC Advisors.
  • Close collaboration with global safety and post-market quality teams to ensure end-to-end compliance in the complaint handling process.
  • Foster a culture of excellence, inclusivity, and collaboration while driving team and individual performance.
  • Cultivate an inspiring team environment that promotes diversity, overcoming the challenges of a multi-location setup.
  • Ensure the team delivers exceptional service while optimizing efficiency and effectiveness through continuous improvement.
  • Drive the talent acquisition and hiring process, focusing on retaining top talent and maintaining a positive work environment.
  • Provide proactive feedback and coaching to team members, managing performance and contributing to succession planning and talent management. Key skills and Experience
  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent professional experience.
  • A fluent command of English is a must; any other language is a plus.
  • Proven leadership experience, preferably in an international setting in a medical regulated environment.
  • Knowledge of Roche products, solutions, and systems is recommended.
