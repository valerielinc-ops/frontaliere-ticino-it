Descrizione completa

Your team

Pictet Alternative Advisors (PAA) is a leading alternative investment asset manager that is part of the Pictet Group, with a focus on private equity, private real estate, private credit and hedge funds. PAA has invested in its data foundations: a shared data architecture, a canonical model of the business and a first catalogue of datasets. PAA is now looking to hire a Head of Data, accountable for making this data estate trustworthy and usable by investment teams, operations and the AI tools working on their behalf. Data ownership sits with the business, domain by domain; the Head of Data builds and runs the model that makes this work, and owns the platform the data lives on. As Head of Data, you will report to the Chief Technology Officer and lead three teams: Data Solutions, which builds PAA's enterprise datasets and acquires data from external sources

  • Business Insights, which makes that data available to PAA teams for analytics; and Data Platform, which builds the foundations both run on. The technology function is small and hands-on, serving an organisation of about 160 people.

Your role

As part of the Technology team, your role will be

  • Setting the data governance model for PAA: naming owners for each data domain with the business, agreeing what each is accountable for, and running the forum where definitions and changes are decided.
  • Leading the three teams as one data function, with one roadmap and clear interfaces between building datasets, serving analytics and running the platform.
  • Representing data at senior level, explaining trade-offs, cost and risk to the Executive Committee and Pictet Group counterparts in plain terms.
  • Agreeing data quality standards with the domain owners, measuring them, and fixing root causes when they are missed.
  • Owning the data platform end to end, including architecture, pipelines, the canonical layer and the catalogue, and the decisions on what is built in-house, what is bought and which external data sources PAA acquires.
  • Turning the data map into a working catalogue, where every dataset carries an owner, a freshness commitment, a sensitivity classification and an access route, kept up to date by the pipeline.
  • Making PAA's data usable by AI, so that the data AI assistants and agents rely on is documented, governed and access-controlled, and investment teams can trust what those tools produce.
  • Developing the people in those teams, including their product managers, and coordinating with Pictet Group engineering resources and external providers.

Your profile

  • University degree in a quantitative or technical field, or qualification deemed equivalent.
  • More than ten years' experience in data, including several years leading a data function or platform team.
  • Direct experience with alternative investments data: funds and vehicles, commitments and cash flows, holdings and look-through, valuations and fees.
  • Hands-on technical depth: able to read and write SQL and Python, review a data model and judge an engineering proposal; experience with a modern data stack (for example dbt, a cloud data warehouse, orchestration and testing).
  • Practical experience preparing data for AI and analytics use, including documentation, lineage, classification and access control.
  • Proven ability to make shared data ownership work, getting business owners to take responsibility for their data without formal authority over them.
  • Senior stakeholder management skills and the ability to explain technical choices clearly to non-technical colleagues.
  • Pragmatic approach, favouring a standard the business adopts over a complete framework it does not.
  • Ability to work in a regulated environment, in a multicultural team and with teams in multiple locations.
  • Fluent in English
  • French is an advantage.
  • Must be resident in Switzerland or willing to relocate.
  • If you want to build the data foundations of an alternative investment business, and make them work for people and AI alike, you will probably be a natural fit for this role.
