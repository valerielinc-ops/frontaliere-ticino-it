About the role Job Summary The Sr. Manufacturing Quality Inspector is responsible for auditing, monitoring, and determining the quality of manufacturing processes and outputs. This role ensures compliance with internal and regulatory standards and supports the timely delivery of quality products. This job profile is intended for support-level staff that generally works autonomously and only requires closer supervision in non-routine situations. SUMMARY: Performs receipt and in-process dimensional and visual inspection of machined and fabricated parts, purchased items and finished assemblies. Create and record inspection results on appropriate inspection report using Microsoft Excel and Word. Reviews sub-contractor procedures and reports; and maintains department records and procedures for current code requirements. Position functions independently with occasional guidance from the Quality Assurance Manager. ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS: % OF TIME 60% Performs dimensional and visual inspection of machined and fabricated parts, purchased items and finished assemblies in accordance with appropriate specifications. 10% Monitors for compliance to processes and procedures. 10% Communicate with manufacturing personnel concerning priority of inspection. 10% Perform surveillance activities at Supplier facilities to determine compliance and acceptability of equipment/process being supplied. 10% Interfaces with 3rd party and Customer inspectors as required.   • Performs other duties as necessary SUPERVISORY RESPONSIBILITIES: None QUALIFICATIONS AND SKILLS:

  • Understanding appropriate application of inspection techniques
