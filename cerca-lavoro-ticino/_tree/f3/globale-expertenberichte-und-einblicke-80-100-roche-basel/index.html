At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position As the Global Expert Reporting & Insights, you will drive executive intelligence and portfolio transparency across major capital investment projects worldwide. Joining our Global Capital Investment team in Basel, you will translate complex project data into actionable business guidance using modern business intelligence, predictive modeling, and generative AI. This is an exciting opportunity to establish a single source of truth and shape data-driven decision-making across an international sites network.

The Opportunity

  • Deliver executive intelligence: Translate global cost, schedule, and risk data into clear, actionable insights for senior leadership decision-making.
  • Standardize portfolio metrics: Standardize key performance indicators (KPIs) and predictive early warning indicators across portfolio tools to anticipate cost, schedule, and scope impacts.
  • Deploy automated BI dashboards: Build and maintain interactive dashboards as a single source of truth, establishing standardized reporting across global project delivery frameworks.
  • Drive digital innovation and AI: Partner with digital strategy and IT teams to develop next-generation analytics products, predictive models, and Generative AI use cases.
  • Ensure data integrity: Oversee the functionality, data quality, and completeness of our central Project and Portfolio Management (PPM) system across multi-million-franc projects.
  • Consolidate portfolio performance: Monitor, consolidate, and report overarching project status for cost, schedule, risk, and change management at the global network level.

Who You Are

  • Educational background: You hold a Master’s degree (MSc or equivalent) in Engineering, Construction Management, Finance, Project Management, or a related discipline, ideally supported by certifications such as PMP, PfMP, CCP, or RMP.
  • Industry & PMO experience: You bring at least 12 years of experience in capital investment project controls or portfolio management within the pharmaceutical or life sciences industry, including 5 years leading or supporting PMO functions for large-scale portfolios (over 500M CHF).
  • Advanced technical mastery: You possess hands-on expertise in portfolio management software, enterprise scheduling tools (e.g., Primavera P6, MS Project), risk modeling techniques (e.g., Monte Carlo), and Earned Value Management (EVM).
  • Matrix leadership & influence: You are a collaborative, courageous leader who excels at building cross-cultural relationships, listening actively, and influencing stakeholders across a matrix organization without direct authority.
  • Analytical & continuous improvement mindset: You combine strong systematic thinking with transparent decision-making, taking pride in establishing data governance, integrity, and capability building.
  • Language & mobility: You are fluent in English (with German skills or a willingness to learn) and are open to international travel up to 20%.
