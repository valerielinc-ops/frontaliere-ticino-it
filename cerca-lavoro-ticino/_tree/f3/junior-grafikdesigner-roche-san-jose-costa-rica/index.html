Your Opportunity: As a Junior Graphic Designer you will collaborate with senior designers, clients, and team members to understand design requirements and objectives. They'll assist in developing design solutions that effectively communicate messages and align with project goals. By leveraging their creative skills and design software knowledge, they contribute to the production of various design assets across different mediums, including print, digital, and social media. Junior Graphic Designers work under the guidance of senior designers to ensure consistency and adherence to brand guidelines in their design deliverables. They assist in the creation of layouts, selecting appropriate typography, and incorporating visual elements to enhance the overall visual appeal. Support the design team in creating visually compelling designs, gaining hands-on experience, and developing their skills in various design aspects. Junior Graphic Designers play a crucial role in assisting the team to deliver high-quality designs that effectively communicate messages and contribute to the overall success of design projects.
