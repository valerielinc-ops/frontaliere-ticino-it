Descrizione completa

It's more than a job

Join Kuehne+Nagel and take a leading role in shaping the reputation and narrative of the global market leader in Sea Logistics. As Communications Manager Sea Logistics, you will serve as a trusted advisor to senior leadership, develop and execute strategic communication initiatives, and drive consistent messaging across global audiences. Your work will directly support business priorities while enhancing our visibility, credibility, and influence worldwide.

How you create impact

  • Develop and execute communication strategies for the global Sea Logistics business.
  • Advise senior leaders on strategic and executive communications.
  • Lead employee communications and engagement initiatives.
  • Manage media relations and build strong journalist networks.
  • Drive social media and thought leadership activities.
  • Create impactful content across internal and external channels.
  • Support reputation management and crisis communications.
  • Partner with global stakeholders to ensure consistent messaging.

What we would like you to bring

  • Several years of experience in Corporate Communications, PR, or Media Relations.
  • Proven expertise in internal and external communications.
  • Excellent writing, storytelling, and presentation skills.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills and experience working with senior leadership teams.
  • Strategic and execution-oriented, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, international environment.
  • Willingness and ability to travel internationally up to 25% of the time.
  • Experience in logistics is an asset.
  • Fluent English communication skills; German is an asset.

What's in it for you

  • Attractive compensation package including performance-based bonus and employee benefits.
  • A highly visible role with direct exposure to senior international leaders.
  • The opportunity to shape the reputation and communications of Kuehne+Nagel's largest global business unit.
  • Global career development opportunities within one of the world's leading logistics companies.
  • Modern office environment with excellent public transport connections, free parking, and discounted public transport passes.
  • Flexible and collaborative working environment with international exposure and cross-functional partnerships.

Who are we

Logistics shapes everyday life - from the goods we consume to the healthcare we rely on. At Kuehne+Nagel, your work goes beyond logistics; it enables both ordinary and special moments in the lives of people around the world.

As a global leader with a strong heritage and a vision to move the world forward, we offer a safe, stable environment where your career can make a real difference. Whether we help deliver life-saving medicines, develop sustainable transportation solutions or support our local communities, your career will contribute to more than you can imagine.
