Descrizione completa

You will join the Service Management and Support Group, which promotes service management best practices and tools across CERN. Working with an integrated service management platform and Service Portal based on ServiceNow, you will contribute to the configuration, customisation, extension and support of tools serving all CERN services. As a member of the ServiceNow developer team, you will play an active role in shaping the future of the platform, supporting continuous improvement and helping deliver efficient, high-quality services across the Organization.

Your responsibilities

  • You contribute to various new developments and platform customisations, including exploration and implementation of AI features offered by the platform.
  • You participate to the introduction of AI driven chat based support through the CERN Service Portal complemented by AI enabled automations replacing largely manual service request and support processes.
  • Your contribution will be key to refactoring existing processes, such as service catalogue, incident and request-management as well as integration with CERN's personnel and asset management databases.
  • You contribute to the operation, configuration and support of the platform to ensure first-class availability, including rota and end-user support duties, smooth application of patches and platform-wide upgrades released by the platform provider, for a system handling tickets for over 750 services across CERN.

Your profile

  • Previous work experience in managing a large scale commercial software platform such as ServiceNow in a customised local environment is an advantage but not a requirement.
  • Experience with complex data models, database design and data integration (ETL) would be an asset but not required.
  • Practical experience building applications that use LLMs or machine learning, such as chatbots or assistants, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), text classification or AI-driven automation would be an asset.

Skills

  • Strong programming skills, in particular good knowledge of JavaScript. Python is a plus.
  • Web application debugging and web services (REST/SOAP).
  • Experience with front-end technologies (HTML/CSS/jQuery). AngularJS is an advantage.
  • Spoken and written English or French, with a commitment to learn the basics of the other language.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • You have a professional background in Computing (or a related field) and have either:
  • a Master's degree with 2 to 6 years of post-graduation professional experience;
  • or a PhD with no more than 3 years of post-graduation professional experience.
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
