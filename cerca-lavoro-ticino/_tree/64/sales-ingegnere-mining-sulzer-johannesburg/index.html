About the role Job Summary The Sales Engineer is responsible for driving sales growth and expanding Sulzer’s market presence within the mining sector. The role combines strong technical understanding with commercial execution to position Sulzer as the preferred OEM partner for reliability, performance, and lifecycle value, while actively engaging with end users, EPCMs, consultants, and channel partners, particularly in greenfield and brownfield projects.
