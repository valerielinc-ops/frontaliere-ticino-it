Please ensure you apply for the position in the correct location for you Please note closing date for applications is 2 weeks from date of posting Step into a pivotal role at the heart of Novartis’ growth strategy, where you will lead and shape high-impact transactions that define the future of our portfolio. As Director, M&A Transactions, you will drive the end-to-end execution of complex deals, partnering across global functions to unlock external innovation, inform critical capital allocation decisions, and deliver sustained value creation. This is a uniquely high-visibility opportunity to influence enterprise strategy, operate at the forefront of deal-making, and play a central role in advancing Novartis’ ambition to transform patient outcomes worldwide. Job Description Key

  • Responsibilities
  • Lead end-to-end execution of mid-scale M&A transactions from deal strategy through signing and closing
  • Define and drive deal strategy, including timelines, governance planning, and engagement with target companies
  • Shape and negotiate commercial terms in partnership with finance, legal, tax, and cross-functional stakeholders
  • Build and lead high-performing, cross-functional deal teams to support robust decision-making at senior governance levels
  • Oversee corporate due diligence processes, ensuring comprehensive risk and value assessment across all functions
  • Develop and present clear, data-driven recommendations to executive committees and Board-level stakeholders
  • Partner with integration teams post-signing to ensure strategic value drivers are realized and successfully embedded Essential
  • Requirements
  • Proven experience leading end-to-end mergers and acquisitions transactions across multiple deal structures and geographies within a pharmaceutical, healthcare or Biotech organisation
  • Strong strategic and commercial acumen with ability to align transactions to enterprise growth priorities
  • Solid financial expertise, including financial modelling, valuation, and scenario planning
  • Demonstrated ability to lead complex, cross-functional teams and influence senior stakeholders
  • Deep understanding of risk assessment across strategic, financial, and operational dimensions
  • Advanced degree in business, finance, or life sciences Commitment to Diversity & Inclusion: Novartis is committed to building an outstanding, inclusive work environment and diverse teams representative of the patients and communities we serve.
