Descrizione completa

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters. The Position For over 60 years, Roche has been guided by a profound vision: a future where cancer can be cured. 22.8 million people have been treated with Roche cancer therapy as of 2025. We have had nearly 30 New Molecular Entities (NMEs) approved in our oncology and haematology portfolio. We are committed to developing innovative medicines and diagnostic solutions that help prevent, stop and cure cancer. Leading with bold moves, while grounded in science and experience, our focus is on turning scientific breakthroughs into better outcomes for people with cancer, healthcare systems and society. We are aiming to deliver seven new transformative cancer medicines by the end of the decade, for those that need them most. To help us achieve this vision we are looking for a dedicated Breast Cancer Specialist Representative to join our dynamic team. If you have a proven track record working in the pharmaceutical industry and a passion for the area of Breast Cancer then we want you to be a part of our success story. As a Breast Cancer Patient Journey Partner (PJP) , you will play a pivotal role in promoting our exciting portfolio of Breast Cancer treatments to healthcare professionals. You will be responsible for building and maintaining strong relationships with key Breast Cancer stakeholders, including oncologist and other respective healthcare professionals/practice staff, to create meaningful solutions that improve patient outcomes. The territory will focus on key Breast Cancer accounts across NSW and the ACT. What you will be working on: You will independently manage territory metrics and targets, showing resilience and confidence when challenging Healthcare Professionals (HCPs). You will work with your team to advance customers through the adoption ladder, drive market growth to meet our patient-focused goals, and contribute to the development of pioneering therapies. Responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Drive adoption of our Breast Cancer products with healthcare providers across NSW and the ACT.
  • Develop and execute territory plans to maximise opportunities to create value for our stakeholders and for Roche to and meet our goals
  • Build strong, long-term relationships with oncologist and other healthcare professionals
  • Review customer engagement metrics to optimise communication channels ensuring regular engagement with target customers through face to face meetings, virtual calls, dinner presentation meetings, clinic/practice luncheons and participation in industry events
  • Provide expert knowledge on the clinical data and benefits of our treatment
  • Collaborate internally with cross-functional teams (marketing, medical etc) to drive business growth and great customer experience
  • Monitor and analyse territory performance, market trends and competitor activity to adjust strategies/tactics to improve outcomes
  • Gather valuable insights from customers and effectively communicate them to the internal team to help validate and define our strategic initiatives.
  • Adhere to company policies and industry regulations, maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and ethics. Who you are: You are someone who demonstrates a passion for patient outcomes, enterprise-level thinking, and an entrepreneurial mindset with an excellent understanding of the pharmaceutical industry. You have:
  • 5+ years or equivalent level of experience as a specialist pharmaceutical representative or in similar capacity
  • In-depth knowledge of the healthcare system
