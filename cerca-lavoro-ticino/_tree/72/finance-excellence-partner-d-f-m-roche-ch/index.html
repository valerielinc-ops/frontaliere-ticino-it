The Position As Finance Excellence Partner (FxP) you collaborate with Commercial Finance colleagues across Diagnostics and beyond geographical boundaries in a networked team, to streamline finance operations by simplifying, standardizing, automating, and utilising insights for enhanced decision making. You also collaborate closely with local Finance Enterprise Partners, finance colleagues across the global Roche finance network and local commercial business partners. You contribute to fostering a culture of innovation and experimentation as well as drive automation and digitalization across finance. Your key responsibilities Your main responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Being the liaison to various stakeholders across the global Roche finance network (e.g. local Finance Enterprise Partners, Roche Shared Service Center, Finance Excellence Partner chapter) on several major finance processes (Month End Closing, Budget, Forecasting, Performance Tracking).
  • Leveraging technology and automation to drive digitalization as well as exploring and applying new technologies and tools in the fields of automation and Artificial Intelligence.
  • Ownership of reporting solutions (sales, P&L, Headcount, Balance sheet) and providing key insights to local commercial affiliate on major KPIs.
  • Identifying and implementing opportunities for efficiency gains, promoting continuous improvement through standardised processes and tools in order to be brilliant at the basics.
  • Collaborating closely with Finance Enterprise Partners and internal commercial stakeholders to enable increased customer experience and business impact along the customer journey.
  • Acting as an enabler for the adaption of the new ways of working by shifting from affiliate focused finance operations towards an above country-cluster level Your profile

  • Profound understanding and experience of key financial processes (Month End Closing, Annual Closing, Budgeting, Forecasting and Actual Performance Monitoring) and financial accounting principles.
