  • Mission
  • The mission consists in performing IT business analysis work for key compliance and risk management projects, in particular for subjects related to anti-money laundering (AML), clients risk scoring, balance sheet management, and Value At Risk (VaR),
  • The candidate needs to have an extensive regulatory IT banking background as well as a strong BA experience
  • Main responsibilities
  • Reporting to the heads of IT Compliance and IT Risk teams, the consultant will work on key compliance and risk projects
  • IT BA Responsibilities

o Analyze regulation, business scenarios/cases and contribute to business / regulatory requirements definition. o Define Functional Design Specification. o Manage vendor (application relationship (software package). o Execute database scripts and queries (SQL) to validate data integrity and ensure alignment with regulatory requirements. o Install and test new versions of applications and exploit the applications in production (batch error management). o Liaise with the business to provide guidance from an IT point of view. o Provide guidance for business analysts, developers, and testers assigned to the projects and ensure good project management practices are employed throughout the project lifecycle. o Identify key risks and issues and define mitigation scenarios.

  • Your Profile
  • Good operational experience in the implementation of various IT Regulatory projects (Anti-Money Laundering, Market Abuse, Client screening, Payment filtering, Client suitability, Automatic Exchange of Information, MIFID, EMIR, Risk Management, etc.
  • Proficient in SQL and database scripting for risk data analytics.
  • Good understanding of the bank’s business processes
  • Good practice of IT BA activities and knowledge of IT project management
  • 5 to 10 years of experience
  • Education : Master Degree in IT
  • French mandatory and English (C1)
  • Others
