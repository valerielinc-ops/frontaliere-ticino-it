Team Leadership and People Development

  • Lead and Coach : Provide direct line management, coaching, mentorship, development to a team of PHC Partners, fostering a culture of high performance, accountability, and entrepreneurial thinking.
  • Performance Management : Set clear performance objectives, conduct regular reviews, and manage the goals/sales performance of the team, ensuring goals/sales and profitability targets are met or exceeded.
  • Recruitment and Onboarding : Participate in the recruitment and development of talent II. Commercial Strategy Execution & Business Results
  • Strategic Planning : Translate the overall business strategy into actionable, time-bound business plans and sales/account strategies for your team and territory to drive adoption of high medical value assays and utilization of hospital point-of-care solutions. Closely collaborate with Diagnostics Value Team (Marketing) and other relevant teams to define and review product marketing strategies.
  • Sales/Account Strategy Execution : Oversee and drive the execution of the account strategies across the designated hospital accounts, drive cross-functional collaboration in particular with Customer Success Partners for Hospital and Lab, other field colleagues and external stakeholders to ensure cohesive execution and maximize account opportunities.
  • Medical Value Delivery: Ensure that the team's strategies deliver demonstrable medical value of high medical value assays to healthcare systems, clinicians, and patients, enabling optimal disease management to achieve better patient outcomes.
  • Account and Territory Management: Manage the overall territory plan and key accounts, allocate resources effectively.
  • Forecasting and Reporting: Ensure accurate sales forecasting, pipeline visibility, and timely reporting on business performance
  • Distributor Management: As distributor owner, collaborate with Strategic Planning, Business and Commercial Excellence Team to drive distributor strategies which enable sales growth III. Customer Experience Excellence
  • KOL (clinicians) Engagement: Working together with Diagnostic Value Team and Medical Team, maintain a high level of market intelligence and a close liaison with Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) and clinical experts in the industry to gather insights and drive changes in respective hospitals and the Taiwan healthcare ecosystem.
  • Customer Advocacy: C hampion customer needs internally, ensuring seamless collaboration with Diagnostics Value (Marketing), Customer Experience Team, Global Service Team (FMI APAC and Global teams) and other functions to improve, resolve issues and enhance the end-to-end customer journey.
