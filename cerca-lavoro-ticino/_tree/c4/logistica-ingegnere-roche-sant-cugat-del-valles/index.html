The Position The Logistics Engineer / Project Manager leads the technical evolution and modernization of our 10,000m2 self-operated distribution center. This role is the strategic architect responsible for transforming a complex, legacy-automated environment into a flexible, next-generation fulfillment hub using AMR and advanced robotics. You will bridge the gap between physical hardware and digital systems, managing the transition from a legacy software stack (WMS/WCS/MFC) to a modern SAP eWM environment. Reporting directly to the Head of Supply Chain, you will own the technical roadmap, from ROI analysis and vendor selection participation to on-site project execution lead, ensuring our automation strategy aligns with future business scalability. Your Main Responsibilities

  • Creation of holistic view of warehouse operations in terms of automation technology, system landscape and warehouse operator needs. Full understanding & documentation of current bottlenecks in terms of throughput and storage capacity. Create suitable measurements (KPIs) where needed in order to translate requirements and current capacities.
  • Automation Transformation & Strategy: Assess and lead the transition from rigid legacy automation to flexible technologies (AMRs, Humanoids, etc.) where required. Take full stock of existing hardware capabilities and map them against future business requirements and market-leading solutions.
  • Systems Architecture & Integration: Fully master the current system landscape (WMS, WCS, MFC). Design the integration strategy to link the physical world with the digital world, acting as the lead for the upcoming SAP eWM implementation.
  • Financial Stewardship & ROI: Drive rigorous ROI analysis for all automation projects collaborating with finance and site management. Have clear visibility for throughput, storage density, and FTE optimization.
  • Material Flow & Process Optimization: Analyze and redesign internal material flows to eliminate bottlenecks. Use data-driven insights to optimize picking strategies, replenishment cycles, and packing efficiency. Processes are redesigned considering sustainability, quality and safety aspects.
  • Project Governance: Act as the on-site Project Lead for major technical and system shifts. Manage the end-to-end lifecycle—from concept and vendor assessment to commissioning and structured handover—ensuring minimal disruption to active operations. Interact with global and local project teams.
  • Stakeholder & Vendor Management: Partner with site management to assess new vendors and technologies. Navigate the complex relationship between legacy hardware providers and modern robotics OEMs, ensuring system interoperability.
  • Technical Communication: Adapt complex technical concepts for different audiences (“zoom in & out”). Clearly articulate the "why" behind automation shifts to senior leadership while providing granular technical direction to on-site maintenance and IT teams.
