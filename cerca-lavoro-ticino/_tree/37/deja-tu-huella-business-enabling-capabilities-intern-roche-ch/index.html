The Position Deja tu Huella: Business Enabling & Capabilities - Intern

What do we offer?

To be part of a new cohort of the Roche-CEU San Pablo postgraduate degree as an expert in the Pharmaceutical Industry. 11 months of internships at Roche where you will participate in challenging projects and benefit from pioneering postgraduate training in the market about the pharmaceutical industry.

You will have the opportunity to get on-the-job training by working on key projects with your designated department from Monday to Thursday and attend in person postgraduate classes at the university on Friday.

Our main location is Madrid, we are open to applications from all over Spain but relocation will be needed as part of the program expectations. Office attendance is expected (with some flexibility) to enjoy the connection to our colleagues, network and get exposure.

Last but not least, you will have mentorship by a Roche tutor who will give you continuous feedback to develop your professional skills. The mentors for the program are Roche’s senior and experienced young talent development professionals.

Apply for this offer and follow us at @RocheES to share with your network a unique opportunity in the market to start your professional career in the pharmaceutical sector.

What are the main responsibilities of the Intern?

This internship is designed to help evolve learning beyond traditional training by contributing to the creation of a modern, digital, and scalable learning ecosystem that prepares Roche talent for the future of healthcare.

The intern will work closely with the Roche Academy Spain team and collaborate cross-functionally with different areas of the organization to help design impactful learning experiences, support capability development initiatives, and contribute to the evolution of Roche’s learning culture.

Main responsibilities include

Digital Learning Ecosystem & SkillUp

  • Supporting the organization and curation of learning content within Roche’s digital learning ecosystem, including SkillUp.
  • Contributing to the design of intuitive and engaging digital learning experiences using technology and AI as facilitators of employee growth.
  • Assisting in the optimization of content structures and learning journeys to improve the employee learning experience.
  • Supporting the exploration and implementation of Generative AI tools and digital learning solutions.

Learning Journeys & Capability Development

  • Collaborating in the creation of innovative learning journeys focused on future-relevant capabilities.
  • Supporting the adaptation of global learning resources and external EdTech trends into high-value local learning experiences.
  • Contributing to the alignment of learning initiatives with Roche’s global Commercialization Capabilities framework, especially for customer-facing teams.
  • Helping identify new methodologies and approaches to improve employee engagement and capability development.

Learning Analytics & Business Impact

  • Supporting the analysis of learning metrics, KPIs, and impact indicators to help measure the value of learning initiatives.
  • Assisting in the unification of learning processes under the Roche Academy Spain identity and philosophy.
  • Collaborating in the preparation of reports and presentations to communicate learning impact and business alignment.
  • Contributing to the development of a data-driven learning culture focused on continuous improvement.
