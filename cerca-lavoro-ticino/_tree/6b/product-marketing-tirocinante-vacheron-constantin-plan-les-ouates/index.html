Founded in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the world’s oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, faithfully perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timepieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

PRODUCT MARKETING INTERN

Plan-les-Ouates

6-month Internship starting 15.02.2027

With internship agreement ("convention de stage")

FOR THOSE WHO SEEK EXCELLENCE

Join one of the most respected Maisons in fine watchmaking where excellence is both a given and a never-ending quest. Play your part in driving our collective success.

YOUR ROLE

We are seeking a Product Marketing Intern to join our Products team and also support the Product Environment team. She/he will participate in the development and monitoring of our product range, from design to marketing. This internship will involve versatile and responsible missions, providing an insight into the role, objectives, and challenges of a Product department within a Haute Horlogerie Maison.

PLAYING YOUR PART

PRODUCT MARKETING (80%)

Daily Activities and Specific Studies (60%)

  • You will help build presentation materials in support of the Maison product offer
  • You will review, update and improve existing materials
  • You will realize studies related to a new product, a new market trend or any other specific topic
  • You will share the results with the Product Marketing team and propose action plan
  • You will support the Product Marketing Manager collaborating with the Innovation Department in the search for information related to specific topics

Competition and trends in luxury sectors (10%)

  • You will ensure active monitoring of competition and trends
  • You will be responsible for keeping competitors’ mappings updated

Watches & Wonders (10%)

  • You will define the department’s needs and watches allocation
  • You will help build presentation materials
  • You will support the team on the coordination and specific needs of the events

PRODUCT ENVIRONMENT (20%)

  • You will focus on competitive monitoring of the product environment
  • You will support the product brief
  • You will create and update market guidelines
  • You will ensure monitoring and proofreading on publishing projects

WHAT WILL MAKE YOU SUCCESSFUL?

Success in this role will require

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree with a strong interest in Luxury and Watchmaking
  • A first internship experience would be a plus
  • Solid communication, analytical and organizational skills
  • PowerPoint and Excels skills
  • Fluency in English and French
  • Rigor, curiosity, proactive attitude and teamwork

Vacheron Constantin celebrates 270 years as the world’s oldest watchmaker in continuous production since 1755. Headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, we operate a global retail network of nearly 200 points of sale across 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional global community of watchmakers, artisans and dedicated professionals share a common devotion to our exceptional heritage and a passion to trailblaze an even brighter future.

If this fires your imagination, we welcome your application.

È un ruolo a tempo pieno, e che tipo di contratto offre Vacheron Constantin?

Questo annuncio è una posizione a stage/tirocinio e tempo pieno. Il tipo di contratto mostrato qui arriva direttamente dall'annuncio originale del datore di lavoro; conferma sempre orario esatto, preavviso e durata del periodo di prova con Vacheron Constantin durante il colloquio, perché questi dettagli possono variare anche all'interno della stessa categoria contrattuale.

Serve un permesso da frontaliere per un ruolo nel canton Ginevra?

I cittadini UE/AELS residenti in uno Stato UE/AELS possono richiedere il Permesso G e lavorare in tutta la Svizzera: le zone di frontiera per loro non valgono più e il rientro è almeno settimanale. Il datore di lavoro svizzero presenta la domanda all'ufficio della migrazione del canton Ginevra dopo la firma del contratto. I tempi di lavorazione variano per canton — verifica i dettagli con l'ufficio della migrazione del canton Ginevra o con HR durante il colloquio.
