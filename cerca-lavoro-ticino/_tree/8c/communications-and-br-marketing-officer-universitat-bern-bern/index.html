Descrizione completa

UniBE is interconnected. With us, you're part of an international community and benefit from interdisciplinary collaboration.

Tasks, skill, responsibilities

  • Strengthen the WTI's visibility, reputation, and positioning within international academic, governmental, business, and policy communities and identify opportunities to strengthen the WTI's international visibility and outreach.
  • Coordinate the preparation and dissemination of institutional announcements, newsletters, and other communication materials.
  • Manage and update the WTI website, social media platforms, newsletters, and other communication channels.
  • Produce and coordinate institutional publications, including the WTI Annual Report, news articles, promotional materials for WTI's courses and programmes.
  • Develop and implement marketing strategies to promote the WTI's academic programmes, executive education courses, and other activities for international audiences.
  • Coordinate communication and promotional activities for conferences, workshops, seminars and events in collaboration with the Administration and IT Team.
  • Liaise with the WTI Alumni Association and support alumni engagement and outreach initiatives.
  • Support fundraising and resource mobilisation activities, including identifying potential donors, sponsors, and funding opportunities, and contributing to funding proposals and partnership development.
  • Maintain stakeholder databases, mailing lists, and communication networks.

Requirements

  • University degree in Communications, Marketing, International Relations, Law, Political Science, Economics, or a related field.
  • Excellent written and spoken English and German.
  • Professional experience in communications, public relations, marketing, or institutional outreach.
  • Experience managing websites, social media platforms, digital communications, and content management systems.
  • Excellent writing, editing, and presentation skills, with the ability to communicate complex academic and policy issues to diverse group of audiences.
  • Experience or interest in fundraising, grant writing, donor relations, sponsorship development, or partnership building.
  • Knowledge of graphic design, digital marketing, CRM systems, and communication analytics is an advantage.
  • Strong organisational and project management skills, initiative, and the ability to work independently as well as within a small international team.
  • An interest in international trade, investment, global economic governance, and sustainability is highly desirable.

Interdisciplinary collaboration Application and Contact Interested candidates should submit the following documents electronically as a single PDF: CV; motivation letter; copies of academic diplomas; relevant certificates; names and contact details of at least two professional references. About us

The World Trade Institute (WTI) at the University of Bern is a leading academic institution dedicated to studies, teaching and research in international trade and investment regulation, economic globalisation and sustainability. As a centre of excellence at the University of Bern with an international, interdisciplinary focus, we explore the interconnections between the fields of law, economics, and political science.
