Who are we?

A High Jewelry Maison with a strong heritage who promotes exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to hire talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that diverse perspectives foster creativity and knowledge to deliver excellence.

ARE YOU A GOOD MATCH?

You have 5/10 years of controlling experience.

You already worked in a supply chain/industrial environment.

You have strong analytical and modelling skills.

You have an agile mindset.

You have strong business partnering and communication skills.

Proactive, you are solution oriented.

You have advanced Excel and financial tools knowledge. Anaplan is a plus.

You are fluent in French and English.

WHAT ARE OUR EXPECTATIONS?

Reporting to the Senior Controlling Manager, you will serve as a strategic financial business partner to Supply Chain Director, driving inventory management, planning and optimization.

In this role, you will partner closely with supply chain teams to deliver accurate stock projections, enhance financial visibility, lead scenario modeling and empower data-driven strategic decision-making.

In this role, you will

· Led inventory forecasting & strategic planning. You will adapt and improve it by building strong cross-functional relationships with procurement, logistics, master data and manufacturing teams. · Provide critical financial analysis and strategic recommendations that optimize supply chain performance, working capital and cash flow. · Prepare, produce and analyze monthly reporting on supply chain activities to optimize stock performance: variances analyze, risks and opportunities, corrective actions recommendations… · Perform stock-related monthly financial closing tasks, including provisions calculation and booking, reconciliations, costing… · Adapt and implement finished goods financial controlling methodologies across the Indirect supply chain team · Enhance forecasting tools and ensure data reliability, integrity and usability. You will design and support deployment of data visualization dashboard and reports and improve financial processes.
