Descrizione completa

Mission

The Laboratory of Behavioral Genetics, led by Prof. Carmen Sandi at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Lausanne (EPFL), is seeking a talented and motivated postdoctoral researcher to investigate how mitochondrial quality-control mechanisms within specific neuronal populations influence brain circuit function and anxiety-related behavior.

The position is part of an international collaborative project that brings together experimental neuroscience and clinical research to investigate mitochondrial mechanisms of anxiety and their potential therapeutic modulation.

We are particularly interested in recruiting a researcher with strong expertise in molecular and circuit neuroscience, including viral-mediated genetic manipulations in rodents , who is enthusiastic about investigating the contribution of mitochondrial processes to brain function and behavior.

Research Project

The project builds on recent discoveries from our laboratory linking anxiety vulnerability to mitochondrial alterations, neuronal structural and synaptic changes, and the beneficial effects of interventions that modulate mitochondrial functions.

The successful candidate will lead experimental studies investigating the causal role of mitochondrial quality-control pathways in the nucleus accumbens, focusing on defined neuronal populations and their contribution to anxiety-related behavior.

A central component will involve cell-type-specific viral genetic manipulations , particularly targeting mitochondrial quality-control pathways. These experiments will be combined with behavioral phenotyping and cellular, molecular and functional analyses of mitochondrial and neuronal properties.

The project will also investigate the mechanisms underlying the behavioral and neuronal effects of a compound that modulates mitochondrial quality control, including direct measurements of mitophagy and mitochondrial function.

As part of the project’s translational objectives, the research will contribute to identifying relationships between mitochondrial mechanisms in the brain and biomarkers measurable in cerebrospinal fluid and blood, in collaboration with clinical researchers in Copenhagen.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, implement and validate viral-mediated genetic manipulations in rodent brain circuits, including cell-type-specific AAV approaches.
  • Conduct stereotaxic interventions and experiments investigating the causal contribution of mitochondrial quality-control pathways to neuronal function and anxiety-related behavior.
  • Design and perform behavioral studies using established rodent models of anxiety vulnerability.
  • Investigate mitochondrial mechanisms through molecular and imaging approaches, including analyses of mitophagy, mitochondrial quality-control signaling and bioenergetics.
  • Integrate mitochondrial findings with analyses of neuronal morphology and synaptic function, in collaboration with other laboratory members.
  • Contribute to translational investigations relating brain mitochondrial mechanisms to peripheral and cerebrospinal fluid biomarkers.
  • Independently plan experiments, analyze and interpret data, prepare scientific publications and present findings at international conferences.
  • Collaborate with other researchers and contribute to the supervision of junior laboratory members.

Qualifications

Essential

  • PhD in Neuroscience, Neurobiology, Molecular Biology or a closely related field.
  • Strong experimental background in molecular and/or circuit neuroscience.
  • Demonstrated hands-on experience with stereotaxic surgery and viral-mediated genetic manipulations in rodents, preferably including cell-type-specific approaches.
  • Experience with molecular and cellular neuroscience techniques, including imaging and quantitative analyses of brain tissue.
  • Strong scientific track record, analytical skills and demonstrated capacity for independent research.
  • Excellent communication skills in English and ability to work effectively in an interdisciplinary and international research environment.

Highly desirable

  • Knowledge of mitochondrial biology, particularly mitochondrial dynamics, bioenergetics, mitophagy or related quality-control pathways.
  • Experience in rodent behavioral neuroscience, particularly anxiety-, stress- or motivation-related paradigms.
  • Expertise in advanced microscopy, mitochondrial reporters or quantitative cellular imaging.
  • Experience with neuronal morphology, synaptic physiology or mitochondrial functional assays.

Prior specialization in mitochondrial biology is not required but would be considered an asset. Thus, we encourage applications from researchers with excellent expertise in in vivo genetic and neural circuit approaches who are interested in expanding their research into mitochondrial neuroscience.

What We Offer

The position provides an opportunity to conduct innovative mechanistic research at the intersection of molecular neuroscience, mitochondrial biology and behavior.

The successful candidate will join an internationally recognized laboratory with a strong track record in stress, anxiety and motivation research, and will benefit from:

  • Substantial scientific independence and responsibility within an ambitious international research project.
  • Access to advanced experimental facilities and complementary expertise in molecular, cellular, mitochondrial and behavioral neuroscience.
  • Close interaction with an interdisciplinary team and international collaborators working across experimental and clinical neuroscience.
  • Opportunities for scientific development, international visibility and publication of high-impact research.

EPFL provides a highly stimulating international research environment, with excellent scientific infrastructure and a collaborative neuroscience community.

Position Details

  • Location: Laboratory of Behavioral Genetics, EPFL, Lausanne, Switzerland.
  • Employment: Full-time (100%), fixed-term postdoctoral position.
  • Duration: Up to 3 years, renewed annually, according to EPFL employment regulations.
  • Starting date: Early 2027, with flexibility depending on the selected candidate's availability.
  • Salary and employment conditions: According to EPFL standards.

Application Process

Interested candidates should submit a single PDF document containing:

  • A cover letter describing their research interests, relevant technical expertise, major scientific achievements and motivation for joining the project, with a clear account of their specific personal contributions to previous research projects (not AI-written).
  • A detailed curriculum vitae, including a complete publication list.
  • Contact information for three professional references.

Applications should be sent by email to Prof. Carmen Sandi ( carmen.sandi@epfl.ch ) , with the subject line “Molecular/Circuit Postdoctoral Application.”

Applications will be reviewed starting December 10th 2026, and until the position is filled.

EPFL is committed to equal opportunity and diversity. We welcome applications from qualified candidates of all backgrounds.
