Store Manager Bilbao - Swatch Group

Store Manager Bilbao The company Swatch Group unites, among other companies, the following watch brands under its roof: Breguet, Harry Winston, Blancpain, Glashütte Original, Jaquet Droz, Omega, Longines, Rado, Union Glashütte, Tissot, Balmain, Certina, Mido, Hamilton, Swatch and Flik Flak. The Group has a strong distribution and multi-brand retail net with two sophisticated boutiques under the name of Tourbillon and Hour Passion. Swatch Group is also an important player in advanced nanomechanical, nanoelectronic and Bluetooth technologies essential to watchmaking and other industries Job description SWATCH has an exciting opportunity for a Store Manager to join one of our stores in Bilbao.

As a Store Manager your main tasks will be

  • Ability to build revenue, manage back-office operations and supervise selling professionals.
  • Develop the strategies and plans to achieve sales results through clientelling, superior customer service, events, partnering and traffic generating actions.
  • Ensure a consistent, quality store experience for customers.
  • Think strategically and drive the business to company standards.
  • Prioritize, plan, delegate, administrate and adapt to business needs.
  • Maximize inventory, cash control and loss prevention techniques.

Profile

The ideal candidate should be an experienced retail sales manager with a strong focus on boosting sales, providing refined customer service and demonstrating strong customer skills. Communicative person, results and customer service oriented and with attention to Retail. The experience within the watch industry and jewelry will be a plus. Professional requirements Flexibility is also needed to adapt the changes to Store needs. Availability for shift work. Intermediate level of informatics (Office).

Languages

Conversation level of English. Knowledge in other language would be a plus.
