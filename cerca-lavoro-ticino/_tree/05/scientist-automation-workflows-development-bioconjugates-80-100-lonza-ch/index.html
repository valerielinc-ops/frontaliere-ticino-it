The actual location of this job is in Visp, Switzerland. Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed. As Scientist Automation Workflows, Development Bioconjugates, your primary focus will be to establish laboratory automation workflows, collaborating closely with project teams to drive the integration of automation into our daily operations. In addition, you will be responsible for the planning and execution of laboratory experiments for bioconjugates process development. We are looking for you if you are passionate about automation, a great team player and motivated to bring bioconjugate-based biopharmaceuticals to the patient. What you will get:

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture
  • A highly collaborative environment that fosters teamwork and inclusion
  • A range of professional development programs to grow your skills and career
  • An ethical workplace where tasks are carried out in accordance to the relevant procedures
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous lifestyle, family, and leisure benefits in Visp: https://bit.ly/3wjkoFi What you will do:
  • Develop and implement automation workflows across various scales in bioconjugate development
  • Collaborate with project teams to translate project needs into automated solutions
  • Train and support laboratory technicians in the execution of automated workflows and ensure scientifically sound execution of experiments
  • Plan, perform and document laboratory experiments in collaboration with laboratory technicians
  • Evaluate data for the preparation of reports or presentations
  • Support project leaders in the planning and organization of projects and experiments What we are looking for:
  • BSc. or MSc. degree in chemistry, biotechnology or a related field
  • Completion of an apprenticeship as a laboratory technician is beneficial
  • Hands-on experience with automated lab equipment, such as liquid handlers and small-scale/g-scale automated systems
  • Good understanding of general laboratory routines
  • Experience in bioconjugation, protein handling, protein chromatography is a plus
  • Sense of accuracy, quality and proactive problem-solving
  • Reliable team player with excellent communication and collaboration skills.
  • Structured, focused and well-organized working attitude; open-minded for new ideas and suggestions; agile, highly motivated and dynamic; solution-oriented
