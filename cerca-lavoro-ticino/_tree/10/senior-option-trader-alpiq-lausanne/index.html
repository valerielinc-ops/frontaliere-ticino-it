Descrizione completa

Mission

Join us in shaping the future of our Trading Division as a Senior Option Trader, where you'll play a pivotal role in driving transformation and realizing our ambitious goals. We're seeking a dynamic individual to lead the enhancement of skills and capabilities within our asset trading perimeter, particularly in the domain of options trading.

Your main responsibilities

  • Drive the development and implementation of option trading strategies to effectively manage risk and optimize revenue from our diverse portfolio, including hydropower and gas-fired units, in close collaboration with our quantitative analytics, risk and trading teams.
  • Contribute to our trading transformation by establishing a consolidated and efficient framework for risk representation, management, asset optimization, and asset-backed trading.
  • Ensure the performance, risk tracking, and reporting of option trading strategies, utilizing benchmarking, backtesting, and stress testing to identify sensitivities and risks accurately.
  • Drive the development of coherent risk representation for our asset portfolio, supporting the creation of corresponding risk and valuation models.
  • Provide guidance, direction, and coaching to the asset trading team, facilitating the rollout of advanced option trading strategies and acting as a strategic value creator.
  • Execute and trade option trading strategies within our Asset Trading domain.

Your profile

  • A strong team player with a proactive and results-driven attitude.
  • Highly collaborative, with the ability to build trust and relationships in a complex and diverse environment.
  • Demonstrated expertise in trading commodities futures and real options within an asset-backed environment.
  • Minimum 7 years of experience in trading European Gas & Power markets.
  • Ability to provide expert thought leadership and drive improvements with minimal direction.
  • Solid understanding and hands-on experience in building and maintaining robust models and scalable infrastructure in the energy trading sector.
  • Master’s degree in Economics, Engineering, Physics, Mathematics, or similar field.
  • Proficiency in English; knowledge of additional languages is advantageous.
