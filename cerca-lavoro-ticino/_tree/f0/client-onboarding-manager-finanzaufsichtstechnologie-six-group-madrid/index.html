Client Onboarding Manager (Financial regulatory technology) Madrid | Working from home up to 40% | Reference 7965 Do you enjoy leading complex client onboardings where trust, clarity and execution matter?   • Understand client workflows, data requirements, operational constraints and regulatory expectations.

  • Identify recurring customer pain points and help turn them into product, process or tooling improvements. What You Bring
  • Strong client-facing experience with financial institutions, ideally in SaaS, RegTech, market data or financial technology.
  • Professional-level German and fluent English
  • Spanish or French are an advantage.
  • Ability to build trust with compliance officers, IT teams, operations specialists and senior stakeholders.
  • Structured project and onboarding management skills.
  • Calm authority, ownership mindset and excellent communication in complex situations.
