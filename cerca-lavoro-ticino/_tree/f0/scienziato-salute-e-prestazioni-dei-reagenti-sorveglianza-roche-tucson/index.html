The Position Scientist - Reagent Health and Performance/Surveillance Department Summary This is a role in Reagent Health and Performance/Surveillance in the Oncology Assay Development organization. Responsible for reagent product monitoring and risk based testing, including real time stability testing to support expiration dating of Ventana reagents. Work closely across Ventana functions, including: Life Cycle, Development, Manufacturing, Quality, Regulatory, and Global Organizations. Individual Contributor role: This individual will support commercialization of new products and post-launch monitoring of on-market products, risk evaluation of the on market portfolio, designing fit for purpose testing to assess product risks, drive failure investigations. This role is based in Tucson, AZ, on site . The Opportunity

  • Research & Innovation: Executes R&D activities to develop new products or processes, identifying technical challenges and creating potentially patentable components.
