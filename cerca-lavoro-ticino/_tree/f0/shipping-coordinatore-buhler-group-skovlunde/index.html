Descrizione completa

As a temporary maternity leave/Shipping Coordinator, you will become part of our team in Skovlunde, which currently consists of 3 employees. You will be responsible for coordinating all types of transport.
This position is a full-time role from November 1, 2026, until February 28, 2027. Aufgaben: • Booking and coordination of national and international courier, air, sea, and road transport • Daily contact with freight forwarders and colleagues at Bühler • Handling of spare parts orders • Preparation and checking of export documents, invoicing, and freight rates • Daily updating and maintenance of data in SAP (knowledge of SAP is not required) • Training in our procedures and work processes Anforderungen: You are a newly qualified Shipping Coordinator/Freight Forwarder or have experience from a similar position. In addition, we imagine that you: • Are structured, detail-oriented, and independent • Are helpful and contribute with a positive attitude • Can maintain an overview in a busy working day • Having good Danish and English skills, both spoken and written Two billion people eat food every day that was produced with Bühler equipment. One billion people drive vehicles whose parts were manufactured with our machines. Bühler aims to balance humanity, nature, and the economy in every decision as it develops solutions that unlock sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed, and mobility industries. We strive to create innovations for a better world, with a special focus on healthy, safe, and sustainable solutions. Therefore, we team up with customers, start-ups, multinationals, and academia to accelerate impact together.
