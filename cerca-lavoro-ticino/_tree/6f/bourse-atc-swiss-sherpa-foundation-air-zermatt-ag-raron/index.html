Scholarship ATC & Swiss Sherpa Foundation

Air Zermatt Training Center & Swiss Sherpa Foundation - Together for global rescue expertise

The scholarship is aimed at dedicated rescue professionals worldwide. It enables participation in specialized courses at the Air Zermatt Training Center - especially for people with limited access to training and financial resources.

Possible courses

  • AHEMS Crew Member Course
  • Mountain Emergency Medicine (MEM) Winter Course
  • Mountain Emergency Medicine (MEM) Summer Course

What is covered?

  • Travel costs & accommodation
  • Course fees

Who can apply?

The scholarship is aimed at rescue professionals worldwide, especially from regions with limited training opportunities and financial resources.

Applications are open to

  • Professionals with a medical background (paramedics, emergency physicians, nurses with emergency experience) → MEM courses
  • Specialists in technical rescue (mountain, water, high-altitude or desert rescue) → HEMS course
  • Members of local or international rescue organizations who actively provide or improve emergency care

Prerequisites

  • Proven professional experience (completed or ongoing) training in rescue services
  • Clear financial need
  • Goal: Improvement of rescue structures in the country of origin
  • Willingness to cooperate internationally
  • Good command of English

How to apply

  • Application documents:
  • Personal details
  • Letter of motivation (max. 1000 words)
  • Proof of education & experience
  • Justification and proof of financial need
  • Letter of reference

Deadline: October 31 each year

E-mail: training@air-zermatt.ch

Language: English

All information about the Swiss Sherpa Foundation can be found here: https://swiss-sherpa.org/
