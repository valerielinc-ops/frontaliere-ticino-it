About the role The Field Machining Supervisor is responsible for overseeing daily production or service center activities, ensuring that processes run smoothly, efficiently, and safely. This role supervises professional-level staff and ensures adherence to quality standards and production or service schedules. This job profile is intended for People Managers of 4+ professional-level roles. Key Responsibilities

  • Supervise production or service staff and assign daily tasks.
  • Monitor workflow and ensure production targets are met.
  • Ensure compliance with safety and quality standards.
  • Maintain equipment and report maintenance needs.
  • Train new employees and support skill development.
  • Identify process improvements and implement changes.
  • Communicate production status to management. Skills & Qualifications
  • Typically requires a technical degree and several years of relevant work experience to be able to manage and coordinate one or more professional-level teams.
