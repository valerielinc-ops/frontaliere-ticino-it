Descrizione completa

Are you passionate about the future of banking through digital transformation and digital assets? Do you see yourself playing an integral part in driving strategic projects and shaping the Group Digital Assets transformation globally? We are seeking an emerging leader to originate, shape, and drive the execution of institutional tokenization initiatives globally across the firm, in close partnership with Investment Bank (IB), Asset Management (AM), Global Wealth Management (GWM), and Personal & Corporate Banking (P&C). This role sits at the intersection of capital markets, regulation, and blockchain-enabled market infrastructure. It is designed for a “rising star” who can identify and translate strategic opportunities into executable, institutionally viable solutions by mobilizing stakeholders across a complex organization. This is not a standalone business development role and not a tokenization platform/product ownership role. Success depends on credibility, influence, conceptual thinking, problem solving skills, and the ability to deliver outcomes across organizational boundaries. Key Responsibilities

  • originate and shape institutional tokenization opportunities across IB, AM, GWM, and P&C, converting strategic priorities into concrete initiatives with clear divisional sponsorship and measurable outcomes
  • drive end-to-end execution of complex tokenization initiatives, including strategic approach, partner selection, delivery planning, and cross-functional coordination across Product, Legal, Compliance, Risk, Operations, and Distribution/Coverage
  • act as a central orchestrator across divisions, aligning stakeholders and maintaining momentum without direct authority, including managing competing priorities and ambiguous problem spaces
  • partner closely with internal and external Tokenization Platform Owner/teams (product & infrastructure ownership) to translate institutional requirements into scalable platform capabilities, while maintaining clear decision rights and separation between origination/structuring and platform ownership
  • plan and support negotiation of strategic partnerships with key ecosystem participants (e.g., custodians, infrastructure providers, tokenization agents, administrators, exchanges), ensuring commercial, operational, and regulatory viability
  • integrate regulatory, governance, and risk considerations into initiative design and execution, ensuring institutionally robust pathways to market across relevant jurisdictions
  • establish and improve repeatable execution patterns (governance, templates, workstreams, decision forums, milestones) to increase delivery velocity and transparency across initiatives
  • represent the firm credibly in key discussions with internal and external executive stakeholders as required by the initiative (e.g., institutional clients, strategic partners, industry forums), focused on delivery and scalable structures Additional US Responsibility
  • act as a key interface to regional management in the US, ensuring a coherent and executable Digital Assets strategy by aligning global priorities with regional market, regulatory, and client requirements What Success Looks Like (12–18 months)
  • a sponsor-backed portfolio of priority ecosystem initiatives across IB, AM, GWM, and P&C with clear governance, decision rights, and delivery milestones
  • progress 2–3 cross-divisional tokenization initiatives from concept through structured delivery planning to executed launch or a defined go-live pathway, depending on complexity and jurisdiction
  • a repeatable “institutional tokenization playbook” covering structuring patterns, partner archetypes, risk/compliance pathways, and execution routines
  • demonstrable stakeholders pull: divisions proactively bring opportunities because the role accelerates delivery rather than creating friction
