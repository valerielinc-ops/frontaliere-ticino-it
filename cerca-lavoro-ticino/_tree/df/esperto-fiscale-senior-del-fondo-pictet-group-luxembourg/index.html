Descrizione completa

Your team The Pictet Group is one of the world’s leading independent wealth and asset managers. Founded in 1805 and headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, the Group is represented at 30 offices in financial centres worldwide, and currently employs over 5200 people. Pictet has been present since 1989 in Luxembourg, where it employs over 700 people. FundPartner Solutions provides a full suite of fund administration and private label funds services. The global team of industry experts specialises in fund domiciliation, registration and governance, as well as risk compliance and investment management services. Operations provide secure and efficient processing of Pictet’s banking transactions and investment funds. Our teams ensure all activities follow regulations and manage risks. By developing and maintaining a modern, robust infrastructure, we guarantee reliable, high-quality services for Pictet wealth management, asset management and real estate clients. Your role

  • Lead and oversee all tax processes for investment funds, ensuring accuracy and compliance.
  • Monitor changes in tax legislation and evaluate their impact on existing processes, implementing necessary updates as required.
  • Act as a key liaison with tax authorities, auditors, clients, and legal advisors on tax matters and regulatory inquiries.
  • Proactively challenge and enhance processes for calculating and controlling tax figures to deliver high-quality service to clients.
  • Stay up to date with tax developments across various jurisdictions (tax watch) and actively build a professional network.
  • Manage tax-related risks, ensuring effective incident tracking and timely reporting to management.
  • Serve as the main point of contact for all tax-related inquiries regarding funds, supporting both internal stakeholders (CRM) and external clients.
  • Participate in client meetings and presentations as the subject matter expert on tax. Your profile
  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Economics, Finance or Engineering
  • 7 years' experience in similar function
  • Fluent in french and english
  • Economic, financial and accounting knowledge
  • Expertise in international tax environment
  • Excellent skills in tax reporting and calculation for funds
  • Comprehensive understanding of fund administration
  • MFGA (Accounting system) knowledge
  • Good knowledge of various financial instruments and their functioning
  • Proficiency in IT tools, particularly Excel and SQL queries
  • Client-focused with strong communication skills
  • Demonstrates rigor, methodological approach, high stress tolerance, initiative, and a high degree of independence
  • Priority management, self-organization
  • Analytical mindset, problem-solving skills, ability to propose solutions
