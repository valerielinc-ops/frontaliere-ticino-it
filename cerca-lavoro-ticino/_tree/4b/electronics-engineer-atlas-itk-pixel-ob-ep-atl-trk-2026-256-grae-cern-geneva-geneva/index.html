Descrizione completa

The ATLAS experiment constructs a new silicon tracking detector, the ATLAS Inner Tracker (ITk). In particular, the CERN team focuses on the development of the Pixel Outer Barrel. The responsibilities relate to the type-1 services as well as test equipment for quality control and electrical testing of detector structures and the final detector integration.

Your responsibilities

  • Type-1 power and data reception activities.
  • T1UT (custom test equipment) user support.
  • Quality control testing of custom cable assemblies for the detector, with a focus on type-1 data.
  • Type-1 environmental cable production testing and supervision, including commissioning after installation during detector construction.
  • Electronics testing and development support.
  • Software development support.

Your profile

  • Experience in the design of PCBs, their production and verification/debugging.
  • Experience in programming microcontrollers.
  • Hands-on experience in soldering and handling of electrical equipment.
  • Experience in writing technical reports, user documentation and manuals.

Skills

  • PCB design software, preferably Altium or KiCad.
  • Programming in C/C++ and Python.
  • Ability to operate multimeters, oscilloscopes and a VNA.
  • Demonstrating flexibility: adapting quickly and resourcefully to shifting priorities and requirements.
  • Achieving results: delivering high-quality work on time and fulfilling expectations; being rigorous and producing quality results at first attempt.
  • Working in a mixed team of engineers, technicians and physicists.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • By the application deadline, you have a maximum of two years of professional experience since graduation in Electronics Engineering (or a related field) and your highest educational qualification is either a Bachelor's or Master's degree .
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
  • Applicants without University degree are not eligible.
  • Applicants with a PhD are not eligible.
