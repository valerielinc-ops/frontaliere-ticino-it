Location: La Tour-de-Peilz, Switzerland Work model: Part time 80% Type of contract: Permanent contract

Be a force for good and grow through experiences that push you further

In this opportunity at Nestlé Suisse, your mission is to bring nutrition expertise into our products and consumer communication. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with marketing and brand teams, research and development experts, regulatory colleagues and our community of nutritionists. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and make a tangible contribution to nutrition in the Swiss market.

How you’ll make an impact as a Nutrition Specialist

  • Applying nutrition science to help develop and improve our products.
  • Assessing our portfolio against our nutrition standards and identifying opportunities to enhance its nutritional quality.
  • Benchmarking our products against local competitors to identify opportunities for differentiation and translate insights into practical recommendations.
  • Partnering with marketing teams to turn consumer, scientific and competitive insights into nutrition guidance that supports brand and category priorities.
  • Providing nutrition expertise to support products, claims and labels in meeting relevant nutrition requirements.
  • Translating nutrition science into clear, credible and engaging content for consumers across multiple channels, from brochures and newsletters to websites, social media campaigns and influencer collaborations.
  • Building nutrition knowledge internally through science-based training and communication.

What will make you successful

  • Having a background in Nutrition and Dietetics, along with relevant professional experience in the field.
  • Bringing strong nutrition expertise and a good understanding of Swiss consumers’ dietary habits and nutrition needs.
  • Using data analysis to assess product portfolios and benchmark competitors, with an openness to using digital tools and artificial intelligence.
  • Collaborating across functions and translating nutrition science into clear, compelling messages for non-expert audiences.
  • Communicating fluently in German and English
  • French is a strong asset.
  • Demonstrating strong presentation skills and the ability to advocate for nutrition across teams.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
