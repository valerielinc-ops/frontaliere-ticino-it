The Position As a ERP Business Analyst - Finance, you will independently manage end-to-end business analysis, tackling complex business problems with emerging ambiguity, strong ability to translate complex business-finance processes into functional specifications. You will demonstrate a full understanding of the business-finance domain, as well as integrate with other domains like commercial, supply chain and procurement, and related technologies, eco-systems and the interdependencies, applying advanced tools and techniques to translate requirements into strategic implementation plans. In this role, you will act as a trusted advisor to cross-functional teams, shaping stakeholder perspectives, handling process interdependencies, and significantly contributing to the design and validation of effective solutions. You will also mentor junior analysts and actively shape team processes, methodologies, and best practices within the broader Community of Practice. Description of the area Job Responsibilities

  • Scope / (Content Leadership): Independently manages end-to-end business analysis workstreams without guidance, tackling problems with emerging ambiguity.
