Descrizione completa

Introduction CERN is strengthening its philanthropic fundraising activities to support its strategic scientific ambitions and future projects. Through the CERN & Society Foundation, the Organization has already been developing relationships with private donors, foundations and corporate partners who share its commitment to advancing benefits of science and technology to society through education, innovation and cultural experience. Now CERN is seeking an experienced fundraising professional to develop and drive its major and principal giving activities. Working under the supervision of the Head of Fundraising & Partnerships and in close collaboration with the CERN Director-General and other senior representatives, you will shape fundraising approaches for selected strategic scientific activities and develop relationships with high-value donors and prospects. This is a unique opportunity to contribute to the development of strategic philanthropy within a leading international scientific organization. The role combines strategic thinking and high-level stakeholder engagement with hands-on responsibility for the full fundraising cycle, from prospect identification and cultivation to donor engagement, stewardship and follow-up. Functions

  • Develop and shape fundraising strategies for selected strategic scientific activities at CERN, identifying opportunities to secure significant philanthropic contributions, up to six-figure or larger philanthropic gifts, from individuals, foundations and corporate partners.
  • Support the CERN Director-General and other senior representatives in developing and managing high-level donor relationships, including identifying engagement opportunities and preparing briefings, proposals and tailored approaches.
  • Lead the full donor cultivation and stewardship process for selected major and principal gift prospects, from identification and research through qualification, engagement, solicitation and follow-up.
  • Build and maintain a portfolio of high-value philanthropic relationships, ensuring effective relationship management, appropriate documentation and continuity.
  • Conduct prospect research and lead due diligence, identify potential donors and assess their interests, motivations and capacity to support CERN's strategic activities.
  • Coordinate donor meetings, visits and other engagement activities, including preparing relevant materials, managing logistics, drafting correspondence and ensuring systematic follow-up.
  • Act as the operational counterpart for possible external fundraising counsel, coordinating CERN's contributions to campaign-readiness, feasibility and strategy development.
  • Capture and retain fundraising expertise, methodologies, tools and recommendations developed through possible external consultancy work, ensuring continuity and supporting future implementation.
  • Ensure that all fundraising activities comply with CERN's ethical principles, policies and standards.

Master's degree or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Fundraising, International Relations, Communications, Business Administration or a related field.

Experience

  • Significant professional experience in fundraising, with a strong focus on major gifts, strategic philanthropy or high-value donor development, in a scientific, academic, international or other complex mission-driven environment.
  • Demonstrated experience in managing donor relationships and driving the fundraising process from prospect identification and cultivation through solicitation and stewardship.
  • Experience engaging with high-net-worth individuals, foundations and/or corporate donors, including the development of significant philanthropic commitments.
  • Proven ability to develop fundraising approaches and strategies and translate them into concrete donor engagement plans.
  • Experience working closely with senior leaders and managing sensitive, high-level stakeholder relationships.
  • Experience in fundraising campaign development, feasibility studies or working with external fundraising consultants would be an asset.

Technical competencies

  • Major and/or principal gift fundraising.
  • Donor cultivation, solicitation and stewardship.
  • Prospect research and portfolio/pipeline management.
  • Senior stakeholder relationship management.
  • Fundraising CRM and donor data management.
  • Preparation of communication: donor briefings, proposals and engagement materials.

Behavioural competencies

  • Creating Vision and Strategic Partnerships: creating shared interests and promoting contact and collaboration with key external partners.
  • Communicating Effectively: delivering presentations in a structured and clear way; adjusting style and content to the audience; responding calmly and confidently to questions.
  • Achieving Results: having a structured and organised approach towards work; being able to set priorities and plan tasks with results in mind.
  • Demonstrating Accountability: working conscientiously and reliably; delivering on promises.
  • Demonstrating Flexibility: adapting quickly and resourcefully to shifting priorities and requirements.

Language skills

Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.
