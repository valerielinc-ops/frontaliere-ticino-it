Senior Deviation Specialist

Location: Visp, Switzerland

The actual location of this job is in Visp, Switzerland.

Join Lonza's Quality organization in Visp and play a critical role in ensuring the quality, compliance, and reliability of life-saving therapies. As a Senior Deviation Specialist, you will lead complex investigations, drive continuous improvement initiatives, and collaborate across functions to maintain the highest GMP standards.

This is a fully site-based role. Working together in person supports close, real-time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What you will get

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance.
  • A variety of benefits dependent on role and location.

The full list of our global benefits can be found here: https://www.lonza.com/careers/benefits

What you will do

  • Lead complex GMP deviation investigations, utilizing structured root cause analysis methodologies to identify underlying issues and implement sustainable solutions.
  • Partner closely with Manufacturing, Quality Control, Engineering, and other cross-functional stakeholders to gather data, evaluate findings, and drive effective CAPAs.
  • Author clear, scientifically sound, and compliant deviation reports in accordance with internal procedures and global regulatory expectations.
  • Monitor CAPA implementation and effectiveness, ensuring timely completion and reducing the risk of recurring deviations.
  • Act as a subject matter expert during internal and external audits, supporting inspection readiness and defending investigation outcomes.
  • Analyze deviation trends, identify improvement opportunities, and contribute to initiatives that strengthen process reliability and product quality.
  • Participate in risk assessments and change control activities to proactively prevent quality issues and support continuous improvement.

What we are looking for

  • Experience working within a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or medical device environment, including several years focused on quality systems, deviation management, quality assurance, or quality control.
  • Strong expertise in root cause analysis methodologies such as 5 Whys, Fishbone Diagrams, FMEA, or similar structured problem-solving approaches.
  • Ability to interpret data, assess risks, and make sound quality decisions in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.
  • Strong understanding of cGMP requirements and global regulatory expectations, including FDA, EMA, and ICH guidelines.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in German and English, with the ability to communicate complex technical information clearly.
  • Experience working with quality management systems such as TrackWise, Veeva, or comparable platforms.
  • Degree in a scientific or engineering discipline, or equivalent experience that provides a strong foundation in quality and compliance principles.
