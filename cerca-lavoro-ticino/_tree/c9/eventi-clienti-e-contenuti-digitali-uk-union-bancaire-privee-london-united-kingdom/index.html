Mission We are looking for a new Events & Marketing Manager to join the Communications & Marketing team in the UK. In this role, the individual will

  • Contribute to & implement UK marketing and events strategy to establish a distinctive trusted brand
  • Leverage off communication and engagement channels with clients, prospects and professional introducers to increase qualified referrals
  • Define content and marketing collateral for clients, prospects and business introducers to strengthen brand visibility
  • Plan and organise events for clients, prospects and professional introducers
  • Define event ROI tracking and follow up process
  • Generate marketing-led opportunities through co creation of content and events with external partners
  • Support AM in marketing initiatives and events Responsible for:
  • Implement UK marketing and business development strategy across digital and traditional channels aligned to business objectives and brand positioning.
  • Lead audience segmentation and value proposition development for clients, prospects, and professional introducers.
  • Establish a robust measurement framework with monthly and quarterly reporting.
  • Create marketing collateral (brochures, presentations, one-pagers, factsheets, pitch decks) with clear value propositions.
  • Plan and execute integrated campaigns and measure content performance
  • Partner with front office to qualify leads, lead routing, and SLAs; build lead-nurture workflows.
  • Oversee website optimisation (UX, SEO, conversion rate optimisation), landing pages, and marketing automation journeys.
  • Establish and manage communication and engagement programs for clients, prospects, and introducers (e.g., tailored newsletters, updates, co-marketing).
  • Set up feedback loops and engagement forums with introducers to identify content needs and joint opportunities.
  • Track and report on introducer pipeline influence, referral volumes, conversion rates, and revenue attribution.
  • Help build the annual events calendar with formats, themes, budgets, and target KPIs (registrations, attendance rate, pipeline influence).
  • Develop post-event follow-up and measure impact on pipeline.
  • Define and track KPIs across brand, engagement, acquisition, and revenue influence Core competencies

  • Be result driven and ability to develop impactful initiatives and develop KPI’s
  • Project management – The ability to articulate complex projects with different stakeholders; good experience to lead the planning, development, and execution of specific projects

  • Teamwork and Inclusion - Collaborates with other team members and contributes productively to the team's work and output. Flexible, dedicated, respectful, team-player
  • Work under pressure - Ability to juggle many projects and meet deadlines in a high-energy, fast-paced environment
