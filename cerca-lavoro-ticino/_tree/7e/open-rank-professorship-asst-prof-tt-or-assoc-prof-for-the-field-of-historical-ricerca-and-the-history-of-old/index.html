Descrizione completa

UniBE is prestigious. With us, you benefit from the excellent reputation of a long-standing institution all around the world.

Duties and objectives

Teaching responsibilities will focus in particular on the Old Catholic Theology degree program. This will take place within the context of and in collaboration with the Faculty of Theology and the other degree programs offered there. If German language skills must still be acquired, it is expected that teaching will also be offered in German two years after taking up the position. The duties of the professorship also include the acquisition of research funding along with the supervision of bachelor's theses, master's theses and dissertations. International networking and participation in academic self-governance are also part of the job profile.

##

  • Qualifications and requirements profile

For this position, we require an outstanding doctoral degree in the field of Old Catholic theology or a comparable doctoral degree with a historical profile. For an associate professorship, we also require a completed qualification, such as the habilitation or an equivalent qualification. We are looking for a committed individual who is willing to further develop their own teaching and research profile. We also welcome applicants with experience in university teaching or who have relevant qualifications. International reputation Application and Contact For your application, please send us a letter of motivation outlining your research profile to date and your interest in the position you are applying for. In addition, we require your current résumé with an overview of your academic background and previous work experience. Please also include a complete list of your publications and the courses you have taught to date, including the results of any evaluations. An important part of your application is a research proposal for the next five years, outlining your planned research activities. If available, you can include a brief outline of your postdoctoral qualification project. We would also ask you to submit a teaching concept that describes your didactic approaches and your ideas about teaching. If you have already obtained external funding, please provide details about this as well. Please send application documents, compiled in one PDF file, by January 24, 2027, to: University of Bern, Dean's Office at the Faculty of Theology, Länggassstrasse 51, CH-3012 Bern, Switzerland With us, you create value. For yourself and for society.

Who we are

An assistant tenure-track professorship or an associate professorship in the field of historical research and the history of Old Catholicism at the Faculty of Theology of the University of Bern is to be filled as of August 1, 2027, at the Institute of Old Catholic Theology. In collaboration with the professorship of Systematic Theology and Ecumenism, the advertised professorship is responsible for Old Catholic research and teaching at the Faculty of Theology in Bern. Once the qualification criteria have been met and after appropriate evaluations have been made, the tenure-track assistant professorship will be converted after six years at the latest.
