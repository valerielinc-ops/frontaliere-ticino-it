The Position P&C Technology Innovation & Solutions Engineer

The P&C Technology Innovation & Solutions Engineer will be a key member of the People & Culture (P&C) Engineering team within Roche Digital Technology (RDT) . Operating at the intersection of public cloud infrastructure and core HR SaaS platforms, the P&C Engineering team provides the technical enablers and hands-on validation required to realize P&C strategic objectives .

As the P&C Technology Innovation & Solutions Engineer, you will act as a hands-on technical partner to Product Delivery Leads (PDLs) and work alongside Solution Architects . In this role, you will evaluate technology roadmaps, assess feature feasibility, write serverless glue code, and build cloud-native prototypes and integration adapters for our P&C platform ecosystem .

The Opportunity

In this role, you will drive hands-on technical execution, rapid prototyping, and architecture validation across our HR technology landscape. You will:

  • Partner daily with Product Delivery Leads and Solution Architects to evaluate feature feasibility, latency, rate limits, and integration impacts across platforms like Workday, ServiceNow, and Cornerstone .
  • Engineer lightweight, serverless cloud glue code using services like AWS Lambda and API Gateway to connect core SaaS platforms with internal cloud environments .
  • Build rapid Proofs-of-Concept (PoCs) to validate integration feasibility and streamline real-world data flow across cloud ecosystems .
  • Lead deep-dive technical discussions with prospective vendors, inspecting API/SDK capabilities, payload schemas, and authentication models beyond high-level product demonstrations .
  • Drive technical Security Risk Assessments (SRAs) and detailed data-flow mappings for new tools and integration patterns in close coordination with Cybersecurity teams .
  • Analyze existing P&C system capabilities and cloud resources to eliminate redundant middleware, optimize runtime performance, and ensure full utilization of tools .
  • Test API payloads, webhooks, and modern authentication protocols in real-world cloud environments to ensure robust execution before squad deployment .
  • Translate complex cloud and API performance parameters into clear technical trade-offs to help business stakeholders and product leads make informed decisions .

Who you are

We are looking for an accountable, self-driven engineer who thrives on solving technical roadblocks and inspecting cloud capabilities . To excel in this role, you bring:

  • Experience: 4–6 years of hands-on experience in cloud engineering, integration engineering, or senior technical systems analysis within an enterprise setting .
  • Education & Certifications: A degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field, alongside active certification in a major public cloud platform (AWS certified like Solutions Architect Associate/Developer preferred, or Azure/GCP) .
  • Cloud Infrastructure Proficiency: Hands-on experience building and managing core serverless cloud services, including AWS Lambda, API Gateway, S3, IAM, EventBridge, CloudWatch, and Secrets Manager .
  • APIs & Integration Expertise: Deep understanding of RESTful APIs, GraphQL, webhooks, JSON/XML payloads, and modern authentication protocols like OAuth 2.0, OIDC, and SAML .
