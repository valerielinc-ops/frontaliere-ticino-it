A career at Lombard Odier means working for a renowned global wealth and asset manager, with a strong focus on sustainable investing. An innovative bank of choice for private and institutional clients, our independently owned Firm is one of the best-capitalised banking groups in the world, managing close to CHF 300 billion and operating from over 25 offices across 4 continents. With a history spanning over 230 years, Lombard Odier is an investment house providing a comprehensive offering of discretionary and advisory portfolio management, wealth services and custody. We also offer asset management services and investment strategies through Lombard Odier Investment Managers and provide advanced banking technology to other financial institutions. “Rethink Everything” is our philosophy – it is at the heart of everything we do. We have grown stronger through more than 40 financial crises by rethinking the world around us to provide a fresh investment perspective for our clients. Internship – Private Banking – 6 months (External Asset Manager team – Luxembourg) The main goal of the position is to support our EAM clients in Europe acting as one of the first points of contact and maintaining strong trusted relationships with all clients throughout the entire life cycle of their business relationship with Lombard Odier, which includes daily and periodic activity. Activities include account opening, periodic compliance reviews, support for client queries, execution services, payment processing and the administration of the relationship to comply with Lombard Odier policies and procedures.   • Manage to keep a low level of Loss (Quality risk report)
