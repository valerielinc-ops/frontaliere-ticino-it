Make an Impact at Tecan

Are you passionate about precision, quality, and hands-on work? Join our team as a Mechanical Assembler, where you will play a key role in building high-quality assemblies that support life science and healthcare innovation.

In this role, you will assemble mechanical components with accuracy and consistency while collaborating with cross-functional teams to meet production and quality goals. You will also gain exposure to continuous improvement initiatives and advanced manufacturing processes.

If you are detail-oriented, collaborative, and results-driven, we invite you to bring your expertise to our growing team.

How Will You Make an Impact?

  • Build mechanical assemblies with strict adherence to established processes and vPoke® principles
  • Ensure components are properly handled, inspected, and installed with precision
  • Cross-train to support multiple functions across the mechanical production floor and other departments
  • Partner with production supervisors and team members to meet output and quality targets
  • Follow all safety, quality, and company compliance standards
  • Support process improvement initiatives and team-based projects
  • Maintain accurate work practices in a fast-paced manufacturing environment

What You Bring

  • Basic mechanical assembly skills; prior experience preferred
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to follow instructions and procedures
  • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision
  • Effective verbal and written communication skills in English
  • Positive attitude and strong teamwork mindset
  • Flexibility to work extended hours based on business needs

Your Background

  • High school diploma or equivalent experience
  • Experience in mechanical assembly or use of basic hand tools
  • Experience working in a manufacturing or production environment
  • Familiarity with workplace safety standards

Nice to Have

Physical Requirements

  • Ability to stand, bend, and perform repetitive tasks throughout the shift
