About the role Job Summary The Group IT Team Lead is responsible for supervising a team of IT support professionals and ensuring the delivery of high-quality IT services across the group. This role coordinates day-to-day IT operations and supports the implementation of IT projects. This job profile is intended for People Managers of 4+ professional-level roles. Lead the Product Configuration & Pricing Technology subdomain as the single point of accountability for Sulzer's Select, Configure, Price, Quote (SCPQ) landscape. You will own the strategic roadmap, platform governance, and end-to-end service delivery for configuration and pricing technologies supporting our global Tendering and Product Management operations.
