Job Description Summary The Comparative Medicine department within Novartis’ BioMedical Research is looking for an experienced and highly motivated Animal Caretaker who can work flexibly across both Experimental Animal Care and Breeding.

The role includes professional care and husbandry of laboratory rodents as well as support for research activities, while ensuring compliance with applicable animal welfare legislation, quality standards, hygiene requirements, HSE regulations, and SOPs.

Job Description

Internal Job Title: Vivarium Operations Associate I Position Location : Basel, CH, onsite

This role is based in Basel, CH. Novartis is unable to offer relocation support for this role: please only apply if this location is accessible for you.

Role Responsibilities

  • Animal Care & Husbandry

• •

  • Daily care, husbandry, and health monitoring of laboratory animals
  • Monitoring of animal housing and cage systems
  • Cleaning and disinfection of animal housing areas, cage materials, and equipment
  • Documentation of relevant observations and unusual events
  • Compliance with applicable animal welfare, hygiene, HSE, and SOP requirements
  • Willingness to flexibly support either Breeding or Experimental Animal Care depending on operational needs
  • Experimental Animal Care
  • Supporting research groups in experimental animal care
  • Providing competent support to laboratory personnel regarding animal husbandry and the use of existing animal housing systems
  • Breeding
  • Care and management of breeding colonies
  • Performing daily breeding and animal health checks
  • Independently performing routine activities related to breeding and animal husbandry
  • Close collaboration with the responsible teams to ensure reliable management and care of breeding colonies

Essential Requirements

  • Completed vocational training as an Animal Caretaker or a comparable qualification or equivalent training
  • Practical experience in handling small animals and laboratory animals
  • Experience in the breeding and/or husbandry of laboratory animals
  • Willingness to work in an animal facility involving radioactive activities
  • English written/verbal communication skills (German a plus)
  • Reliable, accurate, and independent working style
  • Good teamwork and communication skills
  • High degree of flexibility and willingness to work across different functional areas
  • Willingness to work weekends and public holidays as part of a 365-day operation

Desirable Requirements

  • LTK-1 module is an advantage
