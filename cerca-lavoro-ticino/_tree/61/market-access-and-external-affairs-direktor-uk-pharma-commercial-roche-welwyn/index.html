The Position This is a pivotal leadership role within our UK Pharma Commercial team. You join at a moment of radical healthcare transformation — where market access, government affairs, and health economics converge to determine whether life-changing therapies reach the patients who need them. You lead a multidisciplinary function that shapes the UK's position as a priority destination for global pharmaceutical investment. The Opportunity:

  • Shape the future of UK pricing and reimbursement by leading the organisation's strategy across NICE, SMC, and AWMSG — ensuring our portfolio remains commercially viable and accessible to patients
  • Lead high-stakes negotiations for Commercial Access Agreements that protect and grow a billion-pound revenue stream through sustainable, innovative funding models
  • Influence government policy at the highest levels maintaining strategic relationships across the Department of Health and Social Care, HM Treasury, and the broader political landscape
  • Build a world-class Access engine that serves as the gold standard for our global headquarters — proving that the UK is a priority launch market
  • Drive health equity and system transformation by leading initiatives that improve patient outcomes, enhance population health, and reduce per-capita costs in partnership with the NHS
  • Pioneer innovative access models including outcome-based agreements, value-based pricing, and real-world evidence frameworks that can be exported globally Who you are:
  • You bring extensive senior leadership in market access, health economics, or government affairs within the pharmaceutical or life sciences sector
  • You have deep expertise in UK HTA processes and the commercial mechanics of industry-government pricing frameworks
  • You are a proven political advocate, comfortable engaging at ministerial and senior civil servant level to shape policy outcomes
  • You are an enterprise thinker who balances access decisions against commercial, medical, and supply chain considerations across a complex portfolio
  • You lead with purpose — translating strategic patience into tactical urgency to deliver patient access today while building frameworks for tomorrow Preferred:
  • Experience navigating the post-Brexit regulatory and trade landscape as it impacts pharmaceutical operations
  • Background in Real-World Evidence generation and its application to HTA submissions and lifecycle management
  • International or global headquarters experience demonstrating cross-market strategic influence If you're ready to shape the future of patient access in the UK, we'd like to hear from you.
