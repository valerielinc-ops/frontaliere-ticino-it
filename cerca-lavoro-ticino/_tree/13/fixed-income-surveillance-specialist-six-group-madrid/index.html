Fixed Income Surveillance Specialist Madrid | Working from home up to 40% | Reference 8058 BME is the operator of all securities markets and financial systems in our country and is part of the SIX Group. In Fixed Income surveillance team we are looking for a person to join the team to cover the daily basis. As a Fixed Income operation technician you will be learning how to manage our data bases and our trading platforms: AIAF, MARF and SENAF. You will work in a motivated, dynamic environment in which we are responsible for Fixed Income Market. What You Will Do

  • Registration and maintenance of issues in the data base
  • Surveillance of the Fixed income Markets (checking positions, trades, market abuse, doing EOD files & procedures)
  • Management of Members and system connections
  • Collaboration on the testing version updates
  • Monitorization and helpdesk support to Members, both national and international What You Bring
  • Bachelor's degree in Economics, Business Administration or any related field. Studies related with financial sector or Capital Markets will be a plus
  • A minimum of 2 years of experience in the financial sector and in an operational or back office area
  • Good written and oral level of English and Spanish, minimum a B2
  • Thorough, proactive and decisive person, with good teamwork skills
  • Attention to details and patience personality to avoid committing errors
  • Availability to work in weekly rotatory shifts (from 8:00h to 16:30h and from 9:30 to 18:00 h) If you have any questions, check out our FAQ page or call Sara Perez de la Cuesta at +34 91 709 56 80 . For this vacancy we only accept direct applications. Diversity is important to us. Therefore, we are looking to receiving applications regardless of any personal background.
