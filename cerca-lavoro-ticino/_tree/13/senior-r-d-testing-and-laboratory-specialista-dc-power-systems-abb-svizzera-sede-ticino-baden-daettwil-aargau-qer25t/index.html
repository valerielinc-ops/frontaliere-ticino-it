Your role and responsibilities

We are looking for a Senior R&D Testing & Laboratory Specialist to join our Direct Current Data Center Incubator, playing a critical role in driving the transformational shift toward next-generation Direct current data center architectures. Together with power engineering teams, production, software, and control experts, you will push technological boundaries and innovation.


In this role, you are responsible for translating approved product, system and compliance requirements into safe and executable test plans, test setups and procedures for low- and medium-voltage power converter systems of the ABB DC Architecture offering of the new ABB Infinitus Direct Current (DC) Portfolio for Datacenters. You coordinate and perform validation and qualification testing from converters to integrated converter systems and DC-bus assemblies, analyze results, and support cross-functional root-cause investigations.


The location for this position is Baden-Daettwil, Switzerland.

The work model for the role is: onsite


You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Lead the test activities for the Validation, Qualification and certification of the DC architecture components, sub-systems and full DC architecture system in R&D Labs test setups. This includes the definition, practical implementation and execution of test plans, procedures, acceptance criteria, instrumentation and measurement chains in collaboration with R&D engineers, testing and laboratory specialist and technicians, System Engineering and Certification teams.
  • Coordinate the R&D test areas and test setups implementation for the execution of functional, performance, type and qualification tests and test data and results documentation on converters and assigned integrated DC systems or sub-systems.
  • Responsible for the definition of the DC-System dedicated lab test processes and protocols, infrastructure, areas and equipment, with active interaction and collaboration with the different stakeholders in the different locations involved (CH, IT, US), including the Lab Infrastructure and the Health Safety and Environment (HSE) teams.
  • Contribution to the maintenance and further development of the key Low Voltage Direct Current Expertise and Know-How of these technologies in testing, validation and certification.



• Qualifications for the role

  • University, University of Applied Sciences bachelor's or master's degree, or Equivalent Experience in the field of electrical engineering with focus on Power Electronics testing, validation, qualification and certification.
  • Solid background experience and high interest in practical R&D experimentation, testing and validation of Low Voltage and High-Power converters and electrical systems. Expertise in solutions and components validation, productization and certification in LV and/or MV environment is desired.
  • Know-how and expertise in test systems, equipment and infrastructure related to the validation and qualification of power electronics converters and systems testing, including protocols for safety, quality, reliability, and certification.
  • Interest in applied research and development, self-organized, motivated person, that likes to work in a highly qualified research and development team composed of experts in different domains and global locations.
  • Fluency in written and spoken English. German is a plus.
  • Availability to travel sporadically.


More about us

ABB's Distribution Solutions Division facilitates the efficient and reliable distribution, protection and control of power by improving electric power quality while strengthening the resilience of the grid. The Division offers segment-specific products and solutions that largely serve utilities, industry and infrastructure segments, often providing the requisite medium-voltage link between high-voltage transmission systems and low-voltage users. With ABB Ability™ enabled connected solutions at its core, the offering includes medium-voltage air- and gas-insulated switchgear, indoor and outdoor circuit breakers, reclosers, fuses, contactors, relays, instrument transformers, sensors, motor control centers, as well as low-voltage switchgear for the ANSI standard markets.
