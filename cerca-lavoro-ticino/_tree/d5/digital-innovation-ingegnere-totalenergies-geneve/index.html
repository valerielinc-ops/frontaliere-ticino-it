Descrizione completa

Context & Environment: We have an exciting new opportunity to join Digital & Delivery IT for Trading’s in Geneva as a Digital & Innovation Engineer. As part of the Digital & Innovation team, the jobholder will work in close collaboration with business and IT. The job entails leading digital innovation, fostering team collaboration, and driving the development of automated solutions to enhance organizational efficiency. Digital & Innovation Engineer help accelerate the adoption of innovative digital solutions and contribute to the organization’s digital transformation while progressively developing technical expertise and autonomy in leading digital initiatives. Trading & Shipping (T&S) is the trading arm of TotalEnergies for crude oil, refined products, biofuels and paper derivatives. With around 1,000 employees, T&S operates from Geneva, Houston, Singapore, Dubai, Athens and Paris, ensuring the daily supply of refineries and distribution networks worldwide. In energy markets, T&S creates value through fair value assessment of flows, sourcing the best products for customers, and managing operational, financial, regulatory and HSE risks. As logistics and safety professionals, teams operate across the entire energy supply chain in fast-moving markets shaped by geopolitical and economic factors, requiring constant adaptation. Activities: In a business environment that is constantly evolving, Information Systems and Digital Technologies serve as the core engine of Trading & Shipping as well as Trading Gas & Power. They are central to, and inseparable from, business processes. The Digital & Innovation team is committed to driving digital awareness, conducting active technology monitoring, and applying the most relevant and advanced solutions to support the digital transformation of the trading activities, strongly sponsored by the Executive Committees (CODIRs). What you will do

  • Participate in identifying and analyzing digital opportunities , by understanding user needs, pain points, and improvement areas that can be addressed through technology.
  • Contribute to the prototyping, testing, and development of innovative solutions (AI applications, automations, data tools, low-code/no-code systems), using established innovation methodologies (e.g., design thinking, lean startup).
  • Collaborate with IT, data, and business teams to formalize functional requirements, draft basic specifications, and contribute to designing user-centric solutions.
  • Support technology watch and market monitoring, identifying emerging trends (AI, automation, analytics, cloud, etc.) and assessing their relevance for the organization.
  • Assist in digital project management, by helping track tasks, maintain documentation, perform user acceptance tests (UAT), and support communication with stakeholders.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement , by analyzing user feedback, measuring solution impact, and proposing enhancements.
  • Support change management activities, including the preparation of training materials, user guides, and facilitation of demonstration or onboarding sessions. We are committed to meet the energy needs of a growing population and we are committed to the energy transition. The volatility of the energy trading markets requires excellence in risk management and a culture of innovation. We are fully embracing the digital revolution. Our people flourish in an environment that promotes expertise, entrepreneurship spirit, agility and a purpose-driven culture, generating boundless opportunities to learn, grow and achieve collaborative success. Reinforcing the competencies of our people is a key pillar of our culture: we train our people to ensure their development and we pledge to encourage safety in all our actions. We look for passion, ambition and open-mindedness. While we evolve in a demanding industry that requests to be always on the edge, we cultivate a friendly workplace where our people feel good, where team spirit and respect guide our daily routine, where the diversity of our people and their skills create a nourishing experience for all of us. Candidate Profile:
  • You hold a Master's degree (Bac +5) in Information Systems, Digital Technologies, Business Information Systems, or a related field.
  • You have practical experience working with Information Systems and have participated in digital, transformation, or innovation projects.
  • You have hands-on technical skills in AI and agentic solutions (LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, MCP), including agent orchestration through agentic clients (e.g., Copilot Studio, Claude) or agentic platforms (e.g., Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Databricks Agent Bricks, Mistral), with the ability to build and deploy them using Python and MCPs.
  • You have knowledge of one or more corporate functions such as Finance, Legal, Compliance, or Human Resources.
  • You can analyze business needs, identify digital solutions, and contribute to the delivery of business value.
  • You can communicate clearly with users and stakeholders in both technical and non-technical contexts.
  • You demonstrate strong organizational skills and can manage tasks in a structured and reliable manner.
  • You can investigate issues, analyze information, and propose solutions to business or operational challenges.
  • You have experience working with cross-functional teams in a changing or international environment.
  • You have an interest in emerging technologies, digital tools, and innovation practices.
  • You have experience contributing to continuous improvement, process optimization, or digital adoption initiatives.
  • You are fluent in written and spoken English.
