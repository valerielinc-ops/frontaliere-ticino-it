Join the Antigen and Therapeutic Protein Production Unit within the Biologics Research Center as a Research Scientist in our Production Platform group. During this six-month temporary assignment, you will apply your downstream processing and protein characterization expertise to purify antibody-like molecules, therapeutic proteins and research tool proteins. Working in a collaborative, laboratory-based environment, you will use state-of-the-art purification systems, analytical methods and data-driven approaches to deliver high-quality materials, improve laboratory processes and support innovative scientific research.

Job Description Key Responsibilities

  • Purify recombinant proteins, antibody-like molecules and therapeutic proteins to support innovative biotherapeutic discovery and development.
  • Operate advanced ÄKTA chromatography systems across automated, high-throughput and preparative purification scales.
  • Apply established biochemical and biotechnological techniques to deliver high-quality purified proteins for scientific research.
  • Characterize proteins using analytical techniques to assess purity, quality and suitability for downstream applications.
  • Independently plan, execute and troubleshoot experiments while maintaining accuracy, quality and reproducibility.
  • Analyze data, maintain accurate electronic laboratory records and present findings to cross-functional research teams.

Essential Requirements

  • Completed laboratory technician training, a bachelor’s or master’s degree in biology, biochemistry or related discipline, or equivalent experience.
  • Hands-on experience with affinity, size-exclusion, ion-exchange, hydrophobic interaction and reverse-phase chromatography.
  • Knowledge of high-performance liquid chromatography, ultra-performance liquid chromatography, light scattering and mass spectrometry.
  • Experience operating protein purification systems and independent planning, executing and troubleshooting laboratory experiments.
  • Strong organizational and analytical skills, including accurate documentation, data interpretation and timely scientific reporting.
  • Fluent English and effective collaboration skills within an international, multicultural research environment.

Desirable Requirements

  • Experience developing and optimizing protein purification methods and implementing technologies that improve efficiency and protein quality.
  • Familiarity with automated, high-throughput and large-scale protein purification workflows using ÄKTA chromatography systems and/or automated liquid handling systems
