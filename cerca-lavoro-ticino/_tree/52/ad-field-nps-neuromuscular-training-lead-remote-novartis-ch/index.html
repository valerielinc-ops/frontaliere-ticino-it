Develop and deliver New Hire Training and priority ongoing training, including National Meetings and Launch Training, for NPS Field Associates [Associate Directors of Access and Reimbursement (ADARs) and/or Access Reimbursement Managers (ARMs)] in Novartis Patient Support, Neuroscience. Create and implement training plans for assigned products and launches, collaborating with internal teams, agencies, and subject matter experts to produce interactive, effective, and engaging learning solutions. Job Description Major Accountabilities

  • Act as Lead Trainer by developing and implementing ADAR and/or ARM capabilities curriculum for assigned product launches, New Hire Training, & large-scale national meetings.
  • Develop training programs, design curriculum, and provide education to enhance skillsets across NPS Neuroscience Field Teams.
  • Produce and facilitate training workshops that address brand/TA specific needs, changing market conditions, and legal/or regulatory requirements.
  • Establish learning environments that foster adult education and knowledge retention through dynamic facilitation techniques and innovative technologies.
  • Work collaboratively with HQ Training Leadership, Field Leadership, external vendors, SMEs, Skills Trainers, and essential BU functions to create and execute plans. Integrate cross-functional feedback to ensure strategies, tactics, and investments are well-coordinated and aligned.
  • Act as an NPS Neuroscience representative in collaborative training development and delivery efforts.
  • Ensure curriculum supports learning objectives and is consistent with expectations.
  • Act as an NPS Skills & Capabilities Training Team partner, supporting NPS initiatives and programs as assigned.
  • Ensure all materials are approved and compliant, consistent with Novartis policies, MAP/FUSE/LEGAL approved.
  • Identify and execute sustainability opportunities for each learning program and leverage the pre and post training metrics to measure impact on business outcomes.
