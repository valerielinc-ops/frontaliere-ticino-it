As an R&D Scientist in our NGS Library Preparation and Target Enrichment team, you will be a key player in executing sample preparation workflows . You will work within a dynamic environment dedicated to advancing molecular biology research and contributing to innovative development efforts . This is an opportunity to thrive in a collaborative team that values technical excellence and scientific curiosity . The Opportunity

  • Execute NGS library preparation from diverse sample types using various construction methods .
  • Perform DNA and RNA extraction from biological samples, optimizing protocols for high yield and purity .
  • Quantify nucleic acids accurately using instruments like Qubit and NanoDrop .
  • Operate and troubleshoot sequencing platforms for routine laboratory runs .
  • Conduct quality control for nucleic acids and libraries utilizing Agilent TapeStation .
  • Maintain precise experimental records in laboratory notebooks and digital formats .
  • Analyze experimental data to contribute to technical reports and team presentations .
  • Collaborate with cross-functional team members on experimental design and troubleshooting . Who you are
  • You hold a Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Molecular Biology, Genetics, Biochemistry, or a related field .
  • You possess 2-4 years of relevant laboratory experience, ideally within an industry setting .
  • You have hands-on experience with NGS library preparation protocols such as DNA-seq or RNA-seq .
  • You are proficient in PCR, nucleic acid extraction, and standard molecular biology techniques .
  • You demonstrate strong critical thinking skills and meticulous attention to detail in a fast-paced environment . Preferred
  • Experience with advanced DNA QC instruments like Tapestation and Nanodrop .
  • Strong interpersonal skills with a proven ability to work independently or as part of a collaborative team .
