Your Purpose

Explora Journeys is a vibrant, cosmopolitan, European luxury brand, imagined in the heart of Swiss hospitality in Geneva. Leveraging our parent company MSC Group’s hundreds of years of maritime expertise, our fleet of six luxury ships (three currently in the pipeline) are being built and designed in a totally different and unique way that will transform and redefine the cruise experience, creating a category of its own, appealing to the next generation of luxury travellers.

We are seeking an Ocean Wellness Operations & Personnel Coordinator to support our growing luxury cruise brand. This role will coordinate onboard workforce planning, recruitment, and personnel operations, while supporting supplier relationships, operational performance, and collaboration between Geneva headquarters and onboard teams.

Your Impact

Onboard Personnel & Workforce Coordination

  • Coordinate workforce planning and personnel allocation across the fleet, ensuring alignment with Ocean Wellness operational requirements.
  • Support recruitment and selection processes for onboard Ocean Wellness personnel, including job description reviews, interviews, and candidate coordination.
  • Coordinate employee onboarding, orientation, and operational readiness in collaboration with shoreside HR and onboard management.
  • Oversee employee lifecycle processes, including staff rotations, personnel documentation, training follow-up, and performance appraisals.
  • Monitor staffing requirements and personnel-related KPIs to support operational efficiency and workforce optimization.
  • Act as a key point of contact between Geneva headquarters, shoreside HR, and onboard Ocean Wellness teams, ensuring effective communication and operational continuity.

Ocean Wellness Operations & Supplier Coordination

  • Coordinate daily operational activities between Geneva headquarters, onboard Ocean Wellness teams, and relevant internal departments.
  • Manage relationships and operational follow-up with international suppliers, particularly Swiss, Turkish, French, and Italian partners.
  • Conduct supplier benchmarking and market research to identify opportunities for brand and product development, operational improvements, and cost optimization.
  • Monitor operational KPIs, product performance, and resource allocation to support data-driven decision-making.
  • Support operational planning, product availability, and replenishment across the fleet.
  • Maintain operational reporting and documentation through internal systems, including Power BI, CrunchTime, and Otalio

Your Journey so far

  • Master's degree in Management, Business Administration, or equivalent, with a strong foundation in strategic management, organizational processes, and operational optimization.
  • Previous experience within an international luxury cruise company or comparable luxury hospitality environment, preferably in a corporate headquarters setting.
  • Practical experience in HR administration, including employee onboarding, recruitment support, personnel documentation, and employee lifecycle coordination.

Your Essentials

  • Understanding of international supply chain operations, supplier coordination, procurement, and inventory management.
  • Familiarity with international corporate structures, cross-functional collaboration, and luxury hospitality standards.
  • Fluency in English is a must. Fluency in French/Turkish and or Italian/Turkish is preferred to support international operational coordination and communication with onboard teams and suppliers.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint, with experience using corporate information systems and reporting tools.
  • Familiarity with SAP SuccessFactors, Power BI, CrunchTime and Otalio.
