Who are we?

A High Jewelry Maison with a strong heritage who promotes exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to hire talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that diverse perspectives foster creativity and knowledge to deliver excellence.

ARE YOU A GOOD MATCH?

You have 5/10 years of proven experience in leading cross-functional projects.

You have a deep understanding of boutique operations and retail regulatory framework

  • An experience in compliance/internal audit & control is a plus.

You have a strong interest in the luxury sector and boutiques operations & process.

You have excellent communication and presentation skills.

You manage multiples priorities in a fast-paced environment with proactiveness.

You have an agile mindset.

You challenge your environment to find the best recommendations.

You are fluent in French and English.

WHAT ARE OUR EXPECTATIONS?

Reporting to the Retail Compliance Manager, your role is to build the compliance framework of the boutiques, through streamlined and actionable procedures. Within this role, you will ensure that our retail teams transact in a compliant and efficient way, following the Group and Maison rules and guidelines.

In this role, you will

· Build a unified compliance framework for boutique operations at a global and local level. · Draft, maintain and update clear actionable compliance procedures on internal tools, ensuring alignment across regional counterparts and full coverage of boutique use cases. · Define compliance speeches and adapt it to the Maisons’s tone of voice · Co-develop training materials with the Maison’s training teams to empower boutique staff and ensure smooth adoption. · Partner with the Retail Performance team to define compliance KPIs, set up data tracking and lead ongoing monitoring of boutique compliance performance.

MORE THAN A JOB...We recruit for a career!

By joining Van Cleef & Arpels you will be part of a Maison where employee career development is at the heart of our ambition.

Upon your arrival, you will be immersed into our enchanting world with trainings on our history, our know-how and our creations.

THE RECRUITMENT PROCESS

Otherwise, you will receive an email to inform you that your application has not been successful.
