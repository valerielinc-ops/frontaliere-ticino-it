Descrizione completa

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role Recruitment-focused HR role managing hiring, onboarding, data and leader support while building strong relationships across the business. Looking to take ownership of recruitment while still building your broader HR skillset? This is your opportunity to step into a hands-on, high-impact role supporting a global business in a growing and essential industry. We’re on the lookout for a driven HR Advisor to lead end-to-end recruitment whilst also supporting key HR processes that keep our business running smoothly. What you’ll be doing This is a recruitment-heavy role with real ownership and variety. You’ll partner closely with leaders across the business to attract, hire and onboard top talent. Key focus areas include:

  • Leading end-to-end recruitment across a range of roles (with a strong focus on skilled trades)
  • Managing onboarding and candidate experience from offer to day one throughout probation
  • Supporting immigration and visa processes for temporary and permanent employees
  • Maintaining accurate employee data and workflows in Workday
  • Supporting leaders with performance processes (probation, annual reviews)
  • Providing generalist HR support across employee lifecycle activities
  • Contributing to HR reporting and projects as needed You’ll also stay connected with our Service Centres across Australia, building strong relationships and ensuring HR is visible, practical and value-adding. What we’re looking for You’re someone who enjoys being in the detail but also thrives on building relationships and influencing outcomes.
  • 2–3 years’ experience in HR and/or recruitment
  • Strong experience managing end-to-end recruitment processes
  • Confident communicator with the ability to engage stakeholders at all levels
  • Highly organised with strong time management skills
  • Comfortable working across systems (experience with Workday highly regarded)
  • A proactive, solutions-focused mindset What we offer you
  • Performance-based bonus that genuinely rewards impact
  • Company phone & laptop
  • Employee Assistance Program to support your wellbeing
  • Global organisation with strong career development opportunities
  • 12 month contract role with an intension to move to perm Sulzer is an equal opportunity employer. We believe in the strength of a diverse workforce and are committed to offering an inclusive work environment.
