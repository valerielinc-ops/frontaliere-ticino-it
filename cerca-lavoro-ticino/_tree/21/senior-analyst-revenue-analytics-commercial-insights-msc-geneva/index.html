Your Purpose

Explora Journeys is a vibrant, cosmopolitan, European luxury brand, imagined in the heart of Swiss hospitality and luxury in Geneva. Leveraging our parent company MSC Group`s hundreds of years of maritime expertise, our fleet of luxury ships have been designed and built in a totally different and unique way that transforms and redefines the luxury cruise experience, creating a category of its own, appealing to the next generation of luxury travelers.

The Senior Analyst, Revenue Analytics & Commercial Insights will serve as the primary analytics partner supporting commercial decision-making for Explora Journeys, with a particular focus on North America. This role is responsible for developing reporting and forecasting capabilities, setting and evaluating market and channel targets, identifying revenue opportunities, and delivering actionable insights that influence sales, marketing, and revenue management strategies. Working within the HQ Revenue Management team, the role will help translate proven North America practices into scalable approaches that can be adapted to the specific dynamics, maturity and commercial characteristics of other markets. The role will therefore act as a bridge between North America and HQ, combining dedicated market support with the broader objective of strengthening commercial analytics and decision-making capabilities across the organization. The role reports to the Vice President, Revenue Management based in Geneva and maintains a strong partnership and dotted-line relationship with the Vice President, Commercial Strategy & Management based in the United States.

Your Impact

  • Lead the development and continuous evolution of North America commercial performance reporting, dashboards and KPI frameworks, ensuring alignment with HQ Revenue Management standards and the company’s Single Source of Truth.
  • Develop forecasting and planning models used to establish, monitor, and evaluate market, channel, booking, and arrival targets. Provide recommendations to leadership regarding performance gaps and opportunities.
  • Perform advanced diagnostic and root-cause analysis to identify performance drivers, emerging trends, risks, and revenue opportunities across markets, channels, products, and customer segments.
  • Identify, document and codify successful North America analytical approaches and commercial practices, translating proven solutions into repeatable frameworks that can be shared across the wider organization.
  • Partner with HQ Revenue Management and relevant market stakeholders to assess how North America best practices can be adapted to the specific commercial dynamics, maturity and customer characteristics of other markets.
  • Monitor competitor pricing, promotional activity, deployment strategies, and market developments to identify risks, opportunities, and implications for commercial strategy.
  • Partner with Revenue Management leadership to evaluate pricing actions, promotional strategies, inventory deployment, and commercial initiatives through data-driven analysis and forecasting.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Sales, Marketing, Revenue Management, and Commercial Strategy, to ensure alignment on business objectives and priorities.
  • Develop executive-level business reviews and performance updates that clearly communicate trends, risks, opportunities, and recommended actions to senior leadership
  • Identify and implement automation opportunities that improve reporting efficiency, data quality, and speed-to-insight

Your Journey so far

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, Economics, or a related field. MBA preferred
  • 8+ years of experience in revenue management, commercial strategy, pricing, business intelligence, analytics, consulting, or related commercial disciplines within hospitality, travel, transportation, luxury consumer, or adjacent industries.

Your Essentials

  • Expert-level Excel skills required, including advanced modeling, scenario analysis, and automation capabilities
  • Advanced experience with business intelligence and data platforms such as Power BI, Tableau, Databricks, Dataiku, SQL, Python, or similar analytics tools
  • Demonstrated success automating reporting, analytical workflows, and recurring business processes through scalable data and business intelligence solutions
  • Strong analytical and critical-thinking skills, with the ability to identify meaningful relationships, trends, and business implications across multiple data sources
  • Highly self-directed with the ability to independently prioritize work, manage ambiguity, and deliver high-quality outputs with limited oversight
  • Proven ability to act as a trusted thought partner to commercial leaders by translating business questions into analytical frameworks and actionable recommendations
  • Demonstrated strong ability to work with the US market, including understanding of the culture and US consumer mindset
  • Solid understanding of revenue management practices in the North American luxury cruise industry, including demand forecasting, booking curves, pricing, promotional effectiveness, inventory management, channel mix and market performance analysis
  • Ability to quickly develop a thorough understanding of commercial policies, pricing principles, promotional framework and distribution strategy, and apply them consistently in analysis and recommendations.
  • Willingness to work US hours on occasion
  • Ability to communicate complex analyses and recommendations in a concise, executive-ready manner
  • Proven ability to synthesize complex data into actionable insights and strategic recommendations that drive commercial decision-making
  • Solid communication skills, both verbal and written, with the ability to present recommendations
  • Detail-oriented with excellent organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously.
  • Able to work in Geneva, Switzerland office 5-days per week
