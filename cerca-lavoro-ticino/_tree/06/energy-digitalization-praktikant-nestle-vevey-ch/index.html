You will help turning monitoring data into clear insights that unlock actionable energy and financial savings. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with your line manager and with factory and engineering stakeholders across sites using the monitoring ecosystem. This is your opportunity to make an impact while gaining hands on experience and mentorship to kick-start your career. How you wi­ll make an impact as an Energy Digitalization Intern

  • Support monitoring dashboards and insight views in short cycles sites to act quickly on what matters most
  • Integrating factory data into cloud monitoring solutions and strengthening data quality to build trust in the numbers
  • Support the prototyping of Python-based analytics and data pipelines to automate detection logic (anomalies, baselines, opportunity sizing) that scales across factories
  • Advancing new tools (such as AI) for robust energy data capture, modelling, and simulations to speed up engineering decisions for decarbonization projects
  • Maintaining a clear single source of truth for energy KPIs, tool updates, and savings tracking so teams can execute consistently What will make you successful
