Descrizione completa

Workload: 80–100%

Shape the future of Axpo’s SAP ecosystem as a strategic technical and operational leader. This high-impact role combines deep technical expertise with exceptional project management skills, focusing equally on transformational projects and operational excellence.

What you will do

Own the infrastructure topics for SAP RISE-managed and internally-hosted SAP systems.Act as the central technical contact for network and cloud connectivity, including SAP satellite systems, firewall management, certificate administration, and DNS configuration.Lead cross-functional infrastructure projects (e.g., migrations, integrations, system upgrades, cloud transitions).Manage incidents, monitor system health, and ensure security and performance across SAP infrastructure layers.Collaborate with the SAP Basis team to design and deliver infrastructure solutions.Build effective relationships with Network, Cloud, Security, Identity, and Workplace teams to align technical solutions with business needs.Drive automation initiatives and operational efficiency improvements.

What you bring & who you are

7+ years of hands-on experience in infrastructure engineering, systems administration, or technical project management.Expertise in managing on-premises and cloud infrastructure (e.g., Azure or equivalent hyperscalers).Strong understanding of SAP systems operations, including SAP RISE and Basis administration.Proficiency in scripting and infrastructure automation (e.g., PowerShell, shell scripting).Experience with ITSM frameworks (e.g., ServiceNow, Jira) and managing infrastructure in regulated industries.Fluent in German and English (written and spoken).Valid Swiss PSP certification or willingness to obtain.

Even if you don’t meet all the criteria, we encourage you to apply—we value diverse perspectives and skills.

About the team: You’ll work with a collaborative, cross-functional team that thrives on innovation and operational excellence. We value open communication, inclusion, and shared success. Together, we ensure Axpo’s SAP systems operate securely, reliably, and cost-effectively.
