Sales Coordinator - Swatch Group

Sales Coordinator The company Swatch Group unites, among other companies, the following watch brands under its roof: Breguet, Harry Winston, Blancpain, Glashütte Original, Jaquet Droz, Omega, Longines, Rado, Union Glashütte, Tissot, Balmain, Certina, Mido, Hamilton, Swatch and Flik Flak. The Group has a strong distribution and multi-brand retail net with two sophisticated boutiques under the name of Tourbillon and Hour Passion. Swatch Group is also an important player in advanced nanomechanical, nanoelectronic and Bluetooth technologies essential to watchmaking and other industries Job description We’re seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Sales Coordinator to join our team. As a Sales Coordinator, you’ll provide support to Brand Manager to ensure that all parts of the sales process run smoothly. Sales Coordinator supports Brand Manager by preparing reports to help achieve sales targets, managing administrative tasks, analysing sales data, generating reports and maintaining accurate client records.
