Be a force for good and transform your potential into something extraordinary

As a Hot Kitchen Intern at Nestlé Research, your mission is to help bring fresh, homemade cuisine to life every day, from daily menus in our restaurant and cafeterias to buffets and catering for internal events and VIP receptions. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with our Chef, Sous-Chef and Chef de Partie, as well as our service and stewarding teams. This is your opportunity to make an impact while gaining hands on experience and mentorship to kick-start your career.

How you’ll make an impact as a Hot Kitchen Intern

  • Preparing dishes from scratch every day, following recipes, portioning and presentation standards to deliver consistent quality and taste across daily service and events.
  • Championing food safety and hygiene by applying high standards for labeling, temperature control, stock rotation and clean workstations.
  • Organizing and prioritizing your mise en place so every service runs smoothly and on time, even during the busiest moments.
  • Supporting colleagues across the kitchen, service and stewarding teams to build a positive team spirit where knowledge is shared.
  • Bringing fresh ideas and new recipes to enrich our culinary offering, while reducing food waste through smart planning and portioning.

What will make you successful

  • Being a Current student in Culinary Arts
  • Bringing hands-on experience in a professional kitchen, whether in a restaurant, hotel or collective catering.
  • Having confident knife skills, a strong command of cooking techniques and food presentation, and a practical understanding of food safety and hygiene.
  • Being reliable, organized and detail-oriented, with a genuine passion for quality, seasonal, homemade food and the drive to learn and adapt quickly in a fast-paced environment.
  • Communicating fluently in English
  • French is an asset.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

At Nestlé Research, we place people at the heart of everything we do

  • Our benefits are designed to support your growth and wellbeing, so you can thrive at work and in life:
  • Learning and development opportunities to grow your career

What to expect from our hiring process

We are committed to an inclusive and respectful hiring experience and are happy to provide reasonable accommodations if needed. Our workplaces are fully accessible and supported by a strong safety culture, with modern installations and procedures to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

The hiring process typically includes an interview with Talent Acquisition, an interview with the hiring team, and a final discussion and offer. We review applications as they are received and close the role once a strong list of relevant candidates is identified, so we encourage you to apply early. All information is treated confidentially.

Stay vigilant: Nestlé will only contact candidates via official company email addresses and will never ask for payments or sensitive personal information at any stage of the hiring process.
