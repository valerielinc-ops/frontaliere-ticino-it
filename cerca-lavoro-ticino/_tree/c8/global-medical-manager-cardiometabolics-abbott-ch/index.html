Abbott | Established Pharmaceuticals Division

At Abbott , we believe people can live fuller lives through better health. Every day, our medicines and technologies help millions of people around the world manage health conditions, overcome barriers to care, and achieve better outcomes.

Within our Branded Generics business, we provide trusted, high-quality medicines to more than 18 million people daily across Latin America, Asia-Pacific, India, and other emerging markets. Our portfolio spans areas including Women's Health, Gastroenterology, Cardiometabolic Health, CNS & Pain, and Respiratory Care , helping address some of healthcare's most pressing needs.

Abbott Established Pharmaceutical Division (EPD) is looking for a

Global Medical Manager - Cardiometabolics

for its global Pharma Division Headquarter based in Allschwil-Basel.

About the Opportunity

We are hiring to strengthen for growth and are looking for an experienced Global Medical Advisor / Medical Affairs Manager for our Cardio Area.

The job holder will report to the related Global Medical Director of Cardiometabolics and will have a close cooperation with the regional and local Medical Directors, the Innovation & Development Department and Global Marketing.

Main Responsibilities

  • Responsible for specific activities and strategic projects within Medical Affairs for the Cardiometabolics Portfolio
  • Support the Global Medical Director in reviewing and approving global and regional medical / clinical studies and publication proposals for the Therapeutic Area
  • In close collaboration with the Global and Regional Marketing Teams, plan and execute prelaunch activities of cross-regional products/brands
  • With collaboration of regional medical teams run Key Opinion Leader assessment and identification projects, organize Key Opinion Leaders database, ensure regular updates, identify potential global Key Opinion Leaders, and inform internal stakeholder on potential global Key Opinion Leader candidates
  • Work on medical maintenance of global brands by participating in product safety team meetings, Labeling team meetings, writing and/or reviewing scientific documents, updating product scientific library, etc.
  • Collaborating with marketing team on developing promotional materials, including sales aids, web pages, digital platforms, etc. and ensuring scientific validity of the contents
  • For geo-expansion of local products, update/upgrade scientific part of registration dossiers in order to get them into approvable standards in other countries.
  • Conduct medical assessments on new Licensing & Acquisition opportunities for portfolio expansion

The profile

  • Medical Doctor degree or PhD in related life sciences with relevant experience in regional or global Medical Affairs for Cardiometabolics or Endocrinology
  • At least 5 years of regional or global responsibility for compareable Products
  • Operating within and cross-functional international area
  • Strong project management skills across several functional areas
  • Previous international experience, especially in emerging markets is a plus
  • Willed to work hands on and be able to think strategically at the same time
  • Strong organizational skills, able to communicate on different levels and used to adapt fast to a quick changing environment
  • Very good English language skills are required, any other language would be an asset
