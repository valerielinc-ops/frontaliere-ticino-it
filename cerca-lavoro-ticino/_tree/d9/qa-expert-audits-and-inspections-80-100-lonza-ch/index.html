Descrizione completa

The actual location of this job is in Stein, AG, Switzerland. Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed.

Every day, our teams across the world work together to turn scientific breakthroughs into life-changing medicines that reach up to 100 million patients a year. Lonza is a place where people can make a unique impact, grow their careers and belong.

You will coordinate and support audits and inspections at an innovative manufacturing site equipped with state-of-the-art sterile filling technologies, ensuring the smooth execution of all audit-related activities from the back office. This role offers exciting exposure to international customer and regulatory inspections, along with excellent opportunities to further develop your expertise in quality, compliance, and inspection management over the long term. Check out Your Career in Drug Product Services at Lonza! This role is primarily based on site. Working together in person supports close, real‑time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards. We offer flexibility to work remotely for no more than 20% of your time. Requests are considered on a case‑by‑case basis, in line with business needs.

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance.
  • A variety of benefits dependent on role and location.

What you will do

  • Coordinate and support regulatory inspections and customer audits, including preparation, hosting activities, inspection logistics, and audit documentation.
  • Act as a key back-office partner during audits and inspections, ensuring efficient coordination, timely responses, and seamless communication with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Drive audit and inspection follow-up activities in TrackWise, partnering with subject matter experts to develop and track effective CAPA plans.
  • Contribute to inspection readiness initiatives for a state-of-the-art sterile filling facility, helping maintain a culture of continuous compliance and quality excellence.
  • Support GMP compliance and Quality Management System activities, including change controls, deviations, CAPAs, self-inspections, document management, and implementation of global quality standards.

What we are looking for

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Life Sciences or a related field, combined with several years of work experience in a GMP-regulated manufacturing environment (e.g. in shopfloor GMP production, MSAT, QC or similar background).
  • Fluent English communication skills are essential, enabling effective collaboration with global stakeholders, customers, and regulatory authorities.
  • Strong understanding of GMP principles, quality processes, and compliance requirements is a must; experience in sterile manufacturing or biologics/drug substance operations is an advantage.
  • Customer audit and/or regulatory inspection experience is beneficial, with a keen interest in developing expertise in audit and inspection management.
  • Willingness to work primarily at our Stein site and travel approximately one day per week to Basel
  • German skills are a plus, and you should be motivated to further develop your German language proficiency to support your own long-term career growth.

You will feel at home at Lonza if you

  • Love solving problems and thrive in a complex, fast-moving environment
  • Are curious, always looking to learn and improve
  • Work collaboratively, contributing to something bigger than yourself
  • Are motivated to make a difference through work that impacts customers and their patients
