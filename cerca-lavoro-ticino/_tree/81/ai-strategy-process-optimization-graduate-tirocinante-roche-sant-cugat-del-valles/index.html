At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. The Position Purpose of the role: Your mission is to support the evolution of our operational landscape. You will focus on converting traditional routine processes into agile, AI-enhanced workflows. Additionally, you will provide essential support to our Document Management team, ensuring key project documents are managed and processed accurately within our Electronic Document Management Systems (EDMS), specifically DiaDoc and SAP. Main responsibilities:

  • Process Analysis: Critically evaluate established routine processes to identify specific opportunities for AI-driven optimization and efficiency gains.
  • Implementation & Strategy: Support the deployment and maintenance of intelligent solutions while assisting with internal communication strategies to ensure smooth adoption.
  • Operational Support: Assist in the daily management of project documentation, ensuring high standards of data integrity and accessibility. How would your day to day look like?
  • Immersive Learning: You will start by integrating into our daily routines, gaining a deep understanding of our workflows and the interdependencies that drive our business.
  • Collaborative Problem-Solving : You will work closely with cross-functional team members to understand their pain points and translate their ideas into tangible efficiency gains.
  • Technical Innovation: You will spend significant time developing and supporting intelligent, AI-based solutions tailored to our specific organizational needs.
  • Hands-on Document Management : You will gain practical experience in an international regulatory environment by processing selected project documents within our professional EDMS. Who are you?
  • Education: You are a recent Bachelor’s or Master’s graduate looking to launch your career in a high-impact environment.
  • Technical Agility: You possess a sophisticated understanding of the current AI landscape and have the technical aptitude to integrate smart solutions into a professional corporate infrastructure.
  • Solution-Oriented Mindset: You are a motivated problem-solver who is eager to take digital infrastructure to the next level.
  • Global Perspective: You are excited to collaborate with international teams and thrive in a fast-paced, world-class diagnostic organization. Who we are A healthier future drives us to innovate.
