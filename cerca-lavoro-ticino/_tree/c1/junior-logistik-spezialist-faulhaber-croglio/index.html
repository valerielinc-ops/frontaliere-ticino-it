CH - Croglio

Logistics

Fixed employment

Young professional

We are looking for a young and motivated professional who already has a basic understanding of logistics and supply chain processes, combined with a strong interest and background in IT and digital systems. The position offers the opportunity to develop expertise in logistics processes, SAP, data analysis, warehouse management and continuous improvement.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Support the Logistics Manager in the daily management and analysis of logistics activities.
  • Learn and progressively take responsibility for specific logistics processes.
  • Work with SAP and other IT systems supporting logistics and warehouse operations.
  • Analyze logistics data and prepare reports and KPIs.
  • Support the optimization of warehouse processes and material flows.
  • Identify opportunities for process improvement and participate in improvement projects.
  • Support the implementation and optimization of digital solutions within Logistics.
  • Collaborate with Logistics, Production, IT and other departments.
  • Gradually develop ownership of specific processes and projects.

YOUR PROFILE

  • ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENTS
  • Initial experience, education or training in Logistics, Supply Chain, Warehouse Management or a related field.
  • A solid IT background or strong interest and aptitude for IT systems and digital solutions.
  • Good command of English, both written and spoken.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • A structured and practical approach to work.
  • Curiosity, initiative and a strong willingness to learn.
  • Ability to work both independently and as part of a team
  • ADDITIONAL SKILLS CONSIDERED AS A PLUS
  • Experience with SAP.
  • Experience in an industrial or manufacturing environment.
  • Knowledge of warehouse management systems or ERP systems.
  • Experience with data analysis and reporting.
