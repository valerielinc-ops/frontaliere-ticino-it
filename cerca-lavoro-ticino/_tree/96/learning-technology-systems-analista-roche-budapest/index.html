The Position Learning Technology Systems Analyst The Opportunity Network Quality QA/QC Operational Systems team works in partnership across major global business functions, establishing and implementing an overall Technology strategy for PT Quality and driving the delivery and scale of key solutions in support of our PT vision. In this role you will serve as the Global Product Owner, or sub-product owner, for applicable technologies used in PT. The PO will be responsible for translating business use cases and project requirements into standardized technology solutions. This role requires a blend of technical expertise and the ability to bridge the gap between intricate business challenges and transformative solutions. Working in multidisciplinary teams, you’ll maintain, transform, and construct technologies integral to our technology landscape. This role will also be responsible for global master data entity. As a PO, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams consisting of business stakeholders, use case leads, IT, and validation teams to understand business needs, translate them into technical requirements, and deliver impactful solutions that are compliant to regulations. This role works with Business and Informatics to ensure high quality innovative solutions are defined, developed, enhanced and implemented using consistent technologies and processes and assists the organization with expertise during inspections, investigation and resolution of deviations and implementation of initiatives. This role calls for a collaborative, open-minded individual who values learning from colleagues, thoughtfully challenges ideas, and prioritizes impactful work. We seek someone who thrives in a dynamic environment, has a passion for continuous learning, and can be relied upon to work in the best interest of the team.
