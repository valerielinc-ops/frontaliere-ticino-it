The SC transformation project manager ADS Visp is responsible for the implementation of the supply chain transformation related manufacturing execution activities for ADS in Visp. The transformation is supposed to be in line with the global Supply Chain transformation goals and the operational goals of the involved departments. The responsibility includes to ensure material staging to production assets and infrastructure to allow all time seamless operations activities. A key part of the role is also to ensure the close collaboration with further departments within the Supply Chain team but also related functions (Central SC, Operations, procurement and quality) and ensure alignments. Implement system solutions (e.g. SAP ERP and OMP interface) that support the different roles in day-to-day tasks is another key area in this role.

What you will do

  • Lead Supply Chain transformation projects, acting as the primary point of contact and gatekeeper between the business, global functions, and IT.
  • Identify and prioritize improvement opportunities in close collaboration with the line manager; design effective solutions and drive implementation with relevant stakeholders.
  • Support the Supply Chain ADS team in Visp in delivering key strategic initiatives and transformation goals.
  • Build and maintain a strong cross-functional network within Supply Chain and related functions, engaging stakeholders across all levels up to the ADS Site Leadership team.
  • Drive and ensure effective change management, promoting adoption and alignment across Supply Chain and connected functions.
  • Provide clear, timely, and accurate communication on transformation progress to all relevant stakeholders.

What we are looking for

  • Extensive experience in manufacturing and supply chain, with strong expertise in SAP ERP PP and MM modules.
  • Additional knowledge in OMP planning, SAP EWM, SAP S/4, and MES interfaces is considered a strong advantage.
  • Master’s degree (or equivalent) in Supply Chain, Operations, or a scientific discipline.
  • Strong communication skills with proven experience in cross-functional project management and collaboration across supply chain, production, quality, and logistics.
  • Solution-oriented professional with solid experience in driving and managing change initiatives.
  • Highly motivated team player with excellent planning and organizational skills, capable of operating effectively in complex environments.
  • Strong interpersonal and leadership capabilities, with an independent working style requiring minimal supervision
  • Fluent in English, with German as a strong advantage.
