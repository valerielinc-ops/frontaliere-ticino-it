Are you an experienced housekeeping professional with a proven track record in 5\-star luxury hospitality? We are seeking dedicated, detail\-oriented experienced Room Attendants who take pride in flawless presentation, discreet service, and maintaining the highest standards of luxury housekeeping.

Your Responsibilities

  • Flawless Room Maintenance: Daily cleaning, turndown (evening service), and deep maintenance of guest rooms and luxury suites in strict accordance with 5\-star Swiss, LQA, and Forbes Travel Guide guidelines.
  • Eye for Detail: Ensuring pristine presentation of furniture, luxury textiles, floral arrangements, and guest amenities to create an unforgettable first impression.
  • Public \& Guest Area Care: Assisting with the upkeep of high\-profile guest areas, public rest spaces, and back\-of\-house logistics as required.
  • Guest Delight \& Service: Managing minibar replenishment, handling guest requests with utmost discretion, and delivering personalized service touches.
  • Quality \& Hygiene: Adhering strictly to hotel hygiene protocols, safety regulations, and efficient linen/inventory management.

This is you:

  • Minimum 2–3 years of experience as a Room Attendant strictly in a 5\-star luxury hotel (experience in top\-tier Swiss or Alpine luxury properties is strongly preferred).
  • Standard Knowledge: Solid understanding of LQA (Leading Quality Assurance) and/or Forbes Travel Guide standards in housekeeping.
  • Mindset \& Discretion: Absolute reliability, physical resilience, impeccable manners, and complete discretion when interacting with high\-net\-worth international guests.
  • Language Skills: Functional conversational skills in English OR (ability to follow instructions, safety standards, and guest requests); Italian is a strong advantage.
  • Appearance: Flawless personal grooming and a professional demeanor at all times.

What We Offer

  • Work in World\-Class Properties: Be part of an award\-winning housekeeping team in the heart of the Swiss Alps.
  • Professional Environment: Structured workflows, clear quality benchmarks, and continuous training in luxury standards.
  • Attractive Package: Competitive seasonal compensation, employee accommodation in the Engadin, subsidized meals in our staff restaurant, and exclusive group benefits.

Do you meet the 5\-star experience requirements? We look forward to receiving your complete application (CV and employment references).
