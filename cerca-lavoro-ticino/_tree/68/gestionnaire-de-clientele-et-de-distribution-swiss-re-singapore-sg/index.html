Descrizione completa

About the Role Reporting directly to the Head Customer and Distribution Manager SEA, you will drive Swiss Re Corporate Solutions’ strategy with distribution partners and key customers.

The primary responsibilities of the role are

Distribution Management Strategy & Planning

  • Align with brokers on local strategic priorities
  • Execute go-to-market plans with managed brokers to achieve top and bottom line goals
  • Understand brokers' country and regional strategies and communicate insights internally
  • Implement local and regional distribution campaigns

Engagement & Execution

  • Define and manage regular engagement models (e.g., quarterly reviews)
  • Build and manage deal pipelines in coordination with underwriters
  • Support portfolio priorities (OBR) with managed brokers

Data & Insights

  • Analyze broker performance data to identify opportunities and trends
  • Use Swiss Re's thought leadership to strengthen broker relationships
  • Gather and share market intelligence with internal teams

Escalation & Governance

  • Act as primary contact for broker issues requiring escalation
  • Support internal deal escalations for complex cases
  • Contribute to planning and executing Broker Service Agreements

Customer Management Strategy & Planning

  • Develop go-to-market plans for key customers aligned with financial and strategic goals.
  • Build and deepen relationships with key external stakeholders.
  • Ensure consistent knowledge sharing with underwriting, claims, and other internal teams.
  • Support regional initiatives to enhance key customer relationships.

Engagement & Business Development

  • Understand client needs and act as a trusted advisor.
  • Generate new business opportunities and optimize product offerings.
  • Lead all business transactions, renewals, and strategic relationship activities.
  • Drive client negotiations and coordinate cross-functional teams for delivery.
  • Prepare and contribute to executive-level meetings.

Knowledge & Innovation

  • Promote thought leadership with customers via events, webinars, and publications (e.g., SIGMA).
  • Facilitate forums, learning sessions, and speaking opportunities.

Compliance & Performance

  • Ensure adherence to Swiss Re’s business guidelines and Code of Conduct.
  • Model Swiss Re’s values and key behaviors.
  • Meet all relevant internal and external deadlines.

Performance Criteria

  • Financial metrics (e.g., GWP, CoR, retention, number of opportunities).
  • Contribution to strategic and cultural objectives.
  • Progress against agreed strategic initiatives.

About You

Education

  • University degree or equivalent professional experience.

Professional Experience

  • 7-10 years of insurance industry experience in client, distribution, underwriting, risk management
  • Strong network across brokers, corporate risk managers, and insurance professionals.
  • Proven success in sales, relationship management, and complex deal execution.
  • Experience with CRM systems

Competencies & Other attributes

  • Strategic thinker with strong business acumen and results focus.
  • Excellent communicator and negotiator with conflict resolution skills.
  • Creative, solution-oriented, and proactive leader.
  • Collaborative, adaptable, and culturally aware.
  • High integrity with a feedback-oriented mindset.
  • Empowering team player who supports and develops colleagues.

Languages

  • Fluent in English; additional languages preferred depending on location.

About Swiss Re Corporate Solutions Swiss Re is one of the world’s leading providers of reinsurance, insurance and other forms of insurance-based risk transfer. We anticipate and manage risks, from natural catastrophes and climate change to cybercrime.

Swiss Re Corporate Solutions is the commercial insurance arm of the Swiss Re Group. We offer innovative insurance solutions to large and midsized multinational corporations from our approximately 50 locations worldwide. We help clients mitigate their risk exposure, whilst our industry-leading claims service provides them with additional peace of mind.

Our success depends on our ability to build an inclusive culture encouraging fresh perspectives and innovative thinking. Swiss Re Corporate Solutions embraces a workplace where everyone has equal opportunities to thrive and develop professionally regardless of their age, gender, race, ethnicity, gender identity and/or expression, sexual orientation, physical or mental ability, skillset, thought or other characteristics. In our inclusive and flexible environment everyone can bring their authentic selves to work and their passion for sustainability.

If you are an experienced professional returning to the workforce after a career break, we encourage you to apply for open positions that match your skills and experience.
