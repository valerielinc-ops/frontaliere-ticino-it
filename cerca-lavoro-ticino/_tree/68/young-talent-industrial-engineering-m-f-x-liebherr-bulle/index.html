Descrizione completa

Your responsabilities As a Young Talent in Industrial Engineering, you support the analysis and improvement of our assembly processes, with a particular focus on work measurement, standard times and process documentation. With guidance from experienced colleagues:

  • You conduct time studies for assembly and setup processes using REFA methods.
  • You analyse manual work sequences using MTM and contribute to determining, validating and documenting standard times.
  • You create, update and standardise assembly instructions and setup instructions in close collaboration with the production teams.
  • You carry out process analyses to identify improvement opportunities in work sequences, material flow, ergonomics and productivity.
  • You participate in value stream analyses, helping to assess material and information flows and identify opportunities to reduce lead times and waste.
  • You contribute to SMED workshops by analysing setup activities, identifying opportunities to separate internal and external setup operations, and supporting measures to reduce setup times.
  • You support the implementation of improvement measures on the shop floor and help evaluate their effectiveness through observations, measurements and data analysis . Your profile
  • You are currently pursuing or have completed a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Production Engineering or a related technical field, or you hold a Higher Technical Diploma (ES Technician).
  • You have a strong interest in Industrial Engineering, work measurement, lean manufacturing and the assembly of precision mechanical components.
  • Initial knowledge of REFA, MTM, value stream analysis or SMED is an advantage. You are motivated to develop your skills in these methods through training and practical application.
  • You are comfortable using MS Office, particularly Excel for data analysis and documentation. Familiarity with SharePoint, ERP systems or CAD software is an advantage.
  • You work accurately and systematically, have strong analytical skills and enjoy understanding processes directly on the shop floor.
  • You communicate clearly, enjoy working with production teams and can work both independently and collaboratively.
  • You have a good command of French, German and English and can communicate effectively in all three languages in a technical working environment. Our offer You will gain varied and practical experience in Industrial Engineering within an international family business. You will contribute to concrete improvements in our production processes, take on responsibilities suited to your development and have the opportunity to put your ideas into practice. A dynamic team will train, support and guide you as you develop your technical and methodological skills. You will benefit from modern working conditions, including flexible working hours, the possibility of working from home, a company restaurant and many other advantages.The workplace is located in Bulle, in the beautiful region of Gruyère. Plan your personal and professional development with us. To ensure a fair application procedure, we do not accept applications by post or email. Have we awoken your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your online application. If you have any questions, please contact Lea GRIVEL. One Passion. Many Opportunities. The company develops and produces various products, which find their use in building construction and civil engineering, mining, agriculture, decentralised energy, machine and plant engineering, as well as aerospace.
