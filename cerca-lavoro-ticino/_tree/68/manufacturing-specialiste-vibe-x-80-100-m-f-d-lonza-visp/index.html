Manufacturing Specialist Vibe-X 80%-100%(m/f/d)

Location: Visp, Switzerland

Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed.

Join our growing vaccine manufacturing team in Visp and play a key role in bringing innovative therapies to patients worldwide. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to both the startup of new manufacturing facilities and the ongoing production of critical biopharmaceutical products in a state-of-the-art GMP environment.

This is a fully site-based role. Working together in person supports close, real-time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What You Will Get

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance.
  • A variety of benefits dependent on role and location.

The full list of our global benefits can be found here: https://www.lonza.com/careers/benefits

What You Will Do

  • Support project teams in the development, implementation, and validation of innovative vaccine manufacturing processes and production facilities.
  • Execute manufacturing activities within Upstream Processing, Downstream Processing, or Bioconjugates operations in compliance with cGMP requirements and approved procedures.
  • Support Safety, Health & Environment (SHE), Environmental Monitoring (EM), operational, and cleaning risk assessments and contribute to the implementation of mitigation measures.
  • Review and prepare manufacturing documentation, including electronic batch records, SOPs, and production-related documents.
  • Investigate deviations, support change controls, and drive CAPA implementation to ensure compliance with GMP standards.
  • Act as manufacturing subject matter expert during capital investment, commissioning, qualification, and startup projects for new manufacturing units.
  • Train and support manufacturing teams on equipment operation, manufacturing processes, and standard procedures.

What We Are Looking For

  • Completed vocational training in a chemical, pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or related discipline, or a Bachelor's/Master's degree in Biotechnology, Biology, Biochemical Engineering, or a comparable field.
  • Experience or strong theoretical knowledge of biotechnological manufacturing processes, including microbial cultivation, mammalian cell culture, protein purification, or bioconjugation technologies.
  • Ability to work effectively within cross-functional teams and communicate confidently with stakeholders across different functions.
  • Strong problem-solving mindset with a proactive and solution-oriented approach.
  • Fluent English skills are required
  • German language skills are considered an advantage.
  • Willingness to work in a 4-shift manufacturing environment.
  • Basic understanding of GMP-regulated pharmaceutical or biopharmaceutical production is advantageous.
