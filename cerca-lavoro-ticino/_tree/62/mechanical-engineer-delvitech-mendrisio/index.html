Mechanical Engineer

ABOUT DELVITECH

Delvitech stands at the forefront of technological advancement, having pioneered innovative Automatic Optical Inspection (AOI) technology for assembled printed circuit boards (PCB) utilizing artificial intelligence. Our accomplished team is driven by a commitment to exceed technological boundaries and consistently deliver exceptional results. We are actively seeking individuals who are eager to embrace challenges, contribute proactively to achieving our set objectives, and play a pivotal role in propelling the company’s growth. Presently, an opportunity exists for a proficient and motivated Mechanical Engineer

JOB DESCRIPTION

This role is based at our headquarters in Mendrisio. The ideal candidate will bring a patient, organized, and problem-solving approach to their work and must have prior experience in a similar field.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Mechanical design and develop of industrial machines, including:
  • Design mounting for high performance linear motor stages,
  • Design leading-edge precise mechanical assemblies, to reduce cost and time of assembly
  • Design industrial machine structures and enclosures,
  • Design custom parts to package optics and electronics into compact spaces,
  • Design opto-mechanical mounts to provide precise alignment, adjustment, and robustness.
  • Handle design products from concept to production.
  • Identification of risk and reduction through feasibility prototypes, analysis, and testing.
  • Collaborate with the assembly workshop on design to make production quick and easy.
  • Collaborate closely with our interdisciplinary team (electronics and software) to contribute to the development of cutting-edge products and solutions.
  • Collaborate with old and new supply partners for the manufacturing of parts.
  • Create 2D drawings and design documents (e.g., ECR, ECO) for consultation with supply partners and assembly production.
  • Coordinate the assembly and testing of prototypes.
  • Coordinate transition of new designs from draft to production and support existing production.

REQUIREMENTS

  • Master or PhD degree in Mechanical Engineering or equivalent experience.
  • Experience in designing precise mechanical assemblies for linear motion.
  • Experience in designing precision mechanical assemblies for Cartesian machines
  • Experience in designing custom machined and fabricated parts.
  • Experience in designing opto-mechanical and electro-mechanical assemblies is a plus.
  • Experience in a structured development process from the definition of requirements to concept design, to detail design, to testing, and implementation into production.
  • Proficient in mechanical 3D CAD (Solidworks is preferred) and exposure to CAD data management(Solidworks PDM is preferred).
  • Proficient in 2D drawings and technical datasheet for manufacturing.
  • Proficient in spoken and written English.
  • Knowledge in design for production in series.
  • Knowledge in structural analysis and heat transfer analysis (vibration analysis is preferred).
  • Knowledge in manufacturing processes such as CNC, sheet metal, 3D printing.
  • General knowledge of electrical component is appreciated.
  • General knowledge of pneumatic system.
  • Problem solving skills and reasoning ability.
  • Self-motivated with a creative pioneer spirit.
  • Autonomy and self-management.
  • Good interpersonal skills.
  • Good oral and written communication skills.
  • Must work well in teams with other engineering disciplines, including electrical, electronics, optical and software.

If this position matches your skills and aspirations, please send your complete application, including CV and references, to career@delvi.tech with your name and “Mechanical Engineer” in the subject line. Only short list candidates will be contacted.
