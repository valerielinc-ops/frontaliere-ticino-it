The Position As the Global Business Process Specialist (GBPS) – Accounting Tax Integration, you are part of the Global Business Process Management Organization in Roche Service & Solutions. In this position, you report into the Accounting Tax Integration Sub-Chapter Lead within the Finance BPM.

You act as Business Process Specialist for the APAC Regional Deployment team in the Finance/ Tax area. You roll-out the Aspire Global Template to the APAC countries in the area of Taxes, including Tax Localization.

You will work in close collaboration with stakeholders from Finance, Commercials, Demand-to-Pay, Supply Chain and IT, cross divisionally (Pharma and Diagnostics).

You will be

  • Work closely with the E2E GBPMs & GBPS network to ensure fit-for-purpose solutions. Supports the SAP S4/HANA Template deployment. Actively pursue the implementation of the E2E BPM strategy, according to guardrails set by the E2E GBPO and GBPMs

Evaluate Local GAP and design localization extensions for the Global Template as part of the deployment activities for the Aspire roll out.
