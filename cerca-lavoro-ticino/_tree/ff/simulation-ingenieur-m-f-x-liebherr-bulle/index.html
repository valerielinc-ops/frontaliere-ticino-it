Your Responsibilities

  • You perform thermodynamic analyses and 1D simulations of combustion engines, including engine process simulation, gas exchange and turbocharger-engine matching, using GT-SUITE
  • You simulate oil and water circuits to analyze and optimize the thermal management of combustion engines
  • You develop simulation models for advanced thermal management strategies and support the evaluation of new engine concepts
  • you support and participate to discussion with supplier and technically advise purchasing team
  • You support design and development teams in defining technical concepts, selecting components and assessing their performance and mechanical limits
  • You analyze simulation results, prepare technical calculation reports and ensure the validation of simulation models in close collaboration with testing
  • You support test programmes and coordinate the experimental validation of simulation results and calculation models
  • You develop calculation tools, pre- and post-processing solutions and automated workflows to improve simulation efficiency and standardize engineering practices
  • You take technical ownership of specific development topics, contribute to strategic engineering activities and establish best practices and guidelines for engine performance Your Profile
  • You hold a degree in Mechanical Engineering or a comparable technical discipline
  • You have solid professional experience in 1D simulation of combustion engines and thermodynamic systems
  • You have advanced knowledge of GT-SUITE, Amesim or comparable simulation tools
  • You have a good understanding of combustion engine processes, gas exchange, turbocharger matching and thermal management
  • You are able to interpret complex simulation results, translate them into practical engineering recommendations and communicate technical findings effectively
  • You demonstrate a structured and analytical approach, combined with initiative, curiosity and the ability to drive technical topics independently
  • You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary environment and taking ownership of challenging development topics
