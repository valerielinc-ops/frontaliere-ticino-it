Descrizione completa

Are you eager to begin your professional career in a multinational environment? Join our 12-month internship program, gain practical purchasing experience and develop your capabilities within an international industrial company. We are looking for motivated candidates available to start their internship in February/March 2027. Your responsibilities

  • You will perform CFD analyses, particularly internal flow simulations, for Fuel Injection System (FIS) components covering high- and low-pressure systems,
  • You will carry out 1D system and component simulations to develop a deep understanding of physical processes and component behaviour,
  • You will analyse simulation results, prepare technical reports and validate simulation models in collaboration with testing teams,
  • You will define test programmes and coordinate experimental validation of simulation results and calculation models,
  • You will collaborate closely with component development teams to drive cost-effective and high-performance designs,
  • You will contribute to the development of Fuel Injection System (FIS) requirement specifications through simulation activities,
  • You will support pre-development projects, including simulations involving alternative sustainable fuels such as methanol and ethanol,
  • You will actively support and participate in tests related to simulation activities. Your profile
  • You have a degree in Mechanical or Aeronautical Engineering,
  • You already have product development experience, which is a valuable asset,
  • You have a strong understanding of physics, CFD and 1D simulation tools (AMESim),
  • You have knowledge of MATLAB, Python and MS Office,
  • You are fluent in English and French. German is an asset,
  • You are analytical, proactive and able to work independently,
  • You have strong communication and technical reporting skills. Our offer You will have the opportunity to immerse yourself in the professional world and gain varied and exciting experience within an international family-owned company. We offer you the chance to take on interesting assignments, assume responsibility, and contribute your own ideas. You will be trained, supported, and guided by a dynamic team. You will benefit from modern working conditions, including flexible working hours, the possibility to work remotely, an employee restaurant, and many other benefits. The position is based in Bulle, in the beautiful Gruyère region. Plan your personal and professional development with us. To ensure a fair recruitment process, we do not accept applications submitted by post or email. Have we awoken your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your online application. If you have any questions, please contact Sabrina Sotheary BEAN. One Passion. Many Opportunities. The company ​Liebherr Machines Bulle SA is the centre of excellence for combustion engines (diesel and gas engines) and hydraulic components (axial piston pumps and motors). The company develops and produces various products, which find their use in building construction and civil engineering, mining, agriculture, decentralised energy, machine and plant engineering, as well as aerospace.
