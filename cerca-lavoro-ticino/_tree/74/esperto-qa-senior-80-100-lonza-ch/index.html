The actual location of this job is in Stein, AG, Switzerland. Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed. Do you want to play a key role in ensuring the quality of raw materials behind the life-saving therapies of the future? This is a rare opportunity to join a small, dynamic QA team within Materials Management & Supply Chain, where your expertise will directly influence how quality processes for administering and controlling of raw materials are built, optimized, and made fit for the future. In this fast‑evolving environment, you’ll work across global processes and collaborate closely with various suppliers, internal departments, and other sites. You will use systems such as SAP, DMS, and TrackWise to drive change and ensure compliance and contribute to shaping new QMS platforms. This highly cross‑functional role offers strong development opportunities, especially as the organization grows. Are you keen to be part of an exciting grown project?   • An agile career and a dynamic work culture.

  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance. In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous lifestyle, family, and leisure benefits. Our full list of tailored local benefits can be found below.
  • Benefits in Stein: https://bit.ly/42DlBDk What you will do:
  • Act as QA SPOC for Material Management related topics for a new warehouse (under qualification), including and for defining GMP-requirements for material handling, segregation, cleanliness, traceability, storage conditions and contamination control.
  • Own raw material quality processes, including support to new material introductions, review and approval of material specifications and raw material qualification process. Support with quality agreements if required.
  • Execute drug substance batch disposition decisions and oversee material release.
  • Contribute to Warehouse and Logistics quality oversight, maintain material master data, and participate in audits and inspections.
  • Handle deviations, OOS/OOE investigations, and change control and create, review and approve SOPs.
  • Lead and support cross-functional projects, train team members, collaborate with internal and external partners. What we are looking for:
  • Solid work experience in QA in the GMP controlled environment, including handling deviations and CAPA activities.
  • Experience with warehouse management / material management / raw materials is an advantage.
  • Sound scientific or technical education background
  • Fluency in English is a must. German language skills are an advantage.
  • Ability to work accurately and efficiently in a regulated environment with a strong quality mindset.
  • Collaborative, proactive approach with the ability to engage across cross‑functional teams and adapt to evolving processes.
