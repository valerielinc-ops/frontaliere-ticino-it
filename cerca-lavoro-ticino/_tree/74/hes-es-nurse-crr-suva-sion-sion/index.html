Descrizione completa

The Romandy Rehabilitation Clinic (CRR) is a leading player in the field of specialised rehabilitation in Switzerland. A true pillar of the integrated care chain, it works closely with university hospitals and specialised centres to offer coordinated, innovative, and high-quality care focused on functional recovery and sustainable reintegration of patients. To strengthen our nursing team within our para-/tetraplegic rehabilitation sector, we are looking for a: HES – ES Nurse (Para-/tetraplegic rehabilitation sector)

Mission As an HES–ES nurse, you provide specialised care for patients with spinal cord injuries, including cases of paraplegia and tetraplegia, requiring complex care and personalised support throughout their rehabilitation journey. You actively contribute to the prevention of complications, the development of autonomy, and the improvement of patients’ quality of life, within an interdisciplinary approach centred on their rehabilitation goals.

In this role, you

  • Develop, implement, and evaluate individualised care plans, taking into account the specific needs related to spinal cord injuries.
  • Provide specialised technical and relational nursing care, particularly in the areas of bladder-sphincter and bowel management, pressure sore prevention, and monitoring of autonomic functions.
  • Identify, analyse, and anticipate risks of complications specific to spinal cord injuries, ensuring rigorous clinical monitoring and appropriate care.
  • Support patients in acquiring self-care skills, promoting their autonomy, active participation, and reintegration into their living environment.
  • Coordinate and supervise care provided by ASSC, respecting quality, safety standards, and professional competencies.
  • Actively participate in interdisciplinary clinical assessments and decisions, in collaboration with medical staff, therapists, and other professionals involved in rehabilitation.
  • Contribute to the support and therapeutic education of patients and their relatives, considering physical, psychological, and social dimensions.
  • Participate in mentoring learners, integrating new colleagues, and the continuous development of professional practices.

Your profile

  • Bachelor’s degree in nursing HES, ES diploma, or equivalent recognised qualification ( Swiss Red Cross recognition for foreign diplomas ).
  • Experience in a hospital setting and a strong interest in specialised rehabilitation and care for patients with spinal cord injuries.
  • Ability to provide complex care, exercise sound clinical judgement, and adapt interventions to the evolving health status of patients.
  • Strong sense of responsibility, professional autonomy, rigour, and ability to prioritise in a demanding care environment.
  • Excellent interpersonal and psychosocial skills, enabling the establishment of a professional, respectful therapeutic relationship adapted to situations of vulnerability and major life changes.
  • Team spirit, ability to collaborate interdisciplinarily, and willingness to actively contribute to a constructive work dynamic.
  • Stress resilience, emotional stability, and ability to maintain perspective in complex clinical and human situations.
  • Willingness to occasionally work in other units to promote continuity of care and inter-team support.
  • Motivation to develop specific expertise in the field of para-/tetraplegia and to commit long-term to patient support.
  • Proficiency in French, both written and spoken (minimum B2 level) .

Our offer

  • Long-term patient support: Follow patients’ progress and contribute directly to their return to autonomy.
  • Enriching environment: Collaborate with experts from various fields in an interdisciplinary approach.
  • Continuous training: Access a wide range of training to enhance your skills and develop your career.
  • Dynamic team: Work in a supportive environment where efforts are recognised and valued.
  • Social benefits: High social benefits, numerous perks, free parking, and much more .
