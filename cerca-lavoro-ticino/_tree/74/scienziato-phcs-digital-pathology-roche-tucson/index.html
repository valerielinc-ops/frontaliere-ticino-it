The Position A healthier future. It’s what drives us to innovate. To continuously advance science and ensure everyone has access to the healthcare they need today and for generations to come. Creating a world where we all have more time with the people we love. That’s what makes us Roche. Diagnostics are an integral part of decision-making along every step of a person’s health, wellness and disease journey. A diagnostic test alone can help clinicians change the course of someone’s healthcare experience – and their life. We are a healthcare pioneer and the industry’s global leader in in-vitro diagnostics, our Diagnostics Division pushes boundaries and challenges the status quo to redefine what’s possible. Our commitment to research and development means we can bring to the market integrated solutions in diagnostic testing, laboratory efficiency, healthcare digitalisation and decision support tools and software. Our products deliver increasingly precise information to help save lives, prevent or control the risk of infection and monitor treatment effectiveness. The Opportunity This position is primarily responsible for initiating, directing, and executing scientific research, development, and manufacturing process strategies to support both new and existing products. A central pillar of this role involves creating and scaling a comprehensive digital pathology workflow, bridging the gap between traditional histology and high-throughput data analysis. You will investigate the feasibility of applying a wide variety of scientific principles to potential inventions and products, with a specific emphasis on integrating state-of-the-art imaging tools and automated analysis platforms into the laboratory ecosystem. As a subject matter expert, you will maintain a broad knowledge of cutting-edge theories, serving as an in-house and outside consultant while making meaningful contributions to scientific literature and international conferences. You may act as a primary spokesperson for corporate scientific affairs and provide strategic advice to top management regarding the evolution of digital pathology and assay innovation. Furthermore, you will participate in and promote the professional growth of junior scientists. Utilizing advanced professional concepts, you will contribute to the development of product and process principles, achieving complex objectives through creative and effective methodologies.

  • Digital Pathology & CDx Development: Participate in the creation and implementation of digital pathology workflows for Companion Diagnostics (CDx) projects, ensuring all methods and processes are optimized for clinical-grade imaging and data integrity.
  • Research & Experimental Design: Plan and execute complex R&D projects using advanced scientific knowledge and DOE (Design of Experiments) to define new components, identify technical challenges, and develop potentially patentable reagents or systems.
  • Data Analysis & Process Improvement: Analyze experimental data to form technical conclusions and implement improvements in work processes or documentation, consistently promoting a "Quality First Time" approach to all deliverables.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with cross functional teams to share expertise, contribute to project planning, and deliver presentations of results to internal and external stakeholders to advance corporate scientific goals. Who You Are:
