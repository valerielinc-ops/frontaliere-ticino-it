Asset Management Workload: 50-100% Place of work: Level of experience: With work experience FlexOffice: on-site Set new standards and shaping the future together. With us, you don't just tick tasks off a list. You work alongside people who take responsibility, set high standards and have the courage to try new approaches. We believe trust and performance go hand in hand. Your ideas, your initiative and your contributions are what really matter to us. You will have the opportunity to make a difference for our customers, your team and your own personal development. Shape the future of Investment Accounting. Behind every investment decision are numbers people can trust, and you make sure they are. As the go-to expert for your market unit, you bring clarity to complex portfolios and give investment committees a reliable basis for decisions. You'll also help reinvent how Finance works as part of our transformation journey. Unlock your full potential: While this position can be filled from as little as 50%, we are ideally looking for someone who can join us with a workload of 80–100%.

What you'll be doing

  • Own Investment Accounting topics for your assigned market unit and act as a trusted business partner.
  • Ensure accurate investment accounting and period-end closings under IFRS and local GAAP.
  • Deliver meaningful analyses, reporting and insights for management and governance bodies.
  • Develop and maintain the internal control system and support regulatory compliance.
  • Contribute actively to our Finance transformation programmes including setting up SimCorp Dimension: designing processes, configuration and controls
  • Support internal and external audits
  • Partner with stakeholders across Finance, Investments and Risk.

What you'll bring to the role

  • Degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration or a related field.
  • 5-8 years of experience in Investment Accounting, Asset Management, Insurance or Financial Services.
  • Hands-on experience with SimCorp Dimension; SAP S/4HANA is a plus.
  • Strong knowledge of IFRS and local accounting standards.
  • Analytical mindset, collaborative approach and a passion for continuous improvement.
  • Fluent German and English skills.

Why join us? You'll work in a collaborative, international environment where your ideas matter. Together, we'll drive innovation, simplify complexity and create lasting impact while fostering a culture where everyone can thrive.
