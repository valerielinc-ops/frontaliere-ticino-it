The Position The Position Summary As the Chief of Staff for the Roche Diagnostics Japan Enabling Team, you will play a key role in the effective functioning and strategic success of the Japan Dia organization. Reporting to the Chapter Lead, ET Office (Dottedly to GM Japan), you will work side by side with the Dia Japan GM and Dia Japan Enabling Team as the “Chief of Staff”. In this role, you will be involved in the strategic planning processes across the organization through strong collaboration with key stakeholders and network teams. In addition, you will work with the ET to translate vision/ strategy to actionable deliverables and assist them in facilitating effective decision-making. You will follow through and update the ET on progress of these deliverables as well as communication to employees through dashboard or other avenues. You will work with ET to prioritize on agenda and work through the backlog. And you will plan, convene and facilitate ET meetings to ensure objectives are achieved. You will be responsible to: ● Support Japan Dia GM to develop short- and long-term priorities (business, financial and people) aligned to Roche Dia business objectives, by engaging in strategic planning processes across the organization through strong collaboration with key stakeholders and network teams. ● As Chief of Staff, support the ET to ensure they are setting and achieving outcomes, while building and maintaining an environment that keeps the team focused on top priorities. ● Convene and facilitate the ET meetings, plan the agenda and support the team in timely, well-informed decision making. ● Design and facilitate effective meetings by prioritizing key objectives, balancing content review with discussion, and moderating dialogue. ● Strengthen ET cohesiveness by working with the P&C and regular exchanges with individual ET members. ● Increase ET effectiveness

  • Facilitate the formation of ET squads, make accountability clear, track and monitor ET decisions and backlog; hold ET members accountable to decisions and backlog. ● Lead, supported by administrative staff, on all aspects of logistics and planning of ET meetings. ● Collaborate with the GM and subject matter experts to develop communications and presentation materials for various speaking engagements.
