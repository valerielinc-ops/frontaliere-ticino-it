About the role Job Summary The ESH Engineer is responsible for supporting basic environmental, safety, and health activities, including inspections, data collection, and compliance monitoring. This job profile is intended for early-career professionals who apply broad theoretical knowledge and require the ability to investigate and analyse problems. Solutions are typically discussed/verified with a more senior member. Main tasks and responsibilities:

  • Implement applicable national workplace safety laws and regulations, standards and specifications, and the company’s safety management system; implement the safety production responsibility system.
  • Conduct daily on-site safety inspections and hazard identification and rectification. Focus on high-risk activities such as machinery operations, welding, chemical use, dust-related work, working at height, hot work, temporary electrical work, and heavy lifting/handling.
  • Supervise workers correct wearing and use of personal protective equipment (PPE) and promptly stop the “three violations” (unauthorized command, unsafe/unauthorized operation, and violation of work discipline).
  • Organize three-level safety induction training for new employees, pre-job/toolbox safety briefings, special safety training, and emergency drills to enhance safety awareness and emergency response capability.
  • Participate in permit-to-work approvals and on-site monitoring for hazardous operations; verify work conditions, control measures, and personnel qualifications to ensure compliant execution.
  • Conduct periodic inspection, maintenance, and management of fire-fighting facilities, emergency equipment, and protective supplies to ensure they are in good condition and effective.
  • Improve safety signs, warning boards, and safety communication materials, and promote on-site safety culture.
  • Participate in the investigation and handling of safety incidents/accidents; perform root-cause analysis, record and report findings, and track implementation of corrective actions.
  • Establish and maintain safety records, inspection logs, training files, and hazard rectification closure records to ensure they are standardized and complete.
  • Complete other workplace safety management tasks assigned by supervisors.
