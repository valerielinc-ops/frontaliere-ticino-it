The Position The Solution Architect focuses on designing business-driven, standards-based, and future-ready solutions within a specific solution context. In this role, you will design solutions that meet product requirements, business priorities, and enterprise standards while managing non-functional requirements (NFRs) and fitness functions. You will develop and maintain the product's technology roadmap, provide technical oversight to product teams, and evaluate new technologies to continuously improve existing solutions and manage technical debt. Description of the area Senior Solution Architect for ERP Application Lifecycle Management (ALM) with a focus on Technical Extensibility and Automation. Responsible for the strategic design and operational excellence of the "Requirements to Deploy" process. This role bridges the gap between high-level ALM frameworks and the underlying technical architecture. You will lead the design of custom extensions, workflows, and integrations within the SAP ALM ecosystem (Solution Manager and Cloud ALM), ensuring that the toolset is not just "out-of-the-box" but optimized through advanced development patterns to meet complex enterprise needs.
