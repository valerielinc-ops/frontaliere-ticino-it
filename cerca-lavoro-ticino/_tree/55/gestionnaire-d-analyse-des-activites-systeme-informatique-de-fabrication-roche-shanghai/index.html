The Position Responsible for business management, process management, and user management of manufacturing IT systems. 负责生产IT系统的业务管理、流程管理和用户管理。 1. Business Requirement Analysis: Communicate with production business departments to gather and analyze their needs, ensuring IT systems meet business requirements, support and enhance business efficiency, and comply with GxP regulations. 业务需求分析： 与生产业务部门沟通，收集和分析其需求，确保IT系统满足业务需求、支持和提升业务效率且符合GxP法规。 2. System Evaluation: Assess the performance of existing production IT systems, propose improvements and new system requirements based on business needs, and assist in selecting suitable technical solutions. 系统评估： 评估现有生产IT系统的表现，结合业务需求提出改进建议和新的系统需求，并协助选择合适的技术解决方案。 3. Process Optimization: Analyze current business processes, identify areas for improvement, and develop and implement optimization plans. 流程优化： 分析当前业务流程，识别改进点，制定并实施优化方案。 4. Data-Driven Decision Making: Utilize data analysis tools to generate insights reports, supporting business decisions and process optimization. 数据驱动决策： 利用数据分析工具，生成洞察报告，支持业务决策和流程优化。 5. User Access Control: Manage user permissions to ensure only authorized personnel can access corresponding data and functions. 用户权限控制： 管理用户权限，确保只有获得授权的人员才能访问权限相应的数据和功能。 6. Business Continuity Planning (BCP): Develop and maintain business continuity plans to ensure production and business activities can continue in emergencies. 业务持续计划（BCP）： 制定和维护业务持续性计划，以确保在紧急情况下生产和业务活动能够持续进行。 7. Audit Trail Review: Participate in periodical audit trail review to ensure data and operational traceability, complying with GxP regulations. 审计追踪审查： 参与定期的审计追踪审查，确保数据和操作的可追溯性，符合GxP法规的要求。 8. User Support and Training: Support IT system training for production and business teams, assisting them in effectively utilizing IT system features. 用户支持与培训： 支持对生产业务团队的IT系统培训，协助他们能够高效利用IT系统的功能。 9. Problem Solving: Assist in identifying and resolving IT system issues in business applications, providing continuous support and improvement suggestions. 问题解决：协助发现和解决IT系统在业务应用中的问题，提供持续的支持和改进建议。 10. Project Management: Lead or participate in projects related to production IT systems to ensure business goals are met. 项目管理： 领导或参与与生产IT系统相关的项目，确保达到业务目标。 11.
