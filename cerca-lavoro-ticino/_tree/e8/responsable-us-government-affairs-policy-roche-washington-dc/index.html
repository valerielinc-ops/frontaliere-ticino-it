The Position The Opportunity The Head of US Government Affairs & Policy leads Roche Diagnostics’ US government affairs and public policy strategy, shaping the legislative and regulatory environment to advance innovation, protect market access, influence healthcare policy, and mitigate risk. Based in Washington, D.C., this executive serves as Roche Diagnostics’ principal representative to policymakers, regulatory agencies, trade associations and external stakeholders, while acting as a strategic advisor to senior leadership on political and policy developments affecting the business. This role reports to the Global Head of Quality, Regulatory & External Affairs, with a dotted-line relationship to the President and CEO, Roche Diagnostics North America.
