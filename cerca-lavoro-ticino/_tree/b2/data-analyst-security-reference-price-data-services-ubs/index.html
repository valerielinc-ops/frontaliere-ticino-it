Descrizione completa

Do you have strong analytical skills? Are you familiar with statistical concepts and able to transform data into bespoke content? Do you want to help us expand our data platform? We’re looking for a data analyst to:

  • validate Security Reference & Price data from vendors / Internal sources to ensure completeness, accuracy, and consistency.
  • perform operational processes (manual data input / manual data amendment & enrichment, report generation) to enable service delivery.
  • analyze input and created data to identify patterns, trends, anomalies, and exceptions; with purpose of taking necessary corrective action.
  • resolve user queries from Portfolio Managers, Risk Managers, and other teams, in line with defined Service Level Agreements
  • document and review standard operating procedures to ensure its fit for purpose.
  • implement Data Governance policies – in line with Divisional & Group Data Management Framework
  • provide business analysis and requirements to refine / create data management processes and system flows.
  • collaborate with other teams in UBS Asset Management & beyond to ensure seamless integration of data within various systems and applications.
