Your role As part of Operations, you will play a critical role in ensuring the firm operates efficiently, with strong risk control and a seamless client experience. You will be responsible for the accurate and timely execution of securities settlement and transaction processing activities, while contributing to continuous improvement and operational excellence.

In this role, you will

  • Process securities settlement and transaction processes with a high level of accuracy and timeliness
  • Ensure strong adherence to controls, reconciliations, and regulatory requirements
  • Act as a key escalation point for operational issues and support timely resolution
  • Collaborate with internal stakeholders and external counterparties to manage transaction flows
  • Identify inefficiencies and contribute to process improvements and automation initiatives
  • Support digital transformation and continuous improvement within the function
  • Promote a risk-aware, accountable, and collaborative team culture

We’re looking for a Securities Settlement Specialist to

  • Execute processes and controls in a timely, accurate, and complete manner
  • Understand and manage risks associated with your business and function
  • Work across teams and functions to identify the best solutions or courses of action
  • Investigate issues and communicate effectively with internal teams and external counterparties
  • Act as a first point of escalation for operational and people-related matters
  • Proactively identify issues and drive timely resolution
  • Collaborate effectively across teams to deliver optimal outcome
