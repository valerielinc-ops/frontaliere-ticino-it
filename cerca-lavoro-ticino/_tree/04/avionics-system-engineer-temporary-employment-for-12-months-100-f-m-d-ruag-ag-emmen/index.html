This position will initially be hired on a temporary basis with option to extend and/or possibility for permanent employment

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make as significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Armed Forces and other emergency and security organizations can comprehensibly carry out their tasks at all times.

  • Capture requirements, assess feasibility, and define initial design concepts
  • Coordinate the design, development, integration, testing, and certification of complex avionics projects
  • Ensure compliance with relevant regulations for system‑integration certification
  • Establish and review system requirement documents, certification reports and safety analyses (FHA/SSA)
  • Manage external suppliers and liaise with customers, procurement sales and authorities
  • Support new‑technology and innovation initiatives
  • Provide technical guidance to our production and customer‑support teams

What you should bring with you

  • University degree in Electronics or Aerospace Engineering
  • 5 years+ practical experience in systems engineering for integrated avionics within aerospace sector or comparable safety‑critical industry
  • Experience with military aviation or tactical systems is a plus
  • Familiarity with EFIS, FMS, FCS (advantage) and EW/ECM/Weapon systems
  • Working knowledge of FAA/EASA/MAA certification requirements
  • Self‑starter personality with capability to meet tight schedules and collaborate in multicultural teams
  • Business‑level English (C1); German language skills are a strong advantage

About the field of activity

As an Avionics System Engineer you will work in an international working environment, with open-minded, ambitious, and enthusiastic coworkers. You will work on civil and military aircraft (helicopters, fix-wing and UAV) and state of the art mission systems, with the great opportunity to help refine our system development process, getting involved in all phases from conceptual design to lab and flight testing. In this role you will work in our Engineering Center in Emmen

  • Leisure activities: We care about our employees. That is why we give them a day off for their birthday, offer a wide range of sports activities and the opportunity to take additional days off with a pay deduction in addition to their annual vacation entitlement.
