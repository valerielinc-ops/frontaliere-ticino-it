The Position As an IT Business Analyst, you will act as a crucial catalyst for our digital transformation, empowering effective decision-making and continuous improvement across our end-to-end quality operations You will join a forward-thinking, collaborative team dedicated to creating and implementing robust IT infrastructure solutions that directly enable high-performing business processes. This unique role allows you to serve as an ambassador for digitization, turning complex challenges into streamlined, user-friendly solutions that ensure the highest standards of product quality and regulatory compliance. The Opportunity:

  • Drive comprehensive business analysis to outline problems, identify opportunities, and deliver pragmatic software solutions to process owners
  • Implement and maintain critical quality management tools—such as the Global List of Approved Suppliers and Supplier Risk Scorecards—while performing necessary testing and validation
  • Design visually engaging training documentation by translating and mapping complex business processes to ensure cross-functional stakeholder alignment
  • Champion lean and agile ways of working by supporting process owners in troubleshooting issues and enhancing overall reliability and simplicity
  • Analyze large data sets to evaluate existing databases, extracting actionable insights that support external quality decisions and supplier-customer relationships
  • Act as an organizational change agent and digitization ambassador, proactively identifying, applying sound judgment to, and escalating potential quality or regulatory issues Who You Are:
  • You hold a Bachelor’s or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Sciences (such as Pharmacy or Chemistry), or possess equivalent professional experience
  • You have demonstrated experience managing large datasets, utilizing advanced computer skills including Microsoft Office, Google Suite, and data visualization software like Tableau
  • You possess an agile, analytical mindset with a proven ability to synthesize complex topics into clear, pragmatic overviews and actionable recommendations
  • You are an exceptional communicator and quick thinker with strong organizational and time-management skills, allowing you to effectively collaborate with diverse teams
  • You bring a strong foundation in problem-solving and IT infrastructure, with any additional knowledge of Python and R being a distinct advantage for this 12-month opportunity. Additional Information:
