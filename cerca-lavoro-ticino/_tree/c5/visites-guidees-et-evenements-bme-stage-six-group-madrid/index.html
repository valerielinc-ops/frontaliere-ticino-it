Guided Tours & BME Events Internship Madrid | Flexible model | Reference 8004 Are you studying and do you have skills in the field of events management, communication and public relations? Are you interested in the Communication and Marketing sector and looking for an interesting internship to learn more about financial sector? If your answer is yes, our Events and Conferences team, responsible for events and guided tours at the Palacio de la Bolsa, would like to meet you. We are looking for a bright and dedicated person who enjoys working in a team and wants to do a morning internship at BME, here you will provide hands-on assistance in the Events and Conferences area. The function will be based at the Palacio de la Bolsa de Madrid (Plaza de la Lealtad, 1). Don't hesitate and apply now with your CV in English. What You Will Do

  • carry out the guided tours, oversee mailbox and answer inquiries for guided tours, administrating and managing all incoming requests (mail/telephone)
  • collaboration in the development of the materials and speeches for the guided tours, acquiring knowledge and history related to the stock market and its surroundings (Stock Exchange Palace)
  • support the event managers in all aspects of event management (planning, preparing, execution, event material, invitation and registration management, etc.)
  • provide ad hoc support to the events team in their daily work
  • on-Site support at events What You Bring
  • studies in Marketing, Communication, Tourism, Bussines Administration or Event Organization
  • ability to work in a dynamic and global environment with excellent organizational and communication skills
  • strong organizational skills and personal flexibility
  • willingness to collaborate and work autonomously paired with good time management skills
  • very good level of spoken and written English and Spanish (B2 minimum)
  • experience in hosting virtual events is an advantage If you have any questions, check out our FAQ page or call Sara Perez de la Cuesta at +34 91 709 56 80 . For this vacancy we only accept direct applications in English. Diversity is important to us.
