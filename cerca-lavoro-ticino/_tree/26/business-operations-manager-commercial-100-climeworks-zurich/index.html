Descrizione completa

Build the commercial operating engine that enables Climeworks Solutions to scale and become the #1 portfolio manager in the carbon market.

You will turn complexity into simple, effective ways of working, connect processes, systems and data, and create the transparency teams need to make better decisions. Y ou will help shape and scale digital products such as our customer platform for managing carbon removal portfolios – bringing greater transparency and impact visibility to the market.

You will enable Marketing, Sales and Customer Success teams to work seamlessly together, serve a growing customer base, and deliver greater value to our clients.

  • Business analysis & solution design: You structure complex business problems, uncover root causes, and turn them into practical solutions that improve how the commercial organization operates. You align stakeholders, translate business needs into clear requirements, and prioritise improvements based on business impact and feasibility.
  • Process & operational excellence: You continuously improve commercial processes, using AI, automation, and digital solutions where relevant, to remove complexity and inefficiencies and to scale how we work. You partner with commercial teams to ensure improvements are adopted and continuously refined.
  • Analytics, reporting & data quality: You turn commercial data into actionable insights, developing reports and dashboards that help teams understand performance and identify opportunities for improvement. You conduct business analyses and drive data quality and reliability through effective controls and improvement measures.
  • CRM & commercial tools: You drive improvements to Salesforce and our broader commercial toolset, working closely with our technical teams and external implementation partners to bring solutions to life. You remain close to the systems and users, ensuring that technology effectively supports our processes across marketing, sales, and customer success and evolves with the business needs.
  • Knowledge management: You develop and maintain an internal knowledge base of processes, tools and materials, making it easy for commercial teams to access and apply best practices.

Requirements

  • Education & experience: You hold a university degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Sustainability, or a related field and bring 4-10 years of experience in commercial operations or business transformation, ideally across both consulting and in-house environments.
  • Commercial operations & business analysis: You have hands-on experience in business analysis, revenue operations, commercial excellence, or business transformation, ideally across marketing, sales, and customer success. You are able to take business challenges from diagnosis through to implementation: understanding root causes, redesigning processes, translating business needs into clear requirements and practical solutions, and helping teams adopt new ways of working.
  • CRM & business systems: You have a track record in enhancing Salesforce or another CRM, bridging between business and technical teams. You are comfortable contributing to solution design and steering technical partners through implementation, testing, and roll-out. Experience with Salesforce administration is a plus, as is exposure to marketing automation tools and ERPs such as SAP.
  • Data analytics: You are comfortable working with imperfect data, structuring analyses, creating reports and dashboards in CRM or Microsoft tools, and extracting business insights. Experience with Power BI, or comparable analytics tools is a plus.
  • Stakeholder management: You build credibility with stakeholders across functions and seniority levels and enjoy partnering with others to solve business problems.
  • Mindset & ownership: You bring strong business judgement and a high degree of ownership. You do what it takes to move things forward, whether that means shaping the bigger picture or getting into the details and tackling the work yourself. You are pragmatic, with a bias for action and continuous improvement. You know how to balance scalability and speed in a fast-changing environment.
  • Language: You have excellent written and spoken English skills.

Our promise: At Climeworks, you are accepted for who you are, and we are committed to building an inclusive environment where everyone feels valued.   • Ownership: Fair base pay paired with an equity package – because your contributions matter.
