About the job We are seeking a highly driven, organized, and entrepreneurial USAT Institutional Associate to accelerate market adoption, operational connectivity, and execution across core global corridors. As capacity expands across our pipeline, this role will serve as the execution engine for regional expansion initiatives, cross border pilots, and institutional onboarding. Reporting directly to the Head of USAT Institutional, you will take hands on ownership of high priority operational workflows, bridge regional expansion teams, and project manage strategic corridor partnerships from inception to production. The ideal candidate thrives in fast paced environments, possesses a strong baseline in fintech and digital asset execution, and is eager to be directly mentored while driving high impact output.

  • Education & Experience: Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, or a related field. 3–6 years of experience in project management, business development, or operational execution within fintech, cross-border payments, banking, or digital assets.
