Descrizione completa

Fincons Group is an IT business consulting company that has been designing the digital future of leading companies on international markets for 40 years. Fincons Group built its reputation on foreseeing and interpreting new business models and the rapid evolution of IT systems by building a complete range of services: from research and consulting to design and development, from system integration of leading vendor software solutions right up to application management, supporting clients step by step along their digital transformation journey. Fincons is a multinational with over 2,600 people and several offices worldwide (in Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Belgium and the USA), but above all, a Family Company where the founders play a strategic role with commitment and passion, grounding the company in the same principles of a united and caring family. We firmly believe in the value of cooperation and in the contribution that every idea and intuition can bring. We believe that everyone’s effort can make a difference. Transforming knowledge into a strategic resource is our company mission, and we pursue it with the help of our most strategic asset: our people. Job Overview We are seeking a proactive, quality-focused Tech Lead Mobile Developer with a solid understanding of the mobile development lifecycle and modern app architecture. In this role, you will act as a bridge between business stakeholders, product teams and software engineers, helping translate business needs into high-quality mobile solutions. While maintaining a strong technical involvement in the development process, you will also contribute to requirements gathering, solution design, release planning and stakeholder management You will be responsible for writing clean, scalable, and maintainable code and collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver robust mobile applications. Key Responsibilities

  • Gather, analyse and translate business requirements into functional specifications, user stories and acceptance criteria
  • Act as the primary point of contact between business stakeholders, product teams and development teams
  • Facilitate workshops and requirement discovery sessions with clients and internal stakeholders
  • Support solution design activities, ensuring alignment between business objectives and technical implementation
  • Contribute to the design and development of mobile applications based on Kotlin Multiplatform and Kotlin Native
  • Review technical solutions and provide guidance on architecture, scalability, maintainability and performance
  • Collaborate with architects, developers, QA engineers and project managers throughout the software development lifecycle
  • Contribute to release planning, feature prioritization and roadmap activities
  • Ensure high standards of quality, security and performance across mobile solutions
  • Produce and maintain clear functional and technical documentation
  • Support testing activities and participate in defect analysis and resolution
  • Promote best practices related to software architecture, development processes and Agile methodologies

Required Qualifications and Skills

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or equivalent professional experience
  • At least 5 years of experience in software engineering, business analysis or technical consulting roles
  • Strong experience gathering requirements and collaborating with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • Proven ability to translate business needs into functional and technical specifications
  • Strong experience developing mobile applications for Android and iOS platforms
  • Solid software engineering background with Java and Kotlin
  • Hands-on experience with Kotlin Multiplatform and Kotlin Native
  • Experience with modern mobile development frameworks and architectural patterns, including MVVM, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines and Flow
  • Experience integrating REST APIs and backend services
  • Good understanding of software architecture, design patterns and software development best practices
  • Knowledge of mobile application security, performance optimization and responsive user interface design
  • Excellent communication, presentation and stakeholder management skills
  • Professional working proficiency in both English and Italian

Location Requirement

  • Please note that a fully remote work policy is not offered for this role
  • Candidates should be prepared to relocate to Canton Ticino/Switzerland or towns near the Swiss-Italian border
