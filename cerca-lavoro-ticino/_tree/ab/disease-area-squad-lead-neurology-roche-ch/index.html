The Position The Opportunity The Disease Area Squad Lead as an agnostic steward of resources, leverages the expertise across the network to ensure integrated disease area outcomes are achieved through prioritization of business outcomes, optimising resource allocation and seizing opportunities to deliver on evolving DA needs and enable triple win outcomes.
