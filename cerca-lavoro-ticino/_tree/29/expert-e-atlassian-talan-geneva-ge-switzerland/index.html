Description de l'emploi

Role Summary We are seeking an experienced JIRA / Confluence Solution Architect to design, implement, govern, and optimize enterprise Atlassian solutions across Jira, Confluence, Jira Service Management, and related Atlassian Marketplace applications. The successful candidate will partner with IT and Business stakeholders to translate business needs into scalable Atlassian platform solutions. This role requires strong hands-on expertise in Atlassian configuration, workflow design, integrations, automation, security, migration, and enterprise governance. The Solution Architect will act as a trusted advisor, helping teams improve collaboration, service management, software delivery, knowledge management, and reporting through the Atlassian ecosystem. Key Responsibilities

  • Design and architect Atlassian solutions using Jira, Confluence, Jira Service Management, and related Atlassian products for enterprise use cases such as ITSM, SDLC, Agile delivery, knowledge management, and business workflow automation
  • Lead requirements discovery workshops with stakeholders across IT and business teams to understand current-state processes and define future-state workflows
  • Configure and govern Jira projects , including workflows, issue types, custom fields, screens, screen schemes, permission schemes, notification schemes, boards, dashboards, and automation rules
  • Design Confluence information architecture , including spaces, page templates, permissions, knowledge bases, documentation standards, and content governance
  • Develop scalable workflow and process designs that support Agile, Scrum, Kanban, DevOps, ITSM, ITAM, ITOM, and enterprise service management practices
  • Integrate Atlassian tools with enterprise systems , such as Microsoft 365, identity providers, CI/CD tools, reporting platforms, and other business applications
  • Advise on identity, access, and security architecture , including SSO, role-based access control, and data governance
  • Lead Jira and Confluence Cloud migrations including data cleanup activities, customization refactoring and add-ons compatibility management
  • Evaluate, configure, and govern Marketplace apps , such as ScriptRunner, Xray, Tempo, and other plugins used to extend Atlassian capabilities
  • Create solution architecture artifacts , including architecture diagrams, design documents, configuration standards and implementation roadmaps
  • Provide best-practice guidance to avoid common configuration, scalability, performance, and governance pitfalls
  • Support adoption and change management through training, enablement materials, stakeholder communications, and success metrics
  • Mentor Atlassian platform team on platform standards, configuration patterns, and operational support practices

Required Qualifications

  • 8+ years of hands-on Atlassian experience , especially with Jira, Confluence, and Jira Service Management
  • Strong experience designing and implementing Atlassian solutions for ITSM, SDLC, Agile delivery or enterprise workflow management
  • Deep knowledge of Jira configuration , including workflows, schemes, custom fields, boards, dashboards, automation, permissions, and notifications
  • Strong knowledge of Confluence administration and design , including spaces, templates, permissions, knowledge bases, and documentation governance
  • Experience integrating Atlassian tools with external enterprise applications such as Microsoft 365, identity providers, CI/CD tools, and reporting platforms
  • Experience with Atlassian Marketplace apps such as ScriptRunner, XRay, Tempo , or similar tools
  • Understanding of SSO, access control, and enterprise security concepts
  • Experience with Jira/JSM/Confluence Cloud migrations
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to engage technical teams, business users, and senior leadership
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to design solutions where no standard pattern exists

Soft Skills

  • Ability to rally people around solutions
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management
  • Ability to translate functional requirements into technical architecture
  • Strong documentation and presentation skills
  • Comfortable leading workshops, discovery sessions, and design reviews
  • Strong problem-solving mindset and ability to adapt to complex or ambiguous requirements.
