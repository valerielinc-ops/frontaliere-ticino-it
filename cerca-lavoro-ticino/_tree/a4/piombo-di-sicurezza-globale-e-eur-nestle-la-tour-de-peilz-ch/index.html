Location: La Tour-de-Peilz, Switzerland Work model: Full-Time Act. Rate 100% Contract type / duration: Permanent contract Together, we make breakfast better. As a Global & EUR Safety Lead at CPW, your mission is to lead and strengthen our Safety, Health and Environment strategy across our factories. You will make a difference by working closely with local SHE teams, factory leadership, and internal and external stakeholders. This is your opportunity to make breakfast better and help people start their day in the best possible way. How you’ll make an impact as a Global & EUR Safety Lead

  • Embed safety as a core leadership value by strengthening accountability and ensuring SHE is fully integrated into daily decision-making and operational routines.
  • Drive SHE cultural transformation at shopfloor level by elevating frontline leadership behaviours through targeted coaching, and impactful communication.
  • Deploy global SHE standards across CPW factories, ensuring consistency while enabling local ownership and high-performing safety cultures.
  • Strengthen risk management and resilience by driving robust audit frameworks and proactive prevention plans to reduce operational risks.
  • Build sustainable capability by developing factory leaders and SHE professionals, reinforcing expertise, mindset and long-term compliance.
  • Accelerate continuous improvement by identifying, sharing and scaling best practices and innovative solutions across sites.
  • Influence business performance by partnering cross-functionally to elevate SHE outcomes and support safe, sustainable operations. What will make you successful
  • Bringing proven experience in SHE within complex industrial environments, with a strong track record in driving safety culture transformation; hands-on leadership experience at shopfloor level is a strong advantage.
  • Strong expertise in SHE management systems, auditing and risk management, complemented by recognized safety certifications (e.g., NEBOSH, ISO Lead Auditor or equivalent).
  • Highly effective stakeholder influencer, able to engage, challenge and align leaders across functions, geographies and levels of the organization.
  • Clear and impactful communicator, fluent in English (French is a plus), capable of connecting with audiences from shopfloor teams to senior leadership.
