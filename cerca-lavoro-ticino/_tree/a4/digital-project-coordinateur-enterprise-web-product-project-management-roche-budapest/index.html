As a member of the Global Digital Hub Team, you will work to ensure fulfillment of the Global Digital Hub long term outcomes and strategy, as well as excellent delivery of our various digital operations priorities. You are responsible for supporting and executing different initiatives. You work with relevant stakeholders and consult on how to develop their digital performance further and ensure they leverage the available digital tools in order to meet their overall goals. A Digital Project Coordinator (Enterprise Web Product Project Management) is a technically-qualified person (expert) who strives to achieve business goals through web presence and digital platforms by supporting stakeholders with providing hands-on delivery coordination and consultative advisory to local markets and global teams. The Digital Project Coordinator (Enterprise Web Product Project Management) is responsible for consulting and partnering with Roche Divisions and/or affiliates in executing the digital strategy by delivering a full suite of objectives across the Roche online presence. The Digital Project Coordinator (Enterprise Web Product Project Management) is a skilled expert in all aspects of digital project management, content management, and tool development across different platforms, and can build out reporting metrics that support the business in continuing their growth to meet KPIs and objectives.
