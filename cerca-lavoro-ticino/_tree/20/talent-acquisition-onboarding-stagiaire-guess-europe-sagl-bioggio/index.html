Descrizione completa

The Talent Acquisition & Onboarding Intern will support the Talent team throughout the recruitment lifecycle, contributing to the identification and engagement of talents aligned with GUESS’ business needs, values, and culture.

The role will provide hands-on exposure to different stages of the recruitment process (especially for internship positions), from job posting to interview coordination and offer preparation, while also supporting selected onboarding activities to ensure a smooth experience for new joiners.

CORE JOB RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Genuine interest in Talent management and eagerness to learn and develop within the HR field
  • Good organizational skills, attention to detail, and ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities
  • Proactive, flexible, and hands-on approach
  • Discretion and reliability when handling confidential and sensitive information
