Descrizione completa

Become part of a vital chain and contribute to our common goal of making people’s lives better. Headquartered in Lachen, Switzerland, Octapharma is one of the largest human protein manufacturers in the world, developing and producing medicines sourced from human plasma and human cell lines. We are a privately-owned company, where the warmth of family meets the scale of a global organisation.

Join us in shaping our vision to provide new health solutions advancing human life.

Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the medical content of global marketing materials and communication elements.
  • You contribute to the international opinion leader communication and development.
  • You contribute to congresses / symposia by strategy planning and content development and to international and local marketing activities.
  • You contribute to planning and execution of assigned clinical projects over the range of the portfolio.
  • You are responsible for internal / external medical training.
  • You collect customer insights and derive activities to adequately address identified unmet needs.
  • You contribute to clinical life science management projects and to new product development projects.

Your expertise and ideal skill set

  • You have a medical science / life science background (university degree, MD/PhD is desired),
  • You have previous 1-3 years of local or regional working experience in a related function and want to develop into a global responsibility.
  • You have been working on successful projects in market launch, medical communication, key opinion leader management, clinical initiatives, medical marketing, and sales and marketing support.
  • You are used to working diligently with scientific information and clinical data to translate this into creative clinical projects and compelling communication.
  • You are a hands-on person used to work in specialized teams on strategic projects and know how to convince and to motivate your internal and external teams.
  • You are a strong team player, internationally oriented and willing to travel (approximately 25%).

Your department - where you make an impact

  • The International Business Unit department (IBU) is divided into three Business Units: IBU Immunotherapy, IBU Haematology and IBU Critical Care plus an IBU Project Management team
  • The overall responsibility of the IBU department is to ensure long-term strategic development by enabling intelligent business decisions
  • We specialize in global medical marketing, strategic and business development, as well as pipeline project management
  • The department consists of 25 team members.

  • Work with skilled and fun colleagues in a relatively informal organization
