Descrizione completa

Location: Vevey, Switzerland Contract type / duration: Internship – 6 months Start date: February/ March 2027

Be a force for good and transform your potential into something extraordinary.

As an Environmental Sustainability Intern within the Engineering & Environmental team , your mission will be to support the development of environmental monitoring, analysis and reporting processes, contributing to Nestlé’s environmental sustainability ambitions.

You will work across sustainability data, environmental reporting, digital tools and technical guidance. You will help the team monitor performance, create meaningful KPIs and strengthen the tools and evidence used to demonstrate progress in areas such as renewable electricity, energy productivity, water and greenhouse gas emissions.

This is an opportunity to gain hands-on experience with international sustainability reporting frameworks and collaborate with Markets, Zones and factories while developing your analytical, technical and stakeholder-management skills.

How you’ll make an impact as an Environmental Sustainability Intern

  • Monitor and compile environmental KPIs (energy, water, greenhouse gas emissions) and support internal and external reporting
  • Prepare performance reports that turn complex data into clear insights
  • Run gap analyses on reporting and digital tools and propose improvements to documents, processes and tools
  • Update technical guidance, analyse emerging sustainability frameworks and contribute to best practices for Markets and factories
  • Support the administration of reporting systems and internal websites, and create engaging sustainability learning content

What will make you successful

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s student, or recent graduate, in Environmental Science, Engineering or a related field, with a genuine interest in sustainability
  • Strong analytical skills and confidence working with environmental data and KPIs, with good knowledge of Microsoft Excel, Word and PowerPoint
  • Digitally confident and comfortable learning new reporting tools and systems, with strong attention to detail, organisation and time-management skills
  • Ability to communicate findings clearly to different stakeholders; proactive, curious and collaborative, proposing practical improvements and delivering high-quality work
  • Fluency in written and spoken English
