The Position

  • Your key responsibilities
  • ● Supply &Demand Planning :
  • Manage supply planning parameters and monitor inventory levels to balance supply with demand.
  • Lead regular SOP meetings with Sales and Marketing to review forecasts and mitigate risks such as stock-outs or excess inventory.
  • ● Order & Distributor Management:
  • Oversee order fulfillment for local distributors and manage end-to-end order-to-cash (OTC) processes.
  • Act as the primary contact for 3PL warehouse operations to coordinate inbound/outbound schedules and ensure accurate logistics flow.
  • ● Inventory & Data Management :
  • Oversee the material lifecycle by maintaining accurate master data in SAP. Monitor inventory health by analyzing slow-moving, short-expiry, and obsolete stock to optimize working capital.
  • ● Logistics Quality & Claim Handling:
  • Manage logistics exceptions by filing claims for shipping damages, short shipments, or temperature excursions (cold chain deviations).
  • Coordinate with global vendors and insurers to resolve discrepancies and minimize supply disruptions.
  • ● Procurement & Reporting:
  • Execute purchase orders (PO) and monitor shipping schedules from global hubs. Prepare SCM KPI reports, including forecast accuracy, service levels (OTIF), and inventory turnover.

Who you are

  • Education & Qualification
  • ● A Bachelor or Master Degrees in Logistics or Logistics related subjects
  • ● Proficiency in both spoken and written English
  • ● Good command of MS office software application
  • ● Familiar with SAP system in MM and SD modules

Experience & Competencies

  • ● A minimum over 3 years’ experience in supply chain operations & distribution.
  • ● SAP experience in operation at least 1 years
  • ● MS operation / Presentation skill
  • ● Planning, Organizing & Commitment
  • ● Customer Oriented & Relationship Management
  • ● Communication Skill
