Descrizione completa

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role Sulzer is a top employer and one of the world's leading pump manufacturers. Sulzer provides a wide range of products for pumping solutions for water, oil and gas, power, and chemicals industries. Due to continued growth, we are looking for a Product Development Engineer for the Technology Center in Thimister/Belgium. This is a fantastic opportunity for you to focus on the development and standardization of products with our teams globally. As the Product Development Engineer, you will be given the opportunity to work on innovative projects ranging from design activities to product enhancement activities. If you are talented Mechanical Engineer who is enthusiastic, self-motivated and have a passion to produce high quality engineering, we would love to hear from you! We offer you interesting and challenging tasks in a global, multi-cultural environment. This is your chance to join a leading organization, contributing to a cutting-edge initiative, and make a real impact on the future success of Sulzer. Location: We are based in Thimister-Clermont, Belgium. Your main tasks and responsibilities:

  • Responsible for the Product Development project activities and for achieving the project objectives.
  • Design activities such as the creation of design studies, master assemblies and detailed drawings, including related design calculations.
  • Coordinate detailed design drawing activities globally.
  • Standardize key design features for the product range.
  • Create and maintain product documentation such as design catalogues, hydraulic performance curves, product manuals and tools.
  • Utilize current technologies, develop tools and methods to control designs.
  • Effective communication with manufacturing entities and Product Management global organization to ensure a collaborative approach. To succeed in this role, you will need:
  • A degree in Mechanical Engineering with 4+ years’ industry experience
  • Fluency in English and French
  • Strong collaboration skills within a global, multicultural environment
  • Proficiency in 2D and 3D CAD tools (e.g. NX, SolidWorks)
  • A customer-focused mindset with the ability to translate technical needs into solutions
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail What we offer you
  • Continuous development opportunities, with full access to the Sulzer Learning Platform to support your personal and professional growth
  • 36 days of annual leave
  • A comprehensive pension and group insurance scheme designed to support your long-term security
  • Private health insurance for you and your family
  • Meal vouchers daily
  • Global career development opportunities, with exposure to international projects and mobility across the Sulzer network
  • The chance to work in an innovative, agile, and vibrant culture where your ideas and impact truly matter Have a question about the role?
