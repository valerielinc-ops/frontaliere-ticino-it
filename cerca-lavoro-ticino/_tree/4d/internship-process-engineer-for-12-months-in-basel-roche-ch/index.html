The Position The Process Validation Chapter is a part of the Basel Drug Substance Manufacturing organization and is responsible for supporting the validation of the production process during routine commercial manufacturing, technology transfers, process optimizations and for leading implementation of new technologies to drive manufacturing efficiencies. This internship position is located in Basel, Switzerland.

The Opportunity

  • You will contribute to process validation strategies, execution, and projects to drive operational success across manufacturing.
  • Supporting technical process technology transfers enables you to play a key role in transitioning processes from development into commercial-scale manufacturing.
  • You will manage end-to-end validation deliverables, spanning conceptual work, plan creation, training implementation, sample coordination, data interpretation, and report preparation.
  • Cross-functional troubleshooting activities will be planned and coordinated by you in close collaboration with pilot laboratories, production, and the quality department.
  • You will process deviations and changes in full compliance with GMP guidelines.
  • Active involvement in optimization projects will allow you to drive continuous improvement, efficiency, and process robustness.

Who You Are

  • You are currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program, or have completed your degree within the last 12 months prior to the start date, in a scientific or technical field—preferably in Biotechnology, Bioprocess engineering, or (bio)Chemical engineering, Pharmacy, Biology.
  • Practical experience or solid knowledge of working within a regulated GMP environment will be a core foundation of your qualifications.
  • You will bring a collaborative mindset as an independent team player who thrives in a dynamic environment and enjoys partnering across diverse interfaces.
  • Adapting quickly to a fast-paced setting will enable you to manage shifting priorities effectively and respond with agility to operational requests.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills will allow you to articulate complex technical topics clearly and build productive working relationships.
  • Fluency in English is required to collaborate and communicate clearly within an international, cross-functional team. Conversational German skills are considered an asset.

Additional Information

  • Start Date & Duration: The preferred start date for the internship is in early 2027 with a duration of 12 months (or upon mutual agreement).

Application Process

  • Please upload your CV (in English), a motivation letter with your application, and a certificate of enrollment (if you are currently studying) or your degree certificate (if you have already completed your studies).
  • Non-EU/Non-EFTA citizens must attach an official confirmation from your university stating that a mandatory internship is an integral part of your curriculum, and you must remain continuously enrolled throughout the entire duration of this internship.
