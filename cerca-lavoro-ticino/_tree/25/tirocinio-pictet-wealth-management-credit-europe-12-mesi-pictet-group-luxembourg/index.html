Descrizione completa

Your team The Pictet Group is one of the world’s leading independent wealth and asset managers. Founded in 1805 and headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, the Group is represented at 30 offices in financial centres worldwide, and currently employs over 5200 people. Pictet has been present since 1989 in Luxembourg, where it employs over 700 people. Pictet Wealth Management combines more than 200 years of Swiss banking heritage with global investment expertise. The Partner-owned financial service group offers a comprehensive service for wealthy individuals and families including discretionary and advisory investment solutions and family office services. We are looking for a long-term intern to join our Private Wealth Management Investement & Wealth Solutions department. You will be responsible to provide support to credit managers in their daily work, for all branches of BPAG and from an operational perspective.

  • Start date: ASAP
  • Duration: 12 months
  • Employment rate: part-time
  • Prerequisite: a tripartite internship agreement signed with your school Your role
  • Support in the preparation of regular reports for the attention of the Front Office and Management (past due credit limits, upcoming credit limits maturities, Missing Documentation).
  • Perform manual monitoring of credit structures and collateral requirements (“special monitoring” situations that cannot be handled automatically).
  • Handling of standard operational tasks such as credit card guarantees approval and booking, approval on repurchase requests on insurance policies, manual update of interest conditions on client’s current accounts etc.
  • Monitor the conditions set when the credit request is approved.
  • Draft simple credit documentation for standard cases (Luxembourg) and verify documentation prepared by other branches.
  • Tracking and follow-up of overdue credits. Your profile Qualifications and experience:

  • First internship in a similar role. Specific knowledge:
  • Sound knowledge of credit products and related risks.

  • Detail-oriented with an analytical mindset.
