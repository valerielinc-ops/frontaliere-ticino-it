Descrizione completa

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

Team Manager – Industrial Software Research

__

As a Research Scientist in Operational Technology (OT) Cybersecurity, you will shape the future security architecture and design of ABB's software-based products, with a focus on protecting industrial systems in an increasingly connected world. We are looking for a team member with a proven research track record and a drive to translate cutting-edge academic work into real-world industrial environment. You will join a highly motivated, international team of scientists at ABB Research, tackling complex challenges facing digital capabilities within critical infrastructure today. • We offer an open, collaborative environment with broad opportunities to grow your expertise and advance your career at the intersection of research and industry.

The work model for the role is: Hybrid

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Contribute to and/or lead research projects that turn state-of-the-art research results into effective software solutions for ABB

  • Create prototypes of innovative concepts with real business impact

  • Evaluate new technologies and assess their impact on ABB products and roadmaps

  • Disseminate your results in scientific publications, patent applications, and technical reports

    Build a network in the company and actively disseminate your knowledge and expertise



• Qualifications for the role:

  • PhD in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field

  • Hands-on experience in software engineering and system design

  • Deep theoretical and practical knowledge in one or more of the following areas: OT security, computer security, distributed systems, embedded systems, secure communications, or applied cryptography

  • Proficiency in programming languages such as C/C++, Python, or Rust

  • Proven publication track record at top-tier scientific venues

  • Proven ability to drive ideas from concept to working prototype

  • Excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset across multidisciplinary teams


More about us:

At the ABB research center located in Baden-Dättwil in the proximity of Zurich, we work on cutting-edge technologies that shape the future of our products. The Industrial Software Systems research team explores topics like machine learning, cybersecurity, computer networking, distributed systems, and real-time systems. Our solutions are used to support the development of next-generation digital products and services of ABB.

This recruitment process is led by Pedro Labian Sanchez, Talent Acquisition Partner at ABB Switzerland Ltd.
