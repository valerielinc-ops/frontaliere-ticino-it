Descrizione completa

It's more than a job

In this IT role, you will manage, develop, and support the technology infrastructure that powers Kuehne+Nagel’s global systems and operations. This contribution is vital to making both special and ordinary moments in the lives of people a reality. In fact, your work enables our teams to deliver fresh fruit, time-sensitive healthcare products and much more to people all around the world. Working in IT at Kuehne+Nagel contributes to more than we imagine.

‎

As an IT Supporter (m/w/d), you will provide first-level technical support to employees, helping resolve issues with hardware, software, applications, and IT services. You will ensure the smooth operation of local IT systems and infrastructure while delivering efficient, customer-focused support to maintain a high level of service.

How you create impact

  • Provide technical support for desktops, laptops, and Macs, including hardware and software issues.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve issues with Windows 10, Windows 11, and Windows Server.
  • Perform basic network troubleshooting and support printers and other peripheral devices.
  • Support Microsoft 365 applications and business-critical software.
  • Manage user accounts, permissions, and policies using Active Directory and Group Policy.
  • Set up, support, and troubleshoot iOS and Android devices.
  • Install, upgrade, and maintain hardware components such as memory and storage drives.
  • Record, track, and resolve incidents and service requests using ticketing systems while ensuring a high level of customer satisfaction.

What we would like you to bring

  • 2+ years of experience in IT support, help desk, or service desk roles.
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks effectively.
  • Ability to explain technical concepts clearly to non-technical users.
  • Customer-focused mindset with excellent service skills.
  • Ability to work both independently and as part of a team.
  • Fluent in German and English, both written and spoken.

What's in it for you

At Kuehne+Nagel, you will have the opportunity to grow your expertise, shape processes, and deliver innovative solutions for some of the world’s largest customers. We are continuously expanding our global network and product portfolio, creating career opportunities across diverse functions and regions. As a leader in the logistics industry, we offer a collaborative, digital, and customer-focused environment where you will work with motivated colleagues from around the world. We look forward to receiving your comprehensive online application.

Who we are

Logistics shapes everyday life - from the goods we consume to the healthcare we rely on. At Kuehne+Nagel, your work goes beyond logistics; it enables both ordinary and special moments in the lives of people around the world.

As a global leader with a strong heritage and a vision to move the world forward, we offer a safe, stable environment where your career can make a real difference. Whether we help deliver life-saving medicines, develop sustainable transportation solutions or support our local communities, your career will contribute to more than you can imagine.
