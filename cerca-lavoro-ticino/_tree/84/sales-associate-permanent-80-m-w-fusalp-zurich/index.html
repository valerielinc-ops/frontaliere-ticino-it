About Join Fusalp, own your journey

Born in the French Alps in 1952, Fusalp has built its identity on a distinctive vision: bringing together the precision of French tailoring, technical innovation and the freedom of movement. From its origins in skiwear to its international development, the brand continues to evolve while remaining true to the expertise and pionerring spirit that shaped its beginnings.

At the heart of Fusalp are the people who bring the brand to life every day. We believe that excellence is achieved through teamwork, mutual trust and a shared ambition to succeed. Every team member plays an essential role in representing Fusalp, building meaningful client relationships and contributing to its continued success.

Our culture encourages initiative, accountability and continuous learning. Working alongside experienced retail professionals, you will be supported in developing your expertise, encouraged to ask questions, take initiative and understand the business, while being entrusted with responsibilities that reflect your role, experience and development.

As Fusalp continues to grow, we believe in growing our people alongside the House. We value commitment, recognize individual and collective achievements, and encourage those who demonstrate ambition and ability to take on greater responsibilities.

We will challenge you. We will support you. We will give you room to prove yourself.

Bring your energy. Built your expertise. Own your journey.

Job Description Your next move could start here

For our Zurich boutique, we are looking for a German- and English-speaking Sales Associate who genuinely enjoys meeting people, understanding their expectations and building relationships that go beyond a single purchase.

Not every client walks into a boutique knowing exactly what they want.

Some are discovering Fusalp for the first time. Others know our collections and expect you to understand their preferences. Your role is to listen, ask the right questions and offer advice that makes sense for each individual.

You may already have experience in premium retail, or you may still be developing your expertise. What matters to us is your curiosity, your commercial instinct and your willingness to learn.

We value people who know how to listen, enjoy the challenge of selling and understand that trust is what brings clients back.

Your role as sales associate

  • Make every client count. Listen, connect, advise and turn great conversations into sales and lasting relationships.
  • Know what you sell. Develop your Fusalp product expertise and bring our collections to life through genuine, confident advice.
  • Be part of the team. Contribute to sales objectives, boutique operations and the little things that make a big difference every day.

At Fusalp, everyone contributes to the success of the boutique, whatever the task.

Preferred Experience What will help you succeed

Do you know how to turn attentive listening into meaningful sales? Are you comfortable working towards ambitious objectives, taking initiative and building the trust of both clients and colleagues?

Must-Have

  • Proven retail sales experience and strong commercial skills.
  • Ability to build client relationships and work within a structured team environment.
  • German and English.

Nice-to-Have

  • Experience in premium or luxury retail.
  • Clienteling, CRM and KPI knowledge.
  • Interest in developing towards an Assistant Store Manager position.

Why you'll love working here

  • A permanent position in our Zurich boutique, at 80% (33.6 hours/week).
  • CHF 4,500 gross per month (CHF 54,000 annually, paid over 12 months), plus a performance-based bonus to reward your contribution to the boutique's success.
  • Exclusive staff discounts on Fusalp collections, so you can experience, wear and share the products you represent.
  • Learn from people who know their craft. Benefit from product training, commercial coaching and the experience of retail professionals who are there to help you grow.
  • A real opportunity to take your career further. Develop your commercial expertise, take on meaningful responsibilities and potentially progress towards an Assistant Store Manager position as your skills and experience grow, subject to business opportunities.

Please note: Employee accommodation is not provided for this position.
