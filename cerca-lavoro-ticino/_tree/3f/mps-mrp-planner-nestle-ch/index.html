A Day in the Life of a MRP & MPS Planner

  • Build a feasible 18-month production plan based on dispatch plan requirements and agreed stock cover policies.
  • Work hand in hand with our suppliers and provide regular follow-ups and updates.
  • Anticipate potential issues and opportunities for factories within the 18-month horizon such as managing phase-in and phase-out processes.
  • Conduct monthly production review meetings with factories to review performance and consolidate constraints.
  • Update the production plan weekly, considering production constraints and market dispatch, to meet stock cover objectives.
  • Manage exceptions and propose solutions to avoid any issues in collaboration with supply planners.
  • Ensure successful product launches and phase-outs by maintaining retro-planning and tracking stock depletion.
  • Coordinate with suppliers to align capacity and forecast requirements and manage packaging changes or crises.
  • Drive cost efficiency by challenging existing planning processes, implementing improvements, and optimizing inventory levels. What will make you successful
  • Proven experience in factory planning, supply planning, purchasing, or supply chain development.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a focus on detail and accuracy.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration abilities to work effectively with cross-functional teams and suppliers.
  • Proficiency in planning systems and tools (experience with OMP tool is a plus), with the ability to maintain master data accurately.
  • Knowledge of inventory management principles and experience in optimizing stock levels.
  • Ability to challenge existing processes, drive improvements, and monitor requirements evolution.
  • Strong organizational skills to manage multiple tasks and prioritize effectively.
