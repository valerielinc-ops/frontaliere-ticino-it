As our security agent you will

  • Ensure the safety and security of people and property.
  • Monitor and enforce policies and procedures regarding individuals suspected of engaging in prostitution.
  • Monitor and address incidents of public intoxication.
  • Maintain a visible presence throughout all public areas to prevent any disturbances.
  • Perform the duties of a Fire Safety Officer.
  • Conduct fire safety and security patrols.
  • Report and document any malfunctions or irregularities.
  • Comply with Marriott procedures and ensure their proper implementation.
  • Respond to injuries involving employees or guests.
  • Participate in the drafting of incident reports.
  • Report all incidents systematically to the Night Manager and the Security Team Leader.
  • Provide the department manager with a summary of all night-time events at the end of each shift.
  • Ensure the safety and security of hotel guests and Talents (employees).
  • Monitor access to the building and notify management of any suspicious or inappropriate behavior.
