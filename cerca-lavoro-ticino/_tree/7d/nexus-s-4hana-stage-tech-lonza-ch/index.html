Nexus Internship Location: Visp, Switzerland Full Time, On Site. The role focuses on identifying business-relevant SAP AI use cases, assessing infrastructure readiness, and contributing to the design and development of a custom AI agent (“Customer Project Builder”) that automates the conversion of customer contract data into structured SAP project data. What you will get:

  • 3 month internship working for a global organization
  • Hands-on experience with a wide range of tools and implementing artificial intelligence capabilities
  • A supportive team environment with training, coaching and clear development pathways. What you will do:
  • Analyze and evaluate SAP AI technologies, including SAP Joule Co-Pilot and SAP Business AI capabilities
  • Assess current SAP BTP landscape and identify infrastructure gaps for AI enablement
  • Collaborate with Nexus IT and business stakeholders to identify and prioritize AI use cases
  • Support development of SAP AI strategy recommendations and implementation roadmap
  • Support the design and develop a custom AI agent (“Customer Project Builder”) for document-to-data automation What we are looking for
  • Availability for full-time internship during the defined period
  • Interest in working in a global, highly regulated industry (pharma/CDMO)
  • Willingness to work in Visp and collaborate across locations
