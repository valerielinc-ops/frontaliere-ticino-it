Descrizione completa

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position Job Purpose At Roche, we believe it’s urgent to deliver medical solutions right now – even as we develop innovations for the future. We are passionate about improving the lives of patients’ and are fearless in both decision and action. We believe that partnering with health systems around the world can create value for patients, society and for Roche. We are uniquely positioned to realize truly integrated health solutions across pharmaceuticals, diagnostics, digital health and big data solutions; positioning us as a key partner for personalized, sustainable healthcare everywhere. That is why we come to work each day. We commit ourselves to scientific rigor, unassailable ethics, and access to medical innovations for all. We do this today to build a better tomorrow. In the APAC Disease Area Network, Roche is committed to a bold new operating model that leverages our collective capabilities across the enterprise to deliver real impact for patients quickly around the globe. We are expected to pursue meaningful work across the system where our experience, perspectives and skills can have the biggest impact. By collaborating and coordinating our efforts as a network, we can deliver more for patients collectively.

As the Marketing Enabler, you aim to

  • Optimize the overall care experience for patients with cancer
  • Work with a complex set of internal and external stakeholders
  • Understand, develop, drive and monitor the customer value proposition for potential integrated health solutions
  • Work cross functionally to coordinate and steward the integrated marketing plan in the Disease Area (DA)
  • Work to catalyze rapid solutions to significant gaps in the patient journey (diagnostics, digital health solutions, treatments(s), distribution / supply) by connecting with enterprise knowledge and resources.
  • Work in cross border collaborations in Asia Pacific and will be a member of the Network capabilities despite being hired into a country affiliate.
  • Realize and scale country outcomes by contributing to virtual, cross border, cross functional work package teams
  • Jointly cultivate an environment that enables a self-managed team, with strong innovative, agile and enterprise mindset, customer centricity and a culture of discovery, experimentation and learning. Despite being hired into a country affiliate, this role will be a member of the APAC network model, working in cross border collaborations, to realize and scale country outcomes by contributing to virtual, cross border, cross functional work package team

Responsibility and Accountability As part of the Marketing capabilities Chapter, you will be hired into a country affiliate and will be responsible for the following:

  • Translating stakeholder, system and patient needs into a clear vision and valuable partnerships
  • Providing DA knowledge, system awareness, treatment landscape and product(s) expertise to help inform portfolio resourcing, prioritization and other commercial management tasks
  • Shaping, stewarding and contributing to core business processes (affiliate and beyond borders) to ensure the investment and resourcing needs in DA are well served
  • Building and contributing the capabilities to cross functional, cross cultural, self-managed, work package teams that apply agile methodologies to quickly deliver high value work
  • Assuming shared accountability for organizational launch preparedness within and across countries.
  • Serving as a cross-channel digital engagement expert, leading strategie
