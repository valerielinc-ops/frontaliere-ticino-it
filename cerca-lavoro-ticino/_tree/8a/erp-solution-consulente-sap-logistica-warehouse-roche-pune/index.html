The Position We are seeking an experienced SAP EWM Specialist who independently leads EWM solution delivery, proactively solves product issues, and contributes to the architecture for the global Roche ASPIRE EWM System. In this role, you will guide product developments, champion accountability, and effectively communicate with all stakeholders. You will solve complex problems, make independent product-level decisions, provide technical oversight, and report at the product level. By bringing advanced EWM knowledge, expertise within GxP environments, and strong analytical skills, you will actively contribute to continuous improvement and the evolution of our warehouse processes across Roche. Description of the area: Job Responsibilities

  • Scope: Works and leads independently to deliver solution design with increased complexity (e.g., technical, scope, stakeholders) in the EWM area that meet product requirements and business priorities. Proactively identifies issues at the product level, proposes ideas on how to tackle them, and keeps applying a continuous improvement mindset. Documents and evolves the architecture throughout the EWM product life cycle, and leads the development of medium to complex changes. Evaluates, pilots, and drives the adoption of new business processes while providing technical oversight and consultation. Independently makes decisions with impact at the product level under limited direction.
