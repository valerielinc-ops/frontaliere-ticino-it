Descrizione completa

Job Profiles Ecolodge Director The role of Ecolodge Director allows you to play a key part in ecotourism. Leading an environmentally responsible establishment, you ensure a balance between economic profitability, biodiversity conservation, and the involvement of local communities. Job Description: Ecolodge Director

  • Recruiting and training teams, prioritizing the local population, in order to reinforce sustainable and cultural development.
  • Administrative, budgetary, and logistical monitoring: Ensuring operational management, promoting cultural diversity, and limiting negative environmental effects.
  • Communication and awareness: Promoting the establishment, highlighting the transparency of ecological practices, and educating travelers on the protection of flora and fauna.

Skills and Personal Qualities Technical Skills

  • In-depth knowledge of sustainable development (eco-friendly materials, understanding of local traditions, administrative procedures).
  • Mastery of environmental standards and certifications (e.g., Green Globe label¹).
  • Aptitude in communication and marketing to promote the offering and educate clients.
  • Skills in hotel management and general management to oversee an establishment.

Human Qualities

  • Ecological and ethical sensitivity.
  • Leadership and the ability to unite a multicultural team.
  • Organization, rigor, and analytical mindset.
  • Cultural openness and diplomacy.
  • Entrepreneurial spirit and capacity for innovation.

Education and Qualifications

  • Training in hospitality and catering ( Bachelor's in International Hospitality , BTS [Higher Technician Certificate], Professional Licenses).
  • MBA in International Hospitality to acquire managerial and strategic expertise in responsible tourism.

Prior experience in the hotel industry or in the management of tourism structures is highly recommended . Working Conditions

  • Environment in direct connection with nature and local populations .
  • Flexible hours , often intense during high season.
  • High responsibility in ecological, economic, and social management .

Salary

  • Average salary: €2,500 to €4,000 gross per month, depending on the size, location, and management style (independent or group).

Career Progression

After a few years, the Ecolodge Director can advance to

  • Directing a larger establishment or one belonging to an ecotourism chain.
  • Progressing to a general director position in the sustainable hospitality sector.
  • Creating or developing their own ecotourism project.
