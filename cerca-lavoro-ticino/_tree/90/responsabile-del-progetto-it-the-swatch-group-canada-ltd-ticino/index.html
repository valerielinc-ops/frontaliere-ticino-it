IT Project Manager - Swatch Group

IT Project Manager The company The Swatch Group Ltd. in Biel (Switzerland) is the world’s largest manufacturer of finished watches. The Swatch Group is synonymous for durable, emotional consumer products of high quality. With its own worldwide network of distribution organizations, the Group is active in the manufacture of finished watches, movements and components (e.g. ETA). It produces practically all the components necessary to its watch companies. Job description You will join a large and collaborative IT organization and play a key role in managing strategic projects across the American subsidiaries of the Group. Working closely with business stakeholders, IT teams, enterprise architects and external partners, you will ensure the successful delivery of approved projects, from initial demand through handover to operations, while contributing to program management and supporting change initiatives.

Your main responsibilities include

  • Managing approved IT projects according to agreed budgets, timelines and scope.
  • Ensuring effective project execution from demand intake to operational handover, following the Group’s project management methodology.
  • Preparing and maintaining project documentation, investment requests and decision materials.
  • Coordinating with enterprise architects to ensure proper architectural alignment during conceptual phases.
  • Collaborating with external partners and ensuring proper follow‑up on deliverables.
  • Communicating regularly with stakeholders, including business teams, management, PMO and related project teams.
  • Supporting conflict resolution, resource coordination and scope management.
  • Contributing to program management activities and facilitating change management across impacted teams.
