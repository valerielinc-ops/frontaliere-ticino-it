The Position Primary Purpose of Job (Job Summary) The OE & BPM Lead within the Manufacturing Department is responsible for driving a culture of Continuous Improvement on the shop floor. By utilizing Lean Six Sigma methodologies and other optimization tools, this role identifies and eliminates waste, optimizes production business processes, and ensures the achievement of departmental KPIs. This position serves as both a catalyst for production efficiency and a critical bridge for cross-functional process synergy. Principle Roles & Responsibilities / Accountabilities 1. Lean Leadership & Project Management

  • Lead Core Projects: Spearhead major Lean improvement projects on the front line (e.g., OEE enhancement, SMED/changeover reduction, yield loss mitigation, etc.).
  • Capability Building: Design and implement internal Lean training systems; coach production supervisors and staff in applying Lean tools (5S, Kaizen, VSM, etc.).
  • Culture Promotion: Foster a "Continuous Improvement" atmosphere, ensuring Lean thinking is deeply embedded in shop floor operations. 2. KPI & Workshop Management
  • KPI Setting & Cascading: Coordinate the setting of annual/quarterly production goals, cascading site-level objectives into measurable departmental KPIs.
  • Performance Review: Organize and facilitate departmental Management Reviews and Workshops, ensuring early warning of performance deviations.
  • Action Tracking: Responsible for tracking the execution of production improvement action plans to ensure closed-loop management. 3. Business Process Management & Optimization
  • Process Mapping: Identify redundancies in production management and enhance administrative and operational efficiency through Business Process Reengineering.
  • Site-level Collaboration: Represent the Production Department in site-level optimization initiatives, such as Digital Transformation and Supply Chain synergy.
  • Compliance Balance: Ensure all process optimization solutions strictly adhere to GMP requirements, mitigating compliance risks while improving efficiency.
