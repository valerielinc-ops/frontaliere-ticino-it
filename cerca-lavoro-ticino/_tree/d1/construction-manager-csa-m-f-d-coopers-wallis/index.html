Descrizione completa

As Construction Manager and subject matter expert for Civil-Structural and Architectural (CSA) Engineering topics you take over the responsibility for construction management of investment projects of all sizes. In addition you take over design and project managing task for smaller investment and maintenance projects.

Your responsibilities

  • Manage the construction site and bear operational responsibility during the construction and installation phase of a construction site
  • Overall responsibility for coordinating all EHS issues on the construction site and request all necessary work method statements (SiMoKo’s) from the various contractors and check them together with the EHS specialist. Responsible for implementing the EHS Management System on the construction sites and assure that the EHS system is effective by making regular site visits.
  • Support the procurement process and ensure that aspects relevant to construction site safety are incorporated in the work to be performed.
  • Ensure that plan specifications are respected and monitors the construction costs, schedule and quality
  • Organize the building inspections at the end of a construction project and correction of defects.
  • Responsible for the design and execution of smaller investment or maintenance projects as CSA subject matter expert. Prepare tender documents and carry out submission and award contracts together with procurement.
  • Hands-on involvement in the creation and development of CSA standard documents. Making sure that standards and requirements are correctly incorporated.

Your background

  • Very driven to solve problems and meet the aligned goals.
  • Ability to effectively prioritize and execute tasks in a fast-paced environment.
  • Skills and experience in working with matrix-based cross-function teams.
  • Self-motivated and experienced at working both independently and in a team-oriented, collaborative environment is essential.
  • Skilled in usual software tools e.g. MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, MS Office
  • Language: German C1 (required), English B1 (preferred)
