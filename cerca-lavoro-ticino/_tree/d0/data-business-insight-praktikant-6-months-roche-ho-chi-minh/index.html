The Position We advance science so that we all have more time with the people we love. We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Data Scientist Intern to join our team for a 6 month full-time period. This internship offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in data insights within the In Vitro Diagnostics (IVD) market and exposure to AI-driven data analysis. The intern will be responsible for supporting key data initiatives, including data mapping, classification, and cleansing.

Your Opportunity

  • Data Mapping: Map data from the Ministry of Health (MOH) list and the tender dashboard/internal sales dashboard to establish clear connections and insights.
  • Data Classification Support: Provide support in the market data set from in-market volume and value by all players related to the IVD market. Validate the output from internal models to improve accuracy of result. Improving classification models by new patterns, fixing false identification.
  • Data Cleansing: Perform data cleansing for distributors' insight data to ensure accuracy and consistency for analysis.

Who you are

  • English and Vietnamese
  • Keen to gain exposure on data analysis tools (tableau)
  • Working knowledge on Microsoft Excel and Google sheets

The intern will gain valuable experience and exposure to

  • Data management and Interpretation that translate to business strategy
  • The In Vitro Diagnostics (IVD) market.
  • AI-driven data analysis techniques.
