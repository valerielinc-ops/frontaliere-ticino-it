Descrizione completa

Your team The Pictet group is one of the world’s leading independent wealth and asset managers. Founded in 1805 and headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, the Group is represented in 30 offices in financial centres worldwide, currently employing over 5000 people. Your role Pictet is seeking to recruit a Marketing Manager (1-Year Contract) to work in the Marketing Asia - HKG team to support and coordinate marketing activities for Pictet Asset Management in Asia excluding Japan, contributing to brand visibility and business development in the region.

  • Coordinate and execute regional marketing strategies and campaigns, including advertising, events, web, social media, and other marketing initiatives.
  • Assist in the development and production of marketing collaterals, from information and data collection through to distribution, ensuring consistency with brand guidelines.
  • Monitor the publication of global marketing content and support local content production and translation, liaising with external service providers as needed.
  • Demonstrate creativity in developing innovative marketing campaigns and designing thoughtful client gifts that are highly valued and appreciated by recipients.
  • Work closely with internal stakeholders, including local business teams, compliance, and headquarter marketing counterparts, to deliver marketing activities.
  • Help maintain and update the firm’s web, digital, and social media platforms, ensuring content is accurate and engaging.
  • Manage relationships with external vendors for the production of marketing assets, campaigns, events, and other communications materials.
  • Track and report on marketing performance using analytics and KPIs, providing insights for ongoing improvement. Your profile
  • University graduate in Marketing, Communications, or a relevant discipline.
  • 5-10 years of solid marketing and communications experience in financial institutions is highly preferred.
  • Strong attention to detail and creativity.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office and desktop publishing tools.
  • Excellent communication skills (written and verbal) in English and Chinese.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work effectively in a diverse, multicultural environment.
  • A proactive, results-oriented team player with a passion for marketing.
