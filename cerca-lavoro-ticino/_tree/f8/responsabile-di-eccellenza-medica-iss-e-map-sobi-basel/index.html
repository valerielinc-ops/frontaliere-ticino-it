Descrizione completa

The Medical Excellence Manager is responsible for the management of Investigator Sponsored Studies (ISS), Managed Access Programs (MAP) and Post Trial Access (PTA) Programs. Key Responsibilities ISS process

  • Responsible for managing the ISS review and approval process, to include:
  • Validation of proposals for completeness, coordination of review of proposals, timely arrangement of ISS Evaluation Committee (EC) meetings, documentation of EC meeting decisions, communication of decisions to Affiliate teams/external requestors.
  • Is the Global point of contact for ISS management for certain products, as agreed with the team, working with a variety of cross-functional internal and external stakeholders.
  • Maintains a complete and updated ISS database with full tracking of each ISS proposal/study, including relevant documentation and record of decisions made.
  • Facilitates the development and negotiation of contracts between the Sponsor and Sobi in collaboration with the legal function, MPL, the Affiliate teams and other functions, as required.
  • Supports supply chain set-up together with the Affiliate teams and Sobi Supply Chain.
  • Coordinates Sobi follow-up of study reporting and arranges review of study-related publications, as appropriate, by applicable Sobi functions.
  • Responsible for milestone payment tracking and reporting ISS budget to the budget owners.
  • Provides regular status updates of product/project specific ISSs to the responsible teams and leadership, as needed.

MAP/PTA process

  • Responsible for managing the MAP/PTA review and approval process, to include:
  • Coordination of review and approval of requests, arrangement of timely product specific MAP case reviews with relevant members, documentation of meeting decisions, communication of decisions to Affiliate teams, external requestors and third parties.
  • Is the Global point of contact for MAP management for certain products, as agreed with the team, working with a variety of cross-functional internal and external stakeholders.
  • Maintains a complete and updated MAP database with full tracking of each program / individual request, including relevant documentation and record of decisions made.
  • Facilitates contract development between the requestor and Sobi in collaboration with Legal, and other functions, as required.
  • Works with vendors on the set up and management of MAP and PTA programmes, as needed.
  • Provides regular status updates of product MAPs to relevant stakeholders, the responsible teams and leadership, as needed.
