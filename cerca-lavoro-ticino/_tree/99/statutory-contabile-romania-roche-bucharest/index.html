The Position Statutory Accountant (12 month contractor) Responsibilities:

  • Preparation of analytical and synthetic accounting in accordance with Romanian accounting standards;
  • Analytical development of the chart of accounts in accordance with the activities performed by the company, ensuring alignment between the corporate and the local accounts;
  • Preparation of periodic non-tax related returns, as requested by the statutory legal framework and their submission within the legal deadline;
  • Preparation of annual and semi-annual financial statements;
  • Assistance in the preparation of primary documents, including the Journal Register;
  • Assistance in preparation of the postings needed to capture the differences between IFRS and Local GAAP;
  • Preparation of the statutory adjustments postings as needed for the financial statements;
  • Proactively monitor and follow-up with the external auditors and internal stakeholders the progress of the work to complete the statutory financial statements
  • Ensure compliance with any other non-tax local accounting legal provisions (such as, but not limited to, yearly inventory count), liaise with internal stakeholders to this end
  • Assistance in the event of a tax inspection Requirements:
  • University education; preferably in Finance/Accounting related discipline
  • 8 years of experience in a related field and at least 5 years in accountant / audit role
  • Knowledge of Romania accounting principles and practices; financial reporting, audit and taxation
  • Proficiency in relevant accounting software preferably SAP and MS office tools
  • Meticulous, independent, proactive mindset and ability to execute tasks within tight deadlines
  • Proficiency in influencing skills
  • Comfortably working in a dynamic, changing and growing environment
  • Proficiency in English and Romanian 20 hours/week 1 day in the office Who we are A healthier future drives us to innovate.
