The Position The Business Analyst Financial Consolidation serves as a crucial bridge between financial reporting requirements and IT solutions. He possesses in-depth knowledge of the business domain, relevant technologies, and organizational strategy to provide holistic, enterprise-wide consulting advice. In this role, you will play a pivotal role in designing, building, testing, and optimizing the financial consolidation software solutions, ensuring that complex group consolidation processes are efficient, accurate, and compliant. You maintain system integrity, and support the closing process. While maintaining and supporting our current financial consolidation solution is essential, you also largely contribute to consolidation system transformation initiatives. Description of the area The ERP Financial Consolidation team supports the financial consolidation process by managing, configuring, and maintaining the software solutions. The team is embarking on a strategic project to replace our legacy Financial Consolidation system (i.e. SAP FC) with the OneStream . This transformation is driven by the upcoming end-of-life for SAP FC and a commitment to leveraging advanced automation and intelligence to enhance financial consolidation, reporting, and analysis across our global operations.
