Descrizione completa

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role The Equipment Service Technician provides first level customer support for rental units deployed in the field. You will test, diagnose, troubleshoot, repair, and debug electro-mechanical processing equipment and related systems. You must be able to communication and to work closely with Engineering, Project Management, and other Field Technicians on complex problems and assume responsibilities for continued customer satisfaction. This position requires frequent travel to customer sites to assist with installation, start-up, servicing/repairing Wastewater Treatment Equipment (WWTE) and related sub-systems, and Operator training. Principle Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Assist the Service Technician at FRC2 with the refurbishment of rental units.
  • Able and willing to travel 50% to 60% of the time to perform service-related tasks at customer sites.
  • Troubleshoot a variety of Industrial WWTE and resolve mechanical, electrical, and performance issues.
  • Assist customers on the phone and/or in the field with troubleshooting equipment.
  • Prepare detailed reports related to site visits, equipment problems/concerns/recommendations.
  • Adhere to safety policies and use of safety equipment.
  • Adhere to Corporate credit card and travel procedures.
  • Position requires the ability to work extensive hours outdoors in sometimes less than ideal situations (adverse weather, noise, and possibly wet conditions)
