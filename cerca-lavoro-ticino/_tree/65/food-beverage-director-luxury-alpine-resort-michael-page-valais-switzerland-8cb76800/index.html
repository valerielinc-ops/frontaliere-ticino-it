About Our Client Renowned luxury resort located in one of Switzerland's most sought-after Alpine destinations.

Job Description Strategic Direction

  • Shape and implement the Food & Beverage strategy in line with the hotel's luxury positioning and brand values
  • Continuously enhance the dining concepts to maintain their relevance and competitive edge
  • Partner closely with the Executive Chef and hotel leadership to develop distinctive culinary and beverage experiences

Operational Excellence

  • Lead the day-to-day performance of all F&B operations, including restaurants, bars, lounges, in-room dining and banqueting
  • Ensure consistent delivery of exceptional service across every outlet
  • Maintain a visible, hands-on presence and support teams during key service periods
  • Improve operational processes while balancing service quality, cost efficiency and revenue growth

Commercial & Financial Performance

  • Take ownership of budgeting, forecasting and the overall F&B P&L
  • Identify opportunities to increase revenue and profitability through pricing, cost control and outlet optimization
  • Monitor key performance indicators and take appropriate action to improve results

Team Leadership

  • Lead, motivate and develop a large, multicultural F&B team
  • Build a culture based on collaboration, accountability and high performance
  • Coach department leaders and support the development of internal talent
  • Ensure teams are equipped to consistently deliver luxury service standards

Guest Experience

  • Create memorable and highly personalized experiences for an international luxury clientele
  • Engage regularly with guests and maintain a strong presence throughout the operation
  • Respond proactively to feedback and translate insights into service improvements

Quality & Compliance

  • Ensure all operations comply with health, safety, hygiene and regulatory requirements
  • Maintain outstanding standards of quality, presentation and service delivery across the division

The Successful Applicant

  • Significant experience as an F&B Director or Assistant F&B Director within a luxury hotel or palace environment is essential
  • Proven experience leading complex, multi-outlet F&B operations combining high volume with premium positioning
  • Strong financial expertise, including responsibility for substantial budgets and P&L management
  • Demonstrated success in leading and developing large teams in demanding hospitality environments
  • Excellent understanding of international luxury guests and their service expectations
  • A strategic mindset combined with a highly operational and hands-on leadership style
  • Fluent English and French are mandatory, Italian or German a plus
  • Additional languages (e.g., German, Italian) are a plus
  • Charismatic and engaging leader with strong communication skills
  • Passionate about hospitality, service excellence and innovation
  • Detail-oriented, adaptable and resilient in a fast-paced environment
  • Guest-focused, approachable and comfortable being present in daily operations

What's on Offer

  • Opportunity to join a prestigious and internationally recognized luxury property
  • Dynamic and high-performing environment in a prime Alpine destination
  • Competitive compensation package and growth opportunities
