About the role Job Summary Interpret drawings and advise purchaser regarding the sequence of steps in erection, installation, inspection, start-up, or repair of pumps. Explain features and components of products and the functions of those products, and constructively comment on work conditions, methods, and procedures at the jobsite. Key Responsibilities

  • Supervise/assist in troubleshooting of problems, installation, assembly or disassembly of pumps, and/or seals

  • Inform closest Service Center of any potential sales activities while at site
  • Supports, cooperates with, and participates in company-wide LEAN and EHS Management System which includes following all safety, health and environmental policies and procedures.
  • Performs other duties as assigned Qualifications / Requirements
