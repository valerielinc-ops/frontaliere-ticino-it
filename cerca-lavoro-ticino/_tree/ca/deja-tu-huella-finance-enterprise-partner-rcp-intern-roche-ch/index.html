The Position What do we offer? To be part of a new cohort of the Roche-CEU San Pablo postgraduate degree as an expert in the Pharmaceutical Industry. An 11-month internship at Roche where you will participate in challenging projects and benefit from pioneering postgraduate training in the market about the pharmaceutical industry. In this internal customer-facing role, you will join the Finance team acting as a strategic partner to Roche Digital Technology (RDT) and other global functions for one academic year. RDT is our global community of technologists committed to designing the digital future of medicine. Your mission will be to guide the organization through solid financial stewardship, with a clear focus on results, productivity, and cross-functional business transformation initiatives. You will have the opportunity to receive on-the-job training by working on key projects with your designated department from Monday to Thursday and attending in-person postgraduate classes at the university on Friday. Our main location is Madrid. We are open to applications from all over Spain, but relocation will be needed as part of the program expectations. Office attendance is expected (with some flexibility) to encourage collaboration, networking, and exposure to multidisciplinary teams and international stakeholders. Last but not least, you will receive mentorship from a Roche tutor who will provide continuous feedback to support your professional development. The mentors for the program are Roche’s senior and experienced young talent development professionals. Main responsibilities as an intern: As an active member of our finance squad, your work will directly impact our strategy by: Organizational & Restructuring Support: Provide high-impact analytical and operational support to the RDT Finance Enterprise Partnering team during its global organizational restructuring process and other global functions. Cross-functional Collaboration: Collaborate daily with global finance and technology teams on digital transformation initiatives, ensuring the standardization of internal processes and the exchange of best practices. Strategic Partnering (Customer-facing) : Act as a strategic point of contact for RDT's global teams and product owners, facilitating their financial understanding and transforming complex financial data into agile, automated, and transparent tools. Tactical Impact Monitoring: You will assist in tracking digital strategies and internal financial plans to ensure RDT aligns with Roche’s long-term healthcare vision. Through an entrepreneurial lens, you will help monitor financial flows and learn how daily technical and financial decisions create sustainable value.
