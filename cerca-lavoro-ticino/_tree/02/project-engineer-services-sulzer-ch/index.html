About the role Job Summary The Project Engineer is responsible for supporting technical project planning, execution, and monitoring. The role involves working independently with general supervision and contributing to project success through technical and analytical support. This job profile is intended for experienced professionals who apply practical knowledge of a job area and work independently with general supervision. Key Responsibilities

  • Lead end-to-end spare order management, planning and execution by reviewing scope, developing detailed schedules, aligning cross-functional stakeholders, and ensuring delivery within approved budgets and resource constraints, with a focus on achieving more than 95% on-time delivery.
  • Monitor, control, and report project performance by tracking orders, managing backlogs, ensuring schedule adherence, and handling spare order management to meet customer and business requirements.
  • Identify, assess, and manage risks by implementing proactive mitigation strategies to ensure smooth order execution.
  • Manage order budgets, resources, and quality standards while ensuring compliance with applicable organizational requirements, along with maintaining strong control over accounts receivables.
  • Establish and maintain effective communication with customers and internal teams to enhance customer satisfaction and support informed decision-making.
  • Ensure proper project closure through comprehensive documentation, reporting, and formal handover to relevant stakeholders.
  • Apply strategic thinking, problem-solving skills, and innovative approaches to resolve project/order execution challenges.
  • Demonstrate strong technical and manufacturing process knowledge to effectively collaborate with engineering, production, and operations teams.
  • Good Knowledge about Microsoft Office, hands on experience on MS- Excel and SAP are required
  • Exhibit excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Pump, compressor, coupling knowledge would be added advantage. Skills & Qualifications:.
