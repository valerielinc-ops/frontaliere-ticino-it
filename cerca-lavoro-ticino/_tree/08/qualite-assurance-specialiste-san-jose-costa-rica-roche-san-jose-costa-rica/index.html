About the opportunity

  • Conduct QA evaluations of recorded calls, chat interactions, and tickets across D2P-T&E-GFS programs using standardized scoring criteria.
  • Evaluate agent compliance with service standards, scripts, and knowledge management protocols, while mediating QA disputes and technical inquiries.
  • Analyze Voice of Customer (CSAT) data and overall metrics to deliver actionable insights and strategic recommendations to Operations leadership.
  • Develop and deliver comprehensive QA training programs for new hires and Focus Group evaluators.
  • Present overall quality metrics, maintain real-time global dashboards, and escalate critical issues or customer complaints to leadership.
  • Manage and maintain QA systems and documentation with regular updates and reviews.
  • Lead strategic ad-hoc projects aimed at identifying improvement opportunities and driving operational excellence.
  • Conduct regular Governance Calls with key stakeholders to align on quality priorities and strategic initiatives.

Who you are

  • Professional with solid experience in operational quality assurance and multi-channel monitoring (calls, chats, and tickets).
  • Proven ability to analyze customer satisfaction feedback (CSAT) and translate insights into actionable operational improvements.
  • Skilled in developing and delivering QA training programs to enhance evaluator and team performance.
  • Proficient in performance metrics management and maintaining real-time global reporting dashboards.
  • Strong background in managing QA systems, maintaining documentation, and ensuring process compliance.
  • Experienced in resolving QA disputes and driving calibration processes to maintain evaluation consistency.
  • Analytical mindset with a strong focus on governance and leading stakeholder alignment meetings.

  • Problem-solving mindset dedicated to global standardization, compliance, and quality excellence.
