The Position Head of Product Business Workflow Platform and Services Job description The Senior Product Leader is accountable for the entire product lifecycle, from initial ideation and strategic planning through to launch, scaling, and eventual retirement of a complex product. This role is pivotal in transforming scientific and business needs into tangible, high-value technology solutions. You will be responsible for defining the product vision, strategy, and roadmap, ensuring it aligns with the overarching goals of our organization and ultimately accelerates the delivery of life-changing diagnostics and medicines to patients. This role demands a distinctive combination of scientific inquisitiveness, technical proficiency, and business insight to successfully navigate the landscape. Description of the area The Business Workflow Platform and Services function is a part of Enterprise Platforms Products and Services. These platforms are consumed by others to create business facing platforms, products and services.The Business Workflow Platform in use is ServiceNow, from which function specific modules are enabled to allow business functions within Roche to build workflows to serve their specific needs.
