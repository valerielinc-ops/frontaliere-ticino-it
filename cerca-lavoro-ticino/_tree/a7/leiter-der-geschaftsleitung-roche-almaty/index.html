The Position Executive Operations Lead Reporting to: General Manager/ Finance Manager Subordinates: 4 Role Description The Admin Manager / Chief of Staff is a dual-impact role, serving as a strategic partner to the General Manager and a functional leader for the organization’s administrative and business support infrastructure. Reporting to the Finance Manager, the role holder acts as a "bridge-builder" between the Enabling Team and business units, ensuring operational excellence across executive support, office management, and event logistics. As a leader of the Business Support team, they are responsible for optimizing administrative processes, fostering a high-performance service culture, and ensuring that the physical and logistical environment of the office empowers the team to deliver value to patients and stakeholders. Summary of Main Responsibilities: Executive Support & Strategic Coordination

  • Act as the primary Executive Assistant to the General Manager, proactively managing complex calendars, domestic/international travel logistics, and migration/visa requirements.
  • Handle confidential business and personal tasks for the GM to optimize their leadership capacity.
  • Provide high-level administrative partnership to the Enabling Team, ensuring departmental priorities are supported. People Leadership & Process Excellence
  • Directly manage and coach a diverse team including Administrative and Business Support Coordinators and the Driver
  • Standardize and continuously improve administrative business processes to increase organizational efficiency and simplify complex workflows.
  • Distribute tasks among specialists based on their competencies and workload, providing daily mentorship on Roche systems, tools, and methods.
  • Supervise Business Support Coordinators to ensure high-quality logistical assistance and event support is provided to all business-driving teams.
  • Oversee the full lifecycle of vendor relations and documentation, from tendering and contract preparation to the control of invoices and financial closing.

  • Ensure all administrative operations are executed with precision, maintaining high ethical standards and safeguarding confidential company information. Office Operations & Fleet Management
