  • Research and establish new consulting packages and frameworks to position Roche as an industry pioneer.

  • Align local consulting practices with Global and APAC regional lab consulting teams (Lab 1.0).

  • Collaborate intensely with CA (Customer Area) Sales/Marketing and Agile teams to streamline automation proposals. 4. Continuous Skill-Up as a Lab Operations Expert
  • Upskill consulting capabilities to position oneself as a premier laboratory operations expert in the IVD market.
  • Acquire a profound, structured understanding of complex hospital laboratory workflows and total ecosystems.
  • Apply consultative and solution-oriented thinking to eliminate operational waste and maximize customer success. Requirements 1. Core Qualifications
  • Education: Bachelor’s degree or higher (Biomedical Science/Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or healthcare-related preferred).
  • Experience: Minimum 3 years in the IVD industry, hospital laboratory environments, clinical lab operations, or the healthcare sector.
  • Analytical & Tech Skills: Strong data analysis skills with high proficiency in MS Office (Excel/PPT) and a proactive aptitude for utilizing AI productivity tools.
  • Communication: Professional working proficiency in English (verbal/written) with the ability to manage both local and global stakeholders. 2. Preferred Competencies
  • Consulting & Workflow: Experience in healthcare/laboratory consulting, strategic planning, or workflow optimization (Lean Six Sigma understanding preferred).
  • Growth Mindset & Adaptability: High learning agility and resilience to take risks, try new initiatives, and adapt quickly to change.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Proven ability to build networks and collaborate effectively within integrated, cross-functional teams.
