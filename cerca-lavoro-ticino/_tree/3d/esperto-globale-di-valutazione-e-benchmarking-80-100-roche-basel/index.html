The Position As the Global Expert Estimating & Benchmarking, you will lead the integration of data science, predictive modeling, and cost engineering to ensure cost certainty across our global multi-billion-franc capital project portfolio. Joining our Global Capital Investment team in Basel, you will leverage advanced analytics, AI, and historical benchmarking to transform raw data into high-value strategic insights. This is a unique opportunity to shape future-ready estimating standards and drive value-focused investments across an international sites network.

The Opportunity

  • Drive cost certainty & standards: Develop, implement, and maintain global standards for cost estimating and benchmarking in alignment with industry best practices (e.g., AACE, IPA, RICS).
  • Deliver early-stage & conceptual estimates: Lead early-stage cost estimating and oversee conceptual and basic design estimates across major capital projects worldwide.
  • Pioneer predictive modeling & AI: Develop data science strategies using AI, LLMs, custom coding (Python/R), and BIM 5D to build automated parametric models and predictive forecasting tools.
  • Track market economics: Monitor global market trends, inflation, and economic indicators to assess their impact on project budgets and organizational standards.
  • Facilitate risk & value engineering: Support risk workshops to establish data-backed allowances for contingency, design development, and schedule reserves while driving value engineering.
  • Build capability & community: Lead a global Community of Practice for estimating and benchmarking while conducting technical training to build internal engineering competency.

Who You Are

  • Educational & Professional Credentials: You hold a Master’s degree (MSc or higher) in Engineering, Construction Management, Quantity Surveying, or a quantitative field, paired with certifications like CEP, CCP, or MRICS.
  • Mega-Project & Controls Experience: You bring at least 15 years of hands-on experience in mega-project estimating, cost engineering, or global project controls across international, multi-cultural matrix organizations.
  • Data Science & AI Coding Skills: You possess high proficiency in quantitative data analysis with hands-on coding experience in Python, R, and AI-augmented project intelligence (e.g., Claude, LLMs) to automate parametric models.
  • Industry Standards & Methodologies: You bring expert knowledge of AACE/RICS standards, combined with proven ability in market economics tracking, risk workshop facilitation, and value engineering.
  • Collaborative Leadership & Mobility: You are a strong systematic thinker and communicator who thrives on continuous improvement, fluent in English (German is a plus), and open to up to 20% international travel.
