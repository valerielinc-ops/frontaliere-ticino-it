The Position A healthier future is what drives us to innovate. To continuously advance science and ensure everyone has access to the healthcare they need today and for generations to come. Creating a world where we all have more time with the people we love. That’s what makes us Roche. The Role: As a member of FiCoE, you are accountable to deliver short-term financial targets while creating sustainable value in the long term. Other duties include providing insights, enabling enterprise and functional outcomes through financial and business analysis, and steering and monitoring of holistic company performance. You will also drive finance processes, delivering high quality and compliant financials required to support short and long-term planning, reporting, and decision-making. You will collaborate across the broader Roche organization to co-create solutions that ensure broad and equitable access of our diagnostics to patients and provide value to our customers. The Opportunity: As the Finance Rebate Analyst, you are responsible for supporting the accurate and timely processing of customer rebates (including admin fees and chargebacks), lead technical support solutions, and provide financial insights. This is done through collaboration with customers and internal stakeholders to retrieve and thoroughly analyze various data sets from external systems, provide explanations for rebate payments and rejections and interpret terms and agreements within our customer contracts. Through timely processing of our customer rebates submissions, you will ensure compliance to our customers core agreements while providing great customer service. You will provide consultation and insights regarding open rebates disputes, tactics, best practices, policies and procedures. Duties are varied and complex, often involving massive data set processing, researching historical trends, and analysis to ensure proper and time-critical rebate payments. You are responsible for achieving quality objectives through daily actions needed to meet our monthly/quarterly deadlines. The following requirements will be needed for this position: Technical Lead to ensure tools functioning to support business requirements Create Innovative Solutions to increase efficiency and reporting accuracy Ability to Lead projects to support initiatives such as ASPIRE and/or Data Enablement This position is based on-site at our Indianapolis campus and uses a hybrid (3 days per week) working model. Relocation benefits are not available for this job posting. Who You Are:

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field Key Responsibilities:
  • Collaborate with external stakeholders to retrieve data from external websites and systems.
  • Review and analyze rebates and identify outliers to determine the validity of claims based on contract stipulations; create new outlier rules as needed.
  • Provide external stakeholder insights explaining rebate approvals and rejections.
  • Research and analyze historical rebate data to determine trends and ensure proper rebate payments.
  • Collaborate with the contracts team to resolve and provide recommendations regarding contract issues.
  • Support of other financial projects as needed.
  • Assists with the forecast and budget process by performing analysis, setting estimated targets, and partnering with internal departments and stakeholders
  • Prepares month, quarter and year-end analyses, reporting projections and closing process
  • Documents current processes at all times Pref
