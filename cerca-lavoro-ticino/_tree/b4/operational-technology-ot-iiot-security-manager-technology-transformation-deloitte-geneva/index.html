Descrizione completa

Location: Zurich, Geneva Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Start Date: Ongoing About the Role We focus on enabling our clients’ end to end industrial security transformation journey with our end-to-end OT and supply chain cybersecurity approach. Cybercriminals are increasingly attacking large scale manufacturing organisations at their weakest points, the industrial and operations environment. Our mission is to develop OT cybersecurity strategies that improve our clients' defence capabilities along with their operations and supply chain and reduce the risk of costly disruptions. Finding the talent that makes Deloitte unique To that purpose, our cyber teams work closely with our client’s risk, compliance, procurement, supply chain, operations and network teams to embed security and resilience in critical topics such as manufacturing operations, supply chain and logistics, infrastructure and network in the industrial environment, factory 4.0 strategy. How you can make an impact:

  • You will be part of large industrial cybersecurity programmes working in a multidisciplinary model approach across client and Deloitte teams
  • You will drive or assist in driving large scale OT/IIoT cybersecurity transformation programmes
  • You will design and implement global operational technology (OT), Industrial IoT (IIoT) and supply chain cybersecurity strategies, governance, and operating models
  • You will visit factories and plants to perform assessment and discovery operations by deploying multi-protocol passive monitoring devices (MPPM), analysing results, and identifying security gaps to remediate.
  • You will support the design and implementation of core security components in OT (Network segmentation, segregation, security reference architecture, use access control and secure remote access, security monitoring, vulnerability management, patch management) What you bring to succeed:
  • You have 5+ years of hands-on experience in security design and implementation experience on one or more of the OT vendor solutions such as Nozomi Networks, Claroty, Palo Alto Networks, TrendMicro, Tenable OT, Fortinet, etc.
  • You are passionate about the subject of cyber and have experience in industrial security (ICS/OT and/or IoT)
  • You have a technical background, good observational skills, problem-solving skills and the ability to articulate complex concepts in a clear and concise manner
  • Experience in network segmentation and segregation for OT, security architecture and security certifications such as GICSP, GRID, Lead SCADA security engineers, CISSP are desirable
  • Fluency in English is mandatory, French and German would be considered a plus Your team You will join a diverse, international, and highly collaborative team of cyber security professionals who are passionate about helping organisations strengthen their resilience against cyber threats. Most importantly, we foster an open and inclusive culture where people are encouraged to bring new ideas, challenge the status quo, and shape the future of cyber defence. What makes our team different is the opportunity to work across the full cyber defence lifecycle. One day you may be helping a client define their future Cyber Defence Centre strategy, the next designing security monitoring capabilities, improving incident response processes, or supporting the implementation of leading security technologies. We believe that great careers are built through challenging work, continuous learning, and strong support. You will benefit from hands-on experience, dedicated mentoring, professional certifications, and collaboration with experts across Deloitte's global network.
