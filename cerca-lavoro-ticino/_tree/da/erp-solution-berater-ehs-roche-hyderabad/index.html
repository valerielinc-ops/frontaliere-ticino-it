The Position #Job Description: The ERP - SAP IT consultant leads independently complex SAP solution delivery from a technical standpoint. This role is responsible for the specification of the architecture and functionality a global or local IT solutions.. In addition, they advise business and IT stakeholders on technical topics and work alongside IT and business colleagues to drive Idea-to-Requirement and Requirement-to-Implementation processes in a technical consulting role. This IT expert role will work within the ERP Product configuration and Sustainability team. This position includes the below mentioned tasks and responsibilities with main focus on SAP Product and REACH Compliance, SAP Product Safety and/or SAP S/4HANA for Product Compliance Software Solutions.

Job Responsibilities

Solutions/Services

  • Designs and communicates high-level structures to enable and guide the design and development of integrated solutions that meet current and future business needs.
  • Demonstrates understanding of requirements gathering and is able to show how agreed requirements are addressed by the proposed solution
  • Innovates and optimizes processes and methods within the domain to meet system or user requirements and develop new functionality
  • Guides the development of, and modifications to, solution components to ensure that they take account of relevant architectures, strategies, policies, standards and practices and that existing and planned solution components remain compatible
  • Leads cross-solution architecture in the SAP environment.
  • Coordinates and cooperates with a team of external consultants and developers for support processes, change management and DevOps setup.
  • Works and cooperates with a team of external consultants and developers for change and incident management processes in either Waterfall, DevOps or Agile team configurations.

Standards

  • Applies and implements process standards and actively participates in their introduction and rollout.
  • Observes governance processes, highlights areas for improvement and assists in their implementation
  • Implements appropriate framework and processes
  • Uses tools and follows Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
  • Maintains appropriate documentation of tasks and activities performed as required by project and other authorities.
  • Ensures dynamic collaboration with governance & dedicated architecture bodies
  • Supports new and established IT solutions by following ITIL rule sets (esp. incident and service management)

Know-how

  • Understands business processes governing the application and is able to establish strong links with other technical teams responsible for the application and product delivery.
  • Develops business/technical critical knowledge within the area of expertise for the function.
  • Ensures alignment to defined technology roadmap among cross functional teams.
  • Assists in the development of others by coaching and sharing his/her experiences and knowledge in the field of expertise.
  • Supports the implementation of projects within the area of technical expertise, contributes to the implementation planning.
  • Where requested supports Maintenance and Support activities e.g. for technical implementations or Change Management processes in a Waterfall, Agile or DevOps setup.
  • Acts as IT consultant to Product Owners / Product Manager(s) (e.g. input to technical process flows, requirements, functionality gap analysis).
  • Helps in defining testing for System and User Acceptance tests
