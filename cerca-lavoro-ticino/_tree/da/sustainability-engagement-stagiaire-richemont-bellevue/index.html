Descrizione completa

Reference code: JR135864

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

At Richemont, Corporate Sustainability develops the Group’s strategic sustainability framework, policies, and guidance to Maisons, Regions and Functions while offering learning opportunities through the Group’s Sustainability Academy. The Intern contributes to these efforts by working transversally with the entire Engagement team to raise awareness, develop trainings, and fostering a strong sustainability community across the Group.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the development of the department’s projects management platforms via Monday.com by collaborating across teams to ensure the solution responds to the evolving needs of the department.
  • Support the development and publication of sustainability training materials for eLearnings and in-person trainings (text, videos, animations) for the Sustainability Academy.
  • Support the team in content collection template to ensure accuracy and completeness of non-financial reporting;
  • Support the organisational planning (research, logistics, and communications) of the Sustainability engagement moments with internal shareholders including Sustainability Forums, Annual Sustainability Gathering (both virtual and in person)
  • Assist in the writing, look and feel, preparation and distribution of department publications and presentations
  • Assess external learning materials to evaluate potential to respond to the business’ needs
  • Analyse training data and maintain monthly dashboards to review progress against targets

Key Requirements, Skills and Experience

  • Creative and strategic thinker with an eye for trends
  • Strong capacity for multi-tasking and diligent whilst working under supervision to meet deadlines
  • Positive attitude, flexible and team player
  • Strong data analysis and problem-solving skills
  • Bachelor’s degree and/or Master student in sustainability
  • Previous first experience in corporate sustainability preferred
  • Excellent written and spoken English, other languages such as French, Italian or German would be a plus
  • Advanced Microsoft Office skills (proficient command of Excel and PowerPoint)
  • Experience with Monday.com or other project management platforms is a plus
  • Experience with Learning & Development platforms (e.g. EasyGenerator) is a plus
