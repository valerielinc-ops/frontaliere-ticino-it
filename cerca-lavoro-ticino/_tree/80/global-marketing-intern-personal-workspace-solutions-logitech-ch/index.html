About the job Logitech is the Sweet Spot for people who want their actions to have a positive global impact while having the flexibility to do it in their own way.

The Role and the Team

Join Logitech as a Global Marketing Intern in the Personal Workspace Solutions team for a dynamic 6-month internship based in Lausanne. You'll gain hands-on experience by contributing to strategic marketing initiatives, driving brand growth, and expanding our global business. Collaborate with diverse, cross-functional teams on exciting product launches, analyze key consumer and market insights, and execute impactful global marketing programs. This is a fantastic opportunity to kickstart your career in marketing!

Your Contribution

Be Yourself. Be Open. Stay Hungry and Humble. Collaborate. Challenge. Decide and just Do. These are the behaviors you’ll need for success at Logitech.

In this role you will gain

  • Hands-on experience in global brand management and marketing.
  • Exposure to cross-functional teams in a fast-paced, innovative environment.
  • Opportunity to gain marketing strategy, product marketing, and stakeholder management skills in an international environment.
  • Mentorship and guidance to support your professional growth.

Your Qualifications

For consideration, you must bring the following minimum skills and behaviors to our team:

  • Recently graduated from a Bachelor's or Master's degree program, preferably in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field.
  • Strong interest in design, culture, trends, and technology.
  • Strong consumer centricity and a passion for brand building.
  • Excellent communication skills, ability to collaborate effectively with internal and external teams.
  • Highly organised and solution-driven, showing initiative and proactivity in problem-solving.

Fluency in English and ability to work from the Logitech Lausanne office 2-3 days a week are required.

Across Logitech we empower collaboration and foster play. We help teams collaborate/learn from anywhere, without compromising on productivity or continuity so it should be no surprise that most of our jobs are open to work from home from most locations. Our hybrid work model allows some employees to work remotely while others work on-premises. Within this structure, you may have teams or departments split between working remotely and working in-house.

Logitech is an amazing place to work because it is full of authentic people who are inclusive by nature as well as by design. Being a global company, we value our diversity and celebrate all our differences. Don’t meet every single requirement? Not a problem. If you feel you are the right candidate for the opportunity, we strongly recommend that you apply. We want to meet you!

We offer comprehensive and competitive benefits packages and working environments that are designed to be flexible and help you to care for yourself and your loved ones, now and in the future. We believe that good health means more than getting medical care when you need it. Logitech supports a culture that encourages individuals to achieve good physical, financial, emotional, intellectual and social wellbeing so we all can create, achieve and enjoy more and support our families.
