Logitech is the Sweet Spot for people who want their actions to have a positive global impact while having the flexibility to do it in their own way. The role and team: Logitech is looking for an experienced Software Project Manager to join our Software Organization. Be Open. Stay Hungry and Humble. Collaborate. Challenge. Decide and just Do. Share our passion for Equality and the Environment.
