About the role Job Summary The HR Sr. Officer is responsible for delivering HR services and supporting day-to-day HR operations. This role assists in recruitment, onboarding, and employee support, ensuring compliance with policies and procedures. This job profile is intended for experienced professionals who apply practical knowledge of a job area and work independently with general supervision. The HR Representative provides hands-on operational and administrative support to the HRBP team, ensuring efficient execution of core HR processes. This role focuses on reducing transactional workload, improving data accuracy, and supporting payroll and HR systems processes so HRBPs can focus on strategic business initiatives. Key Responsibilities

  • Act as day to day HR Support for assigned employee base
  • Act as the primary support resource for HRBPs, assisting with day-to-day operational tasks
  • Prepare and execute HR transactions in Workday and other systems (e.g., job changes, compensation updates, transfers)
  • Draft communications and documentation to support business and HR initiatives
  • Ensure timely follow-up on HR action items and requests
  • Serve as a liaison between HRBPs and Payroll to support accurate and timely processing
  • Validate data changes between HRIS (e.g., Workday) and payroll systems (e.g., ADP) to ensure alignment
  • Reduce manual rework by leveraging reporting and system tools to capture employee changes
  • Identify discrepancies and escalate issues proactively for resolution
  • Support efforts to streamline payroll-related processes and minimize duplicate tracking
  • Run and analyze standard reports (e.g., change reports, headcount, audit reports)
  • Monitor data integrity across HR systems and address inconsistencies
  • Support audits and compliance checks related to employee data and payroll
  • Respond to employee and manager inquiries related to HR processes, pay, benefits, and data
  • Provide guidance on system use (e.g., Workday self-service)
  • Escalate complex or sensitive issues to HR Manager as needed Qualifications
