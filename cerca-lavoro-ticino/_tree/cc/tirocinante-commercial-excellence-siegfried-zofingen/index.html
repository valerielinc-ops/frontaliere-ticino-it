Your Role: At Siegfried we are currently looking for an Intern Commercial Excellence to join our team in our site based in Zofingen.   • Support development of customer / product segmentation databases.

Education: Master Studies in Business Administration, Finance, Business Engineering, Business Chemistry, Business Informatics Languages: Fluent in English Experience: General understanding of end-to-end business processes in process industries with focus on pharmaceuticals / chemical manufacturing MS Excel / MS PowerPoint Experience in data analytics and data visualization applications will be a plus Competences: Self-motivated with entrepreneurial and pro-active spirit Analytical and strategic thinking Systematic and ordered attention to detail Strives to simplify and make things simple Can do attitude Team worker Duration: Min. 3 month or around 2 days per week on average over 6-12 month Your Benefits: In addition, you will learn how different company functions will work together to create value for Siegfried and our customers globally. Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network of 16 sites across three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly skilled professionals, we take the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to industrial scale and manufacture safe drugs for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations today that can carry out the development and manufacturing of APIs and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner in the pharmaceutical industry. Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
