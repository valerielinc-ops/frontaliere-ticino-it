Job Description Summary #LI-Hybrid Location: Basel

The TM AI Strategy and Transformation Director will be responsible for shaping, orchestrating and overseeing the Translational Medicine (TM) AI strategy and the portfolio of AI initiatives across TM. This role acts as the TM-level integrator connecting business priorities, operational/process AI use cases, data, technology and operational delivery, ensuring AI investments are prioritized, governed and transitioned into business-as-usual capabilities that make TM decisions faster, smarter and more predictive.

This role translates the TM line function strategic AI priorities into a coherent, realistic and trackable AI roadmap with clear decision rights, delivery plans, KPIs, ROI assessments and value frameworks. Working in close partnership with the Data Science Leaders in TM, Strategy and Planning line function leads, and Head Clinical AI Strategy – Translational Research, this role provides the TM-wide view across all line functions, from operational and process AI to scientific and decision-support applications.

The individual will act as a role model and champion for AI adoption, fostering an innovation mindset and AI literacy across TM, keeping TM efforts aligned with the Biomedical Research and overall Novartis R&D AI strategies and enterprise standards. Through matrix leadership, the role integrates function- and TM-level AI objectives and acts as the single point of contact for TM AI updates to the Leadership Team and Steering Committees.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

  • In partnership with the TM Data Science LT and TM leadership, co-develop and maintain the multi-year TM AI roadmap, turning agreed priorities into clear objectives, milestones and resource plans.
  • Run a single view of the TM AI portfolio, from intake and prioritization through stage gates, budget and status, and bring clear recommendations to leadership for decision.
  • Coordinate TM AI governance: help define decision rights, prepare executive-level content and facilitate the forums where leadership makes investment decisions.
  • Partner with project teams and line functions to keep endorsed initiatives on track, and ensure proven solutions are embedded as everyday ways of working with clear ownership.
  • Oversee the transition of proven operational and process AI solutions into fully operationalized business-as-usual capabilities, with clear post-go-live ownership, support models and process changes agreed with the line functions.
  • Work with line functions to identify where AI, including AI agents, can redesign how TM works, and help prioritize those opportunities .
  • Define, develop and track impact measures, KPIs and value cases for the TM AI portfolio, and report value delivered to TM leadership and BR stakeholders.
  • Work with enterprise data, digital and AI teams to avoid duplication, reuse shared platforms and meet GxP and validation standards .
  • Scout and evaluate external AI partners, vendors and technologies, and support business cases, budget proposals and contracting together with procurement and partner line functions.
  • Champion AI literacy and adoption across TM, working with the TM Academy and TM Operational Excellence teams, to develop training, champion networks and targeted communications, and act as a trusted advisor to TM leadership on AI trends and value.

Essential Requirements

  • Bachelors in life sciences, relevant technology or related field required.
  • Master's or advanced degree desirable. Project management certification, MBA or AI/data science training desirable
  • 8+ years' experience in relevant industry such as pharmaceutical or clinical drug development, ideally including early-phase clinical studies.
  • Proven track record of leading innovation or technology-driven change in R&D or clinical trials, and embedding it into routine use.
  • Strong project and portfolio management skills, including planning, prioritization and running several complex initiatives at once.
  • Working knowledge of AI, data science and digital technologies and how they apply in drug development, with awareness of emerging trends such as generative AI. Hands-on involvement in at least one AI or data-driven initiative is desirable; deep technical expertise is not required.
  • Experience preparing and presenting executive-level content, such as strategy papers, investment proposals and portfolio updates, to senior leadership forums including Leadership Teams and Steering Committees and supporting their decision-making.
  • Experience building business and value cases (e.g., quality, time and cost) and tracking impact across a portfolio of initiatives through KPIs.
  • Experience leading cross-functional teams in a global, matrixed organization, influencing without authority and communicating clearly at all levels.
  • Pragmatic, structured thinker who turns agreed priorities into clear plans, decisions and measurable results across a broad portfolio, and adapts well to change.
