Descrizione completa

Your role with us

  • Responsibility for the efficient handling of all warehouse processes (goods receipt, putaway, order picking and dispatch)
  • You will provide support as required across all operational areas (stand-in role)
  • Ensuring timely and high-quality order processing, particularly of customer orders
  • Monitoring, managing and optimising stock levels, including carrying out stock-takes
  • Analysing existing warehousing processes and continuously developing and optimising them (e.g. LEAN approaches)
  • Working closely with the Purchasing and Sales departments
  • Ensuring compliance with quality and safety regulations
  • Supporting the Warehousing Team Lead with optimisation projects and the development and maintenance of key performance indicators
  • Assisting with the training of logistics apprentices

What you bring to the role

  • A completed qualification in logistics (e.g. Logistics Specialist EFZ) or a technical/commercial qualification with relevant practical experience in logistics
  • Several years’ professional experience in warehouse logistics
  • A high degree of flexibility combined with a quick grasp of processes and expertise in all operational areas of logistics
  • Experience in using warehouse management systems (e.g. SAP, WMS or Microsoft Dynamics)
  • Good knowledge of MS Office (particularly Excel)
  • Very good command of German and a basic knowledge of English
  • Further training in logistics or supply chain management would be an advantage
  • Strong IT skills and a passion for working with systems
  • Strong process-oriented mindset and knowledge of LEAN management
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented approach to work
  • Analytical thinking and a good understanding of interrelationships
  • Ability to work in a team, assertiveness, and a high degree of reliability and sense of responsibility
