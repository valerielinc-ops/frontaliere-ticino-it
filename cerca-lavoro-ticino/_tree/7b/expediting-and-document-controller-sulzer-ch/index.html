Purpose of the Job: The Expeditor is responsible for overseeing the timely production of our industrial equipment manufactured by subcontractors, ensuring on-time delivery and meeting quality standards. Main tasks and responsibilities:

  • As an Expediter, you will oversee the following tasks:
  • 1. Establish the project Doc management system and procedure
  • 2. Manage & maintain the project Doc sheet & schedule with other stakeholders
  • 3. Monitoring and expediting the project Doc and production of raw materials & goods to ensure timely delivery.
  • 4. Communicating regularly with the suppliers to get updates on order status & progression.
  • 5. Solving problems and managing recovery plans in case of damaged goods or manufacturing delays with the suppliers and the project team.
  • 6. Ensuring that the suppliers’ schedules are aligned with our internal requirements.
  • 7. Coordinate the goods delivery with the logistics groups.
  • 8. Developing and implementing new processes to improve internal work efficiency.
  • 9. Maintaining an efficient document management system to ensure accessibility of documents.
  • 10. Coordinating document validation, approval, and modification processes.
  • 11. Providing support to internal and external teams regarding documentation.
