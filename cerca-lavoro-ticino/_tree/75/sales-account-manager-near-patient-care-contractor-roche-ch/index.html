The Position As Sales Account Manager you will be responsible for achieving regional sales targets for our Near Patient Care (NPC) Roche Diagnostics portfolio. You will manage the full customer lifecycle, from prospecting new clients to maintaining relationships with existing ones through proactive communication. Key responsibilities include providing demand-driven sales and after-sales support, conducting customer training, and installing NPC products, all while ensuring the highest level of customer satisfaction. You will also monitor market trends and competitor activity. Our NPC covers healthcare customers such as physicians (General practitioners, ambulatory specialists - diabetologists, internal medicine doctors, cardiologists, pneumologists) and hospitals. The NPC portfolio consists of various POCT devices, professional/hospital and patient blood glucose meters and CGM.
