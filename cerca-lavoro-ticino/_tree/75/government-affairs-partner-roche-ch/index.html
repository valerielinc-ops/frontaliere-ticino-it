The Position In an Organization with big ambition, there is opportunity to make meaningful change a reality. Our mission is to deliver better outcomes for more patients faster. It is worth our hard work and innovation. The Government Affairs Partner is a full time role in Algiers. His/Her mission is to focus on shaping public policy to instigate system change in the Algerian healthcare environment. The role: Optimize system change through Public Policy

  • Develops a Roche Algeria Public Policy strategy to shape and future proof the Algerian Healthcare environment for innovative technologies.
  • Navigating political landscape and delivering projects with relevant stakeholders e.g.: Government Industry Associations, Advocacy Groups, Journalists, KOLs, Decision Makers and all stakeholders within the healthcare system
  • Identifies opportunities to shape policy in collaboration with Primary Points of Contact (Patient Journey Partners & Healthcare System Partners)
  • Identifies opportunities to enhance Roche’s reputation and positioning as a preferred and impactful partner for sustainable healthcare.
  • Provides in-depth knowledge and insight on healthcare legalization, keeping relevant internal stakeholders up-to-date with any changes.
  • Liaises with Regional counterparts on Public Policy projects to ensure shared learning and efficient delivery of initiatives.
  • Collaborates closely with internal Stakeholders to ensure alignment of objectives and coordination of activities.
  • Leads the production of high quality internal/external responses to relevant policies and discussion documents as required.
  • Works with the GM on key policies shifts.
  • Provides support to PPOCs on issues of a political or healthcare policy, to deliver on company mission to get better outcomes, for more patients, faster. Corporate Communications:
  • Contribute to the delivery of Policy inputs into an integrated communications plan aligned to Roche Algeria key goals
  • Ensure an understanding of Healthcare environmental influences to direct the development of communications strategies aligned to healthcare policy opportunities. Compliance / Monitoring:
  • Agree KPIs in line with Policy Plan and monitor regularly to ensure appropriate action taken
  • Take personal responsibility for maintaining high ethical standards and operating in compliance with local and global policies. Who you are:
  • You have a knowledge of Algeria Healthcare and General Health policy and understanding of how this is shaped.
  • You have a strong networking and influencing skills to facilitate stakeholder engagement.
  • You have and excellent written and oral communication skills to facilitate communicating complex information clearly internally and Externally (Arabic, French and English)
  • You have good Interpersonal Skills
  • You are a self-starter with demonstrable ability to plan both strategically and tactically and to effectively prioritize tasks.
