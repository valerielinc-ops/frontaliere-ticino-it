Descrizione completa

Mission

As a Social Media Content Creator , you actively shape Alpiq’s digital presence. Your mission is to identify strong stories, bring them to life visually across channels, and create content that generates real impact – not just output. You combine creative storytelling with efficient execution and a clear understanding of target audiences, platform logic, and performance.

Your Responsibilities

  • You conceptualise and produce digital content with focus on social media, for all digital channels, such as videos, animations, and emerging formats
  • You manage productions end-to-end – from ideation to shooting, editing, and publishing
  • You act as a superuser for our digital channels (social media, website, intranet)
  • You bring in your own story ideas and execute them independently, creatively, and in a target-group-oriented way
  • You monitor social media and video trends and translate them into new formats and approaches
  • You analyse the performance of your content and derive optimisations
  • You support and mentor a Mediamatik apprentice, actively sharing your knowledge
  • You take on an active role in the Alpiq corporate newsroom
  • Together with a cross-functional team, you continuously develop our digital presence
  • You bring in ‘a different kind of energy’

Your Impact

With your work, you make Alpiq’s brand promise tangible. You ensure high-quality, consistent, and audience-oriented content across all relevant channels. Through data-driven optimisation, a strong sense of trends, and creative execution, you play a key role in advancing digital communications and content strategy – strengthening Alpiq’s positioning.

Your Profile

  • Apprenticeship as a Mediamatiker EFZ or a degree in multimedia production
  • Initial professional experience in content or media production
  • Proficient in Adobe Creative Suite and open to working with new AI tools
  • Experience with social media, CMS (ideally TYPO3), and SharePoint-based solutions
  • Structured and independent working style with strong initiative
  • Reliable in project execution with solid deadline management
  • Team player with a positive mindset and hands-on mentality
  • Fluent in German and English
  • French is a plus
