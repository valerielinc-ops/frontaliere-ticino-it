Job Description Summary #LI-Hybrid

Location: Basel, Switzerland

Relocation Support: This role is based in Basel, Switzerland. Novartis is unable to offer relocation support: please only apply if accessible.

As a Senior Accounting Specialist within the General Ledger team, you will play an important role in delivering accurate and timely financial and statutory reporting. You will take ownership of core accounting activities, from balance sheet reconciliations and reporting to statutory financial statements, while partnering with stakeholders to resolve accounting matters and maintain strong financial controls.

Job Description Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare accurate monthly and quarterly financial reporting packages, ensuring completeness and timely submission.
  • Perform balance sheet reconciliations and drive timely resolution of reconciling items.
  • Prepare and post manual journal entries with complete and accurate supporting documentation.
  • Prepare statutory financial statements in line with applicable accounting and reporting requirements.
  • Analyze accounting topics and collaborate with stakeholders to resolve financial reporting issues.
  • Support internal and external audits by preparing documentation and responding to accounting-related requests.
  • Strengthen internal control activities and help ensure consistent compliance with financial reporting requirements.

Essential Requirements

  • University degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field.
  • At least five years of relevant finance experience, ideally in General Ledger accounting within a multinational environment.
  • Strong financial accounting knowledge, including International Financial Reporting Standards and month-end and quarter-end closing activities.
  • Strong SAP and Microsoft Excel skills, with practical experience in General Ledger accounting and journal entries.
  • Ability to work independently with accuracy, ownership, and confidence while managing demanding reporting deadlines.
  • Fluent English with strong communication and stakeholder management skills.

This role is for temporary contract of 15 months .
