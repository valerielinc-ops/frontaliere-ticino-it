Descrizione completa

The Galatea

The Galatea Laboratory at EPFL explores novel paradigms for laser-based local tailoring of material properties. Our research spans three areas: laser-matter interaction phenomena, 3D direct-write laser manufacturing processes, and multi-scale monolithic systems. The laboratory is equipped with state-of-the-art laser facilities built for pioneering research.

We are part of EPFL’s Faculty of Engineering (STI) and based at the Neuchâtel campus, embedded in Microcity — a hub focused on microtechnology.

EPFL ranks among the top 20 universities worldwide and is one of Europe’s most dynamic research campuses, home to over 16,000 people from 120+ countries, including 12,000 students and 4,000 researchers.

We are currently looking for a postdoctoral researcher to join the lab in the following role:

Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Photonics Integration

Mission

To strengthen our research program on direct-write optical integrated systems, we are seeking a post-doctoral researcher to investigate a novel femtosecond laser-based process for efficient 3D photonics integration in glass substrates.

With a system-design mindset, your task will be to design and optimize photonics interconnects that leverage the process capabilities. The work will combine experimental research with a rigorous analysis to predict optical propagation at various wavelengths through complex 3D architectures.

Profile

  • You recently obtained a PhD in physics, electrical engineering, micro-technologies or related fields with a specialty in photonics.
  • You have experience in modeling and simulating optical propagation phenomena.
  • Knowledge with laser-based manufacturing processes is a plus, but not mandatory.
  • You have a genuine interest in optical system design in general and integrated optics in particular.
  • You are autonomous, creative, solution-oriented, flexible and a team-player.

Main duties and responsibilities

  • Define research and experimental plans, sustained with theoretical considerations and knowledge of the relevant literature.
  • Conduct experiments and analyze data.
  • Report and disseminate the research outcome in journal publications and at international conferences.
  • Interact and collaborate with industrial partners.
  • Occasionally supervise undergraduate students (bachelor or master levels).
  • Contribute to the laboratory operations through shared duties.

We offer

  • The opportunity to contribute to cutting-edge research topics impacting emerging technologies.
  • Access to state-of-the-art facilities within an established, multidisciplinary laboratory in advanced manufacturing.
  • An opportunity to directly interact with the high-tech industry.
  • A vibrant, international environment at EPFL, a top-ranked global university with a broad range of scientific training and networking events in a thriving entrepreneurial community.

EPFL is an equal-opportunity employer. Selection is based solely on merit.
