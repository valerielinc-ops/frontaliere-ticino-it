Responsabilità principali

  • Main duties and responsibilities As a PhD Student, you will be expected to:
  • Have full responsibility for your own dissertation
  • Research in close collaboration with industry partners Experiment design and execution Analyze and interpret experimental results
  • Write scientific articles for publication in peer-reviewed journals Present at international conferences
  • Participate in education and supervise student projects
  • More specifically, within this project, you will c onduct research into how material properties, surface finish, and geometric tolerances affect the aerodynamic performance of small-scale turbomachinery, with a focus on understanding how degrees of design freedom related to manufacturing and material properties influence overall performance outcomes.
  • Building on these insights, identify ideal aeromechanical turbomachinery designs that exploit new materials and manufacturing processes, and validate these findings through experimental comparisons of conventional and ceramic turbocompressor components manufactured using the novel processes developed. Required competences
  • Strong background in fluid dynamics fundamentals, turbomachinery aerodynamics, aeromechanical design principles, experience with CFD & FEA.
  • Materials science, particularly properties of ceramics and conventional metals/alloys, manufacturing processes, surface engineering and its effect on flow behavior, geometric dimensioning and tolerancing.
  • Experience with turbomachinery testing procedures, instrumentation and sensor selection, data acquisition, uncertainty analysis and measurement validation. Special requirements

Processo di candidatura

  • You will be working on the TurboImpact project, funded by Horizon Europe – MSCA-DN, tackles challenges in designing, optimizing, selecting materials, and manufacturing small-scale turbomachinery for applications in heat pumps, fuel cells, organic Rankine cycles, or gas turbines.
  • This project develops robust system and component design methods, an integrated manufacturing platform, and multi-material joining techniques to efficiently produce small-scale turbomachinery.
  • The goal will be achieved by establishing a network of 14 Doctoral Candidates (DC) working in synergy on complementary topics and supported by highly qualified supervisors from European universities and research-intensive European companies Application procedure
  • Your complete online application must be submitted no later than 30 November 2026 (23:59h).
  • Applications must be submitted as one PDF file containing all materials to be given consideration.
  • To apply, please open the link "Apply now", fill out the online application form, and attach all your materials in English in one PDF file. The file must include: A motivation letter Curriculum vitae
  • The names of three referees who are willing to write a reference letter
  • You may apply prior to obtaining your master's degree but cannot begin before having received it.

Che stipendio offre EPFL per questa posizione?

È un ruolo a tempo pieno, e che tipo di contratto offre EPFL?

Regole sulla zona di frontiera e tempi di lavorazione variano per paese confinante e canton — verifica i dettagli con l'ufficio della migrazione del canton Neuchâtel o con HR durante il colloquio.
